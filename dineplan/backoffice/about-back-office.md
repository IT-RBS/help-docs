# About Back Office

### Overview

The **Back Office** section provides access to configuration and administrative functions used to manage and maintain DinePlan. It brings together features that help users configure system settings, manage printers and products, configure tickets, manage accounts and automation, maintain entities and users, and handle feedback.

The available options in the Back Office section are:

#### Accessing the Back Office

To access the **Back Office** section:

1. Log in to DinePlan.
2. From the **Dashboard**, select **“Back Office”**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (166).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. The Back Office screen opens with the available options.
4. Select the required option to access its respective functionality.

#### Back Office Functions

The Back Office features support different aspects of system configuration and administration. Each option provides tools for managing a specific area of DinePlan and helps maintain the required settings for restaurant operations.

* [**Settings**](settings/) – Manage general system and operational configurations. These settings help configure DinePlan according to the restaurant's operational requirements.
* [**Printer**](printer/) – Configure printer-related settings for POS operations. This includes managing the printer configurations required for printing bills, tickets, and other POS information.
* [**Products**](products/) – Manage and configure products used in the POS. Users can maintain product-related information and settings required for ordering and billing.
* [**Tickets**](tickets/) – Manage ticket-related configurations and settings. These options help control how tickets are configured and handled during restaurant operations.
* [**Accounts**](accounts/) – Manage account-related configurations. This section provides options for maintaining account information and related settings within DinePlan.
* [**Automation**](automation/) – Configure available automated processes and functions. These options help simplify recurring or system-driven activities.
* [**Entities**](enitites/) – Manage and configure entities used within the system. This helps maintain the different entities and their associated configurations.
* [**Users**](users/) – Manage users and their access to DinePlan. This allows authorized users to maintain user information and control access based on the required permissions.
* [**Feedback**](feedback/) – Access and manage feedback-related activities. This provides a dedicated area for handling feedback associated with the application or its operations.
