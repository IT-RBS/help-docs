# FAQ's

## 1. How do I open the Message screen in DinePlan from the main dashboard?

To access the internal communication panel from your main screen:

1. Log in to **DinePlan**.
2. From the **"Dashboard"**, select **"Front Desk"**.
3. Select **"Message"**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (419).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Result:** The **Message** screen opens to display your messages and conversation controls.

## 2. How do I view a list of all incoming messages and active conversations?

To check incoming system notifications or staff messages:

1. Open the **"Front Desk"** module and select **"Message"**.
2. Look at the **"Messages"** list (also known as the **Message List**) on the left panel.
3. Browse the list to see all available messages, notifications, and active conversations.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (422).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 3. How do I review the full message history for a specific conversation?

To read a complete conversation thread:

1. Navigate to **"Front Desk"** → **"Message"**.
2. From the **"Messages"** list, select the required conversation.
3. Review the complete message history displayed in the **Conversation Panel**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (419).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 4. How do I send a reply to an incoming message in DinePlan?

To respond to a message thread:

1. Open the **"Front Desk"** module and select **"Message"**.
2. From the **"Messages"** list, select the required conversation.
3. Review the existing message history in the **Conversation Panel**.
4. Click inside the **"Reply Here"** text box.
5. Type your response message.
6. Select **Send**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (413).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Result:** Your reply is sent to the selected conversation, and the thread updates automatically.

## 5. Where do I type my response when replying to a system or user notification?

To locate the text input area:

1. Select the target thread from the **"Messages"** list.
2. Locate the **"Reply Here"** text box located below the conversation view.
3. Type your reply directly into the **"Reply Here"** text field.
4. Select **Send** to transmit your message.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (415).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 6. What happens after I click Send on a message thread?

When you click **Send**:

1. The reply text typed in the **"Reply Here"** text box is sent to the selected conversation.
2. The system updates the conversation record in real time.
3. The **Conversation Panel** refreshes to display your newly sent reply in the message history.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (415).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 7. How do I check active operational details like business type, date, shift number, and user while on the Message screen?

To verify system status and login credentials while viewing messages:

1. Open the **Message** screen via **"Front Desk"** → **"Message"**.
2. Look at the **Status Bar** along the bottom of the screen.
3. Read the **Status Bar** to check:
   * **Active Business Type** (e.g., _Restaurant_, _Take Away_, or _Zomato_)
   * **Business Date**
   * **Shift Number**
   * **Logged-in User**
   * **Navigation Controls**

## 8. How do I exit the Message screen and return to the Front Desk module?

To exit the messaging interface:

1. Locate the **Close** button on the **Message** screen.
2. Select **Close**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (414).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Result:** The **Message** screen closes immediately, returning you to the **Front Desk** module.

## 9. What are the main screen elements available on the DinePlan Message interface?

The **Message** screen consists of five primary interface elements:

* **Messages (Message List):** Displays the list of available messages, notifications, or conversations.
* **Conversation Panel:** Displays the complete message history for the selected conversation.
* **Reply Here:** Text box where you enter your response to the selected message.
* **Send:** Button that sends the entered reply to the selected conversation.
* **Close:** Button that closes the Message screen and returns to the Front Desk module.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (413).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 10. Can I reply to internal messages without leaving the DinePlan application?

Yes.

The **Message** screen functions as a centralized communication panel directly inside DinePlan. It enables cashiers, store managers, restaurant owners, and administrators to monitor incoming notifications, review complete message histories, and issue direct replies without exiting or navigating away from the DinePlan software.
