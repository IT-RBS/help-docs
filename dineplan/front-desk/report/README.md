# Report

## Overview

The Reports section provides access to different types of reports available in DinePlan. It allows users to review and analyze information related to restaurant operations, sales, transactions, items, tickets, and other business activities.

Reports are organized into different categories, making it easier for users to locate the required report based on the type of information they want to review. Each category contains reports related to a specific area of restaurant operations.

The available report categories are:

* [**General**](general-reports.md) – Contains reports covering general restaurant and operational information.
* [**Day Shift**](day-shift-reports.md) – Provides reports related to day and shift-based activities.
* [**Item**](item-reports.md) – Contains reports related to items and their transactional information.
* [**Ticket**](ticket-reports.md) – Provides reports related to tickets and ticket transactions.
* [**Group**](group-reports.md) – Contains reports organized based on configured groups.
* [**Entity**](entity-reports.md) – Provides reports based on configured entities.
* [**Member**](member-reports.md) – Contains reports related to members and their activities.
* [**Summary**](summary-reports.md) – Provides summarized information for reviewing overall activities and transactions.
* [**Custom**](custom-reports.md) – Provides access to customized reports configured according to specific requirements.

The Reports section helps users review restaurant activities, monitor transactions, and access relevant business information from a centralized location.

## Accessing Reports

To access the Reports section:

**Dashboard → Front Desk → Report**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (165).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After selecting “Report”, the Report screen opens with the available report categories. Users can select the required category and then choose the appropriate report to view the relevant information.
