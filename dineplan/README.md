# About DinePlan

## Overview

DinePlan is a cloud-enabled Restaurant Point of Sale (POS) application designed to simplify and manage the day-to-day operations of restaurants. It provides a centralized platform for managing customer orders, billing, payments, cashier activities, tickets, operational settings, and daily restaurant activities.

The application provides a simple and user-friendly interface that enables restaurant staff to perform their tasks efficiently and manage transactions with greater accuracy. DinePlan also supports different operational requirements through features such as multiple payment options, offline billing, role-based access, and cloud synchronization.

DinePlan brings key restaurant POS activities together in one application, helping users manage their daily operations in an organized and efficient manner.

The application is organized into six main sections:

1. [**Front Desk**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/0EE0WqKESoD8hlEpFQQL/front-desk)
2. [**Work Day**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/0EE0WqKESoD8hlEpFQQL/workday)
3. [**Back Office**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/0EE0WqKESoD8hlEpFQQL/backoffice)
4. [**POS**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/0EE0WqKESoD8hlEpFQQL/pos)
5. [**Tickets**](tickets/)
6. [**Log Out**](logout.md)

## Main Features of DinePlan

### 1. Front Desk

The **Front Desk** section provides access to operational functions required for managing the restaurant's daily activities.

It allows authorized users to access information and perform tasks related to the restaurant's front-desk operations.

#### Key Functions

* **Reports** – Provides access to relevant operational and billing reports.
* **Till** – Helps users manage and review cashier and till-related activities.
* **Sold Out** – Allows users to manage products or items that are currently unavailable for ordering.

The Front Desk section is primarily useful for **cashiers, store managers, and other staff responsible for monitoring daily restaurant operations**.

### 2. Work Day

The **Work Day** section is used to manage the restaurant's business day and related operational activities.

It helps users maintain the correct working-day status and ensures that POS operations are performed within the appropriate business period.

#### Key Functions

* Manage the current **Work Day**.
* Start and manage daily operational activities.
* Maintain the business-day context for transactions and POS operations.
* Support daily cashier and restaurant workflows.

The Work Day functionality is important because restaurant transactions and operational activities are associated with a specific business day.

### 3. Back Office

The **Back Office** section is used to manage the configuration and administrative aspects of DinePlan.

While the POS is primarily used for daily restaurant transactions, the Back Office provides authorized users with tools to **configure and maintain the system**.

#### Key Functions

* Product and menu-related configuration.
* Restaurant and POS configuration.
* User and access-related configuration.
* System and operational settings.
* Configuration of features required for day-to-day POS operations.
* Management of supporting settings used by the POS.

The Back Office is mainly intended for **administrators, restaurant managers, and authorized users** responsible for maintaining the restaurant's configuration.

### 4. POS

The **POS** section is the primary area used for processing restaurant transactions.

It enables restaurant staff to create orders, manage items, generate bills, and process payments.

#### Key Functions

* Create and manage customer orders.
* Add products or menu items to an order.
* Manage order details.
* Generate customer bills.
* Process payments.
* Support different payment methods.
* Perform cashier-related activities.
* Handle restaurant and takeaway transactions.
* Support billing during offline operation when connectivity is unavailable.

The POS section is mainly used by **cashiers and POS operators** during regular restaurant operations.

#### Typical POS Workflow

**Select Products → Create Order → Review Order → Generate Bill → Select Payment Method → Process Payment → Complete Transaction**

### 5. Tickets

The **Tickets** section is used to manage and work with restaurant order tickets.

Tickets help staff track and manage orders as they move through the restaurant's operational workflow.

#### Key Functions

* View available tickets.
* Select required tickets.
* Manage ticket information.
* Handle ticket-related operations.
* Support ticket-based order workflows.
* Perform actions such as **merging tickets**, where applicable.

The Tickets section is particularly useful for restaurant staff who need to **track, review, combine, or manage customer orders during service**.

### 6. Log Out

The **Log Out** option allows the currently signed-in user to safely exit the DinePlan application.

Logging out is especially important when multiple employees use the same POS device or workstation.

It helps ensure that:

* The current user's session is closed.
* Another employee can sign in with their own credentials.
* User-specific access permissions are maintained.
* Unauthorized users cannot continue using the previous user's session.

Users should log out when they have completed their POS activities or when handing the workstation over to another employee.

## Supporting Features

#### Offline Billing

DinePlan supports offline billing, allowing users to continue essential billing activities even when the system temporarily loses internet connectivity. This helps reduce interruptions during restaurant operations.

#### Multiple Payment Options

DinePlan supports multiple payment options, allowing restaurant staff to select the appropriate payment method while processing customer bills.

#### Role-Based Access

DinePlan supports role-based access, allowing organizations to control which features and functions are available to different users based on their assigned roles and permissions.

#### Cloud Synchronization

As a cloud-enabled application, DinePlan supports cloud synchronization, helping keep relevant restaurant data synchronized with the cloud when connectivity is available.

#### Restaurant and Takeaway Operations

DinePlan supports different order types, including Restaurant and Take Away, allowing the POS workflow to accommodate different types of customer orders.

## Who Can Use DinePlan?

DinePlan is designed to support different users involved in restaurant operations:

* **Cashiers** – Process customer orders, bills, and payments.
* **POS Operators** – Manage orders and POS transactions.
* **Store Managers** – Monitor daily operations and manage operational activities.
* **Restaurant Owners** – Review restaurant operations and relevant information.
* **Administrators** – Manage system configuration, users, permissions, and other administrative setting
