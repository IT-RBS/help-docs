# Store Month Closure

**Store Month Closure** is the process of officially closing inventory transactions for a particular month after completing the physical inventory verification and stock reconciliation.

## Steps to Perform Store Month Closure

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Store Month Closure**.

* The Store Month Closure screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 16-54-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Store** field.

1. Select the store that needs to be closed for the month.

* The system will automatically load the most recent open month available for closure.

Step 3: Review the month displayed by the system.

1. Ensure that all transactions for the selected month have been completed and verified.
2. Confirm that physical inventory reconciliation has been performed successfully.

Step 4: Click the **Close Month** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 16-57-06.png>)

1. The system will process the month-end closure for the selected store.
