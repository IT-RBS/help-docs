# Material Request – Short Close

The **Material Request Short Close** option is used to close a Material Request that can no longer be fulfilled completely or is no longer required. Once a Material Request is short-closed, the remaining pending quantity is cancelled, and no further processing can be performed against that request.

## Steps to Perform a Material Request Short Close

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Request Short Close**.

* The Material Request Short Close screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-58-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-59-04.png>)

1. Select the appropriate **Issue Store** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-59-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click the **Search** icon.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 11-00-10.png>)

* The system will display the list of open Material Requests associated with the selected Issue Store.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 11-00-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the Material Request that needs to be short-closed.

1. Click the **Short Close** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 11-02-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* A confirmation window will appear requesting a reason for the short close.

Step 4: In the **Reason** field, enter the reason for closing the Material Request (Examples: Requirement cancelled, Item no longer needed, Stock unavailable).

1. After entering the reason, click **OK**.

* The selected Material Request will be marked for short closure.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 11-03-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-26-56.png>)

* The Material Request will be successfully short-closed and removed from the list of pending requests.
