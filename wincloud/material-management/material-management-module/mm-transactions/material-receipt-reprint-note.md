# Material Receipt Reprint Note

The **Material Receipt Reprint Note** option allows users to generate and print an additional copy of a previously created **Material Receipt Note (MRN)**.This feature is useful when the original printout is lost, damaged, or when additional copies are required for inventory records, supplier verification, auditing, or documentation purposes.

## Steps to Perform a Material Receipt Reprint

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Receipt Reprint**.

* The Material Receipt Reprint screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-08-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Specify the **From Date** and **To Date** to define the period for which Material Receipt records need to be displayed.

1. Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-11-13.png>)

* Use the available filters to narrow down the search results based on specific criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-09-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will retrieve and display all Material Receipt Notes created within the selected date range.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-12-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: From the displayed list, select the Material Receipt Note that needs to be reprinted.

* Verify that the correct receipt has been selected.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-13-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the required receipt, click the **Print** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-41-24 (2).png>)

* The system will generate a reprint of the selected Material Receipt Note.
