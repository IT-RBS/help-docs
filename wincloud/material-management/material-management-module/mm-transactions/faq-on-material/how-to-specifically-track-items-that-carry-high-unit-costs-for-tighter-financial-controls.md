# How to specifically track items that carry high unit costs for tighter financial controls?

To view the High Value Stock Items report for tracking items with high unit costs or significant total monetary value, follow these steps:

1. Go to Analysis > High Value Stock Items.
2. Use the Funnel Filter or Group By drop-down options to narrow down your search or filter by specific items and categories.

This report allows you to closely monitor high-cost and critical items to ensure tighter risk management, effective inventory control, and financial oversight.
