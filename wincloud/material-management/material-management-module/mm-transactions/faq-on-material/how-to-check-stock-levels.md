# How to check stock levels?

To check stock levels in the system, you can choose from a few different methods depending on whether you need a quick store check, a specific item lookup, or an overall stock summary report:1. Check Stock (Transaction)Use this option to quickly review and verify the current item quantities available in a specific store:

1. Go to Transaction > Check Stock.
2. Click on CC Store Name and select the desired store.
3. Select the Date range and click the Check button to view current stock levels.

2\. Item Stock Query (Queries)Use this option to search stock details for a specific product:

1. Go to Queries > Item Stock Query.
2. Click the Item Search icon and choose the item name.
3. Click the Display button to view the stock status.

3\. Stock Summary (Reports)Use this report to view a full overview of current stock quantities, rates, and total values:

1. Go to Reports > Stock Summary.
2. Use the Funnel Filter to narrow down results by product or product group if necessary.
3. Click the Display button to view the report. You can also print or export it to Excel
