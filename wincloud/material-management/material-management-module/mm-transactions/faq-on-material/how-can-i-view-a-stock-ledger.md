# How can I view a Stock Ledger?

You can view your stock ledger in two ways depending on whether you need a detailed item-by-item movement record or an overall period summary:1. Item Stock Ledger (Detailed View)This report tracks all stock transactions (receipts, issues, adjustments, and balances) for an item in chronological order.

* Steps:
  1. Go to Reports > Item Stock Ledger.
  2. Click on the Advance filter option.
  3. Click the Display button to load the report.
  4. _(Optional)_ Click the Print or Excel button to print or download the report.

***

2\. Stock Ledger Summary (Period Summary View)This report provides a summary showing the Opening Quantity, Received Quantity, and Issued Quantity for a selected period.

* Steps:
  1. Go to Reports > Stock Ledger Summary.
  2. Use the Funnel Filter, Store By, or Group By options to filter by specific products or stores.
  3. Select whether to display the report by Quantity, Value, or Both.
  4. Click the Display button
