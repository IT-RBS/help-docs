# How to analyze item usage and costs broken down by individual cost centers ?

To view the Cost Center Item Summary report, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Analysis > Cost Center Item Summary.
2. Use the Funnel filter or Group By drop-down options to refine your parameters or filter by specific items and cost centers.
3. Load the report on your screen to review item-wise quantities and related costs.

This report captures item utilization based on consumption, issue, or transaction records to help you monitor budgets and track costs across individual cost centers
