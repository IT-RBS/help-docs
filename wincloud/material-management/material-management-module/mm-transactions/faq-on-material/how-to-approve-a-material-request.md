# How to approve a material request?

To approve a Material Request (MR), follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Transaction > Material Request Approval.
2. Select the specific Material request you need to approve.
3. Click on the Approve button.

***

Configuring Approval Rules & Authorities: If you need to set up or modify who is authorized to approve material requests:

* Approval Settings: Navigate to Master > Approval Settings to select the transaction type, store type, and define the required approval levels and procedures.
* Approval Authority: Navigate to Master > Approval Authority to assign specific users or department heads as authorized approvers
