# How to view report for further supplier tracking and procurement analysis?

Here is how you can view both reports for supplier tracking and procurement analysis:

1\. Receipt Analysis

This report provides information on all receipts processed within the Inventory Module, including bill numbers, bill dates, and other supplier-related receipt details.

* Navigation: Go to Analysis > Receipt Analysis.
* Steps:
  1. Use the Funnel Filter or Group By drop-down options to filter or group specific information.
  2. Click the Option button to apply additional filter criteria if needed.
  3. Click the Display button to load the report.

***

2\. Supplier Wise Receipt:

This report details the receipt of goods, materials, or services categorized individually for each supplier. It tracks delivered quantities, delivery dates, purchase order references, and other key details to assist with vendor reconciliation, inventory management, and payment processing.

* Navigation: Go to Analysis > Supplier Wise Receipt.
* Steps:
  1. Go to Analysis > Supplier Wise Receipt.
  2. Use the Funnel Filter and Group By drop-down options to filter or search for specific suppliers and parameters.
