# How do I check for items that are about to expire?

To check for items that have expired or are approaching their expiry date, you can generate the Expiry Item List report.Here are the steps:

1. Go to Reports > Expiry Item List.
2. Click on the Advance Filter option to filter by product, product group, or other specific criteria.
3. Click the Display button to view the list of items along with their expiry dates.
4. _(Optional)_ Click the Print or Excel button to download or print the report
