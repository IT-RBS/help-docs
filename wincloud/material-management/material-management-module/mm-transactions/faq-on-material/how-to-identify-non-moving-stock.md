# How to identify non-moving stock?

To identify inventory items that have shown no consumption, issue, or movement over a defined period, you can use the Non Moving Stock analysis report. This report helps highlight excess or obsolete stock so you can make decisions regarding disposal, redistribution, or inventory optimization.Steps to View Non-Moving Stock:

1. Navigate to Analysis > Non Moving Stock.
2. Use the Funnel Filter or Group By drop-down options to filter for specific products, categories, or criteria.
3. Load the report to review the list of inactive items.
