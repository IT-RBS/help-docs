# How to view the MR Status Report to check whether your request has been approved, short closed ?

To view the Material Request Status Report and check whether your request has been approved, short-closed, pending, or completed, follow these steps:

1. Go to Reports > Material Request Status Report.
2. Use the Funnel Filter or additional filter options to filter by a specific status, date range, product, department, or cost center.
3. Click the Display button to load the report on your screen.

This report provides a complete list of all raised Material Requests (MR / Store Indents) for a specific store or across all stores along with their current operational status
