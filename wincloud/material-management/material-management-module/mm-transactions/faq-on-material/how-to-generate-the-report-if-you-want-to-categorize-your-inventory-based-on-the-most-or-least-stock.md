# How to generate the report if you want to categorize your inventory based on the most or least stock

Here is how you can generate both reports to categorize your inventory by stock value or consumption value:

1\. **XYZ Stock Analysis** (Categorization by Stock Value)This report categorizes inventory products based on the most or least stocked items/groups by total stock value.

* Navigation: Go to Analysis > XYZ Stock Analysis.
* How to Filter & Analyze:
  * Stock Filter: Filter by stock percentage (%) to view the most stocked or least stocked items relative to total stock value.
  * Classes: Items are categorized into X Item Class down to Y Item Class and Z Item Class.
  * Search: Use the Funnel Filter to search for a specific Product or Group.

***

2\. **ABC Consumption Analysis** (Categorization by Consumption Value)This report categorizes inventory products based on the most or least consumed items by total consumption value.

* Navigation: Go to Analysis > ABC Consumption Analysis.
* How to Filter & Analyze:
  * Consumption Filter: Filter by consumption percentage (%) to isolate the most or least consumed items.
  * Classes: Items are categorized into A Item Class (most consumed) down to B Item Class and C Item Class.
  * Grouping & Search: Use Group By to group results by Product Group, and use the Funnel Filter to search for specific items or criteria
