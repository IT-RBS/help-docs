# How to finalize your physical inventory reconciliation and open up the system for next month's ?

To perform a Store Month Closure after completing your Physical Inventory Reconciliation, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to Transaction > Store Month Closure.
2. Select the Store. _(The last month will load automatically.)_
3. Click the Close Month button.

Important Details:

* Purpose: This process confirms your final closing stock on the last day of the month and finalizes operations for that period.
* Unlocking Next Month: Completing the Store Month Close is necessary based on your system settings to start recording transactions for the upcoming month.
* Applicability: Based on system settings, this closure applies to all store types, including perishables, circulating, and stock-able items
