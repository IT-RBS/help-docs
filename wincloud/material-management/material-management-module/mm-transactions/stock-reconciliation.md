# Stock Reconciliation

**Stock Reconciliation** is the process of comparing the stock quantity recorded in the system with the actual physical stock available in the store or warehouse.

&#x20;If any differences are identified between the system stock and the physical stock, the discrepancies are reviewed and adjusted to ensure that the inventory records accurately reflect the actual stock on hand.

## Steps to Perform Stock Reconciliation

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Stock Reconciliation**.

* The Stock Reconciliation screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-35-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Store** field.

1. Select the store for which stock reconciliation needs to be performed.

* The selected store's inventory records will be available for reconciliation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-35-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Search** icon ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-37-21.png>)next to the **Product Name** field.

1. Select the product that needs to be reconciled.

* The system will display the current stock quantity recorded for the selected product.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-38-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter the actual stock quantity in the **Actual Stock** field.

* After entering the quantity, the system will automatically calculate and display the **Stock Variance**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-40-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-40-19 (1).png>)

* The stock reconciliation transaction will be recorded, and the inventory records will be updated accordingly.
