# Material Request Approval

The **Material Request Approval** process allows authorised personnel, such as Department Heads, to review and approve Material Requests submitted by users. Approval is required before the requested materials can be processed and issued from the store. This process helps ensure that all material requests are authorised and comply with departmental requirements.

## Steps to Perform Material Request Approval

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Request Approval**.

* The Material Request Approval screen will be displayed.

Step 2: From the list of pending requests, select the Material Request that requires approval.

1. Ensure that the correct request has been selected before proceeding.

Step 3: Click the **Approve** button.

* Once approved, the Material Request will be available for further processing and material issuance.
