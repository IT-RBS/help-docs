# Receipt Return

**Receipt Return** is the process of returning received materials or products back to the supplier. This transaction is used when materials are damaged, defective, incorrectly supplied, excess in quantity, or no longer required.

## Steps to Perform a Receipt Return

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Receipt Return**.

* The Receipt Return screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-54-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Basis** field.

1. Select the appropriate basis for the receipt return from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-55-17.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Vendor Name** field.

1. Select the vendor to whom the materials are being returned.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/b1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Confirm that the correct vendor and materials have been selected for return.
* Ensure that the returned quantities do not exceed the quantities originally received.

Step 4: After verifying all details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-26-56.png>)

* The Receipt Return document will be created successfully, and the inventory records will be updated accordingly.
