# Store Day Closure

**Store Day Closure** is the process of closing and finalising all inventory transactions for a specific day. This procedure ensures that all stock movements, receipts, issues, transfers, and adjustments for the day have been recorded and verified before the next business day begins.

## Steps to Perform Store Day Closure

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Store Day Closure**.

* The Store Day Closure screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Select the date for which the day closure needs to be performed.

1. Verify that the selected date is correct and that all transactions for that day have been completed.

Step 3: Click the **Save** button.

* The system will process the day-end closure and finalise all inventory transactions for the selected date.
