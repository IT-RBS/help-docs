# Material Issue

**Material Issue** is the process of issuing materials from the store to a department, production unit, or authorised user based on their requirements.Materials are maintained in the store inventory and are issued whenever they are required for operational, production, maintenance, or departmental activities.&#x20;

## Steps to Perform a Material Issue

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Issue**.

* The Material Issue screen will be displayed.

Step 2: In the **Type** field, select the appropriate issue type.

1. In the **Store Name** field, select the store from which the materials will be issued.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-31-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* After selecting the store, the inventory details for the selected store will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-35-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Search** icon.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-35-57.png>)

1. Select the required **Product Name** from the list.

* The selected product will be added to the Material Issue transaction.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-36-37.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Locate the **Issue Quantity** field.

1. Enter the quantity of the product to be issued.

* Ensure that the entered quantity does not exceed the available stock quantity.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-38-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Verify the details and click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-40-19.png>)

* The Material Issue transaction will be successfully recorded, and the inventory stock will be updated accordingly.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-38-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
