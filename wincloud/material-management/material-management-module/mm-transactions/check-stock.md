# Check Stock

**Check Stock** is the process of reviewing and verifying the current quantity of items available in a store or warehouse. This feature allows users to view stock balances for a specific store and date range, helping ensure accurate inventory records and stock availability.

## Steps to View Check Stock

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Check Stock**.

* The Check Stock screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/b3 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **CC Store Name** field.

1. Select the required store from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-58-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Enter the **From Date** and **To Date** to specify the period for which stock information is required.

Step 4: Click the **Check** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 16-40-45.png>)

1. The system will display the stock details for the selected store and date range.
