# Material Issue Reprint

The **Material Issue Reprint** option is used to generate and print an additional copy of a previously created Material Issue document.This feature is useful when the original printout is lost, damaged, or when extra copies are required for records, auditing, or departmental reference. It does not create a new Material Issue transaction; it only reprints an existing record.

## Steps to Perform Material Issue Reprint

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Issue Reprint**.

* The Material Issue Reprint screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-56-41.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the **Issue CC Store** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-57-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Select the **From Date** and **To Date** to define the required period.
2. Click the **Display** button.

* The system will display all Material Issue records based on the selected store and date range.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-59-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: From the displayed list, select the required **Material Issue** document.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 14-59-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required Material Issue record, click the **Print** ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-00-03.png>)button.

1. The system will generate a reprint of the selected Material Issue document.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 15-00-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
