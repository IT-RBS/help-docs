# Material Receipt

A **Material Receipt Note (MRN)** is a document used to record the receipt of materials from a supplier into the inventory system. It serves as proof that the ordered materials have been received and accepted at the designated inventory location.

## Steps to Create a Material Receipt

Step 1: Navigate to **Transactions** in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Receipt**.

* The Material Receipt screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-33-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-34-00.png>)

* Fill the following details:

1. In the **Basis** field, select the appropriate receipt basis.
2. In the **PO Type** field, select the required Purchase Order type.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-34-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. In the **PO Number** field, choose the Purchase Order against which the materials are being received.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-35-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* These details will help the system identify the corresponding Purchase Order and the materials to be received.

Step 3: Once the Purchase Order is selected, the available PO items will be displayed.

1. Select the required Purchase Order item(s) to be received.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-35-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 10-26-56 (3).png>)

* The Material Receipt Note (MRN) will be created and saved successfully in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-01 12-42-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
