# POS Post Settings

**POS Post Settings** define the rules and configurations that control how transactions from a Point of Sale (POS) system are posted into the accounting, inventory, or ERP system.

* These settings ensure that all sales, payments, and stock movements are accurately recorded and reflected in the system for reporting and analysis.

## Steps to Configure POS Post Settings

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **POS Post Settings**.

* The POS Post Settings screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-30-42 (2).png>)

1. From the available list, select the required **Outlet Name**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/d1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3:Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-53-04 (2).png>)

1. Select the required **Product Group**.
2. Select the corresponding **CC Store**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/d2 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After completing the required configurations, review all selected details.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-32-52 (3).png>)

* The POS Post Settings will be successfully created and applied for the selected outlet.
