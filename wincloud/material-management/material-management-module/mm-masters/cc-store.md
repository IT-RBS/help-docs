# CC Store

The **CC Store Master** is the master record within the inventory management system that defines and maintains all information related to a Centralised/Corporate (CC) Store.

## Steps to Create and View a CC Store

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **CC Store**.

* The CC Store screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-19-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button on the CC Store screen.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-19-33.png>)

Step 3: Fill in the required store information.

1. Verify that all required information has been entered correctly.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-20-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all necessary information, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-25 (1).png>)

* The new CC Store will be created and saved successfully in the system.
