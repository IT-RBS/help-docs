# Parent Cost Center

The **Parent Cost Center Master** is a master record within the financial or ERP system that defines a top-level cost center. It serves as the primary reference for organising and managing related sub-cost centers within the organisational hierarchy.

## Steps to Create Parent Cost Center

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Parent Cost Center**.

* The Parent Cost Center screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-32-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-32-33.png>)

Step 3: Enter the required information.

1. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-33-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-25.png>)

* The Parent Cost Center will be created successfully and stored in the system.
