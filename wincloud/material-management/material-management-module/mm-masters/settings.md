# Settings

**Settings** in the context of master data refer to configurable parameters, system rules, and preferences that control how business processes, operations, and transactions are managed within the application.

## Steps to View and Modify Settings

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Settings**.

* The Settings screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-55-57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-56-32.png>)

Step 3: Configure Required Settings

1. Review the available settings options.
2. Modify the required settings based on your requirements.

* **User Restrictions** – Configure user permissions and access controls.
* **Print Templates** – Select or customise document and report print formats.

3. Verify all modifications before saving.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-57-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After completing the required changes, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-25.png>)

* The updated settings will be saved and applied within the system.
