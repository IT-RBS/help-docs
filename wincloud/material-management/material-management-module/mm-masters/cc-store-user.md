# CC Store User

A **CC Store User** is an individual who is authorised to access and operate within a Centralised/Corporate (CC) Store in the system. CC Store Users are responsible for managing store-related activities and ensuring that inventory transactions are performed accurately and according to established procedures.

## Steps to Create and View a CC Store User

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the Inventory module.

1. Click on **CC Store User**.

* The CC Store User screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 16-31-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-56-32 (1).png>)

Step 3: In the **CC Store User** screen, select the **Basis, User, Department** and **CC Store.**

1. Choose the appropriate CC Store based on the user's operational requirements.
2. Verify that the selected user, department, and store details are correct.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 16-33-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-25 (4).png>)

* The user will be successfully assigned to the selected CC Store.
