# CC Store Group

A **CC Store Group (Cost Center Store Group)** is a classification used to group multiple stores or inventory locations under a specific cost center or a group of cost centers.

## Steps to Create CC Store Group

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **CC Store Group**.

* The CC Store Group screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-48-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-30-42 (1).png>)

Step 3: From the available list, select the required **Group Name**.

1. The system will display the details associated with the selected group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-48-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-53-04.png>)

1. Enter the **New CC Store Name** that needs to be included in the selected group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-49-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After adding the required CC Store details, review the information carefully.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-49-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-32-52 (1).png>)

* The CC Store Group will be created or updated successfully in the system.
