# Reorder Level

The **Reorder Level** is the predefined minimum stock quantity of a specific inventory item that triggers the need for replenishment. When the available stock reaches or falls below this level, it indicates that a new purchase order or production request should be initiated to maintain adequate inventory levels.

## Steps to View Reorder Level

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Reorder Level**.

* The Reorder Level screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-25-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-30-42.png>)

Step 3: Select the appropriate **Store** from the available list.

1. Select the required **Product Group**.
2. Select the corresponding **Product Subgroup**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-31-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* After selecting the required criteria, the system will display the associated inventory items along with their reorder level information.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-34-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Verify the displayed reorder level information.

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-32-52.png>)
