# Approval Authority

**Approval Authority** is used to define the users who are authorised to approve specific transactions within the system. It determines the approval hierarchy and identifies the individuals responsible for reviewing and approving transactions before they are processed.

## Steps to Create Approval Authority

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Approval Authority**.

* The Approval Authority screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-10-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the Approval Authority screen, select the required **Transaction**.

Step 3: Select the appropriate **Store Type** from the available options.

Step 4: Select the **User** who should be assigned as an approver for the selected transaction.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-11-14 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click the **Add** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 17-11-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: If multiple approvers are required, select additional users from the user list.

Step 6: Ensure that all required approval authorities have been assigned correctly.

1. Click the **Save** button.

* The Approval Authority configuration will be saved successfully.
