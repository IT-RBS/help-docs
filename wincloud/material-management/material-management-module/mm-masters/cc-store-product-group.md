# CC Store Product Group

A **CC Store Product Group** is a classification of products within a Centralised/Corporate (CC) Store based on common characteristics such as product type, usage, category, department, or function.

## Steps to Create and View a CC Store Product Group

Step 1: Navigate to **Master** menu in the **Inventory** modul&#x65;**.**

1. Select **CC Store Product Group**.

* The CC Store Product Group master screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-01-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-02-32.png>)

1. Select the **Basics** tab.
2. Select the **CC Store** option.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-03-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The CC Store Product Group creation screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-03-49.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Create the store **Product Group**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-06-03.png>)

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required information, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 15-04-25.png>)

* The CC Store Product Group will be created successfully and stored in the system.
