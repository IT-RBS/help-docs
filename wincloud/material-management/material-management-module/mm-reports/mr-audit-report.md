# MR Audit Report

The **MR Audit Report** provides a detailed record of all **Material Requests (MR)** or **Store Indents** along with their operational status and transaction history.This report is primarily used for monitoring, tracking, and audit purposes, helping users review the complete life-cycle of material requests across a specific store or all stores within the organisation.

## Steps to View the MR Audit Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **MR Audit Report**.

* The MR Audit Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-03-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11 (6).png>)

* This filter help users retrieve specific audit records and generate more accurate reports.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-03-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filter criteria, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the MR Audit Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-07-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the MR Audit Report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-05-11 (6).png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-05-32 (6).png>)
