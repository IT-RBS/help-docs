# Receipt Note- Summary

The **Receipt Note – Summary Report** provides comprehensive information on all receipt transactions recorded within a selected period and for selected store(s).This report displays important **Goods Receipt Note (GRN)** details, including Transaction Number, Bill Number, Receipt Date, Supplier Name, Tax Details and Other related transaction information.

## Steps to View the Receipt Note – Summary Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Receipt Note – Summary**.

* The Receipt Note – Summary Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-33-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Funnel Filter** icon.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11 (7).png>)

1. Select the required filter criteria to refine the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-34-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Option** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-35-40 (1).png>)

1. Select the required options according to your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-36-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **% (Percentage)** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-36-17.png>)

1. Select the required charge and tax fields to be included in the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-38-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the required filters and report options, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Receipt Note – Summary Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-40-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
