# Reorder Item List

**Reorder Item List**

This report gives an insight and understanding about the items, time and quantity of re ordering to maintain the stock as per stock maintenance.

**Funnel Filter** is for additional filters.

You can “**Group By**” Product (Default), Product Group and Product Sub Group.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (245).png>)

It shows you the list of items with the current stock, their defined minimum and maximum stock.
