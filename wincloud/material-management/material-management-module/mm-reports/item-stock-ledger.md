# Item Stock Ledger

The **Item Stock Ledger** is a detailed inventory report that tracks all stock transactions for a specific item. It records every inventory movement, including Receipts, Issues, Stock Adjustments,Transfers, Opening and Closing Balances. The report provides a chronological view of stock movements, helping users monitor inventory activity and track the current stock status of an item.

## Steps to View the Item Stock Ledger Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Item Stock Ledger**.

* The Item Stock Ledger Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-03-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11 (4).png>)

1. Select the required criteria according to your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-04-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filters, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will generate and display the Item Stock Ledger Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-05-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the Item Stock Ledger Report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1376).png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1377).png>)
