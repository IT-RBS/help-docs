# Department wise Product Group Summary

**Department wise Product Group Summary**

This report gives a summarised view of all the Items issued to cost centers of any department for a selected period.

A **Funnel Filter** is provided to search for a Specific Product, Department or a cost center etc.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (271).png>)

A **Group By** drop down filter is provided for multiple report formats.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (44).png>)

Report is generated in a format based on the Group By.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (14).png>)
