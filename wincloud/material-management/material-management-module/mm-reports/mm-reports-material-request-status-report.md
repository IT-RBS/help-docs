# Material Request Status Report

The **Material Request Status Report** provides a complete list of all **Material Requests (MR)** or **Store Indents** along with their current status across one or more stores.This report helps users monitor the progress of material requests and track whether they are approved, pending, short closed, or completed.

## Steps to View the Material Request Status Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Material Request Status Report**.

* The report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-35-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Funnel Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11 (5).png>)

1. Use the available filter options to refine the report.

* You can filter the report based on Material Request Number (MR No.)
* These filters help you locate specific Material Requests quickly and accurately.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-35-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the required filter **Options**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-33 (2).png>)based on your requirement.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-36-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After applying the required filters, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will generate and display the Material Request Status Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 14-36-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1376).png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1377).png>)
