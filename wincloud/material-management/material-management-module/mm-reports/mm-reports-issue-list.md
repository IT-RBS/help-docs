# Issue List

The **Issue List Report** provides a detailed record of all items issued from a store, including **Perishable Stores**, within a selected period.

This report allows users to track issued items and apply various filters such as **Product**, **Product Group**, **Supplier**, **Department**, and other available criteria. The report can also be printed or exported to Excel for further analysis and record-keeping.

## Steps to View the Issue List Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Click on **Issue List**.

* The Issue List Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-03-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Advanced Filter** option![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11.png>) available on the screen.

* Select the required filter criteria according to your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-04-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step &#x33;**:** Select the required **filter options.**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-33.png>)

1. You can filter the report by **Product**, **Product Group**, and other available criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-04-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filter criteria, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will generate and display the Issue List Report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-05-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Once the Issue List Report is displayed, you can:

* Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1376).png>)
* Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1377).png>)
