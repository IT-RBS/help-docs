# Stock Summary

The **Stock Summary Report** provides a consolidated view of the current inventory available in stock. The report displays item quantities, item rates, average rates, and the total value of stock on hand.

## Steps to View the Stock Summary Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Stock Summary**.

* The **Stock Summary Report** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-07-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Funnel Filter**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11.png>)icon to access the available filtering options.

* You can filter the report for a specific Product, Product Group and other available criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-08-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the Required Filter **Options.**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-33.png>)

1. Choose the required filter criteria based on your reporting needs.

* You can filter the report by Product, Product Group and other available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-09-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filter criteria, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will generate and display the **Stock Summary Report**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-11-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the Stock Summary Report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1376).png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1377).png>)
