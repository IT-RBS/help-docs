# Stock Ledger Summary

The **Stock Ledger Summary Report** provides a summarised view of stock transactions for a selected period. Similar to the Stock Ledger Report, this report displays detailed inventory movement information, including Opening Quantity, Received Quantity and Issued Quantity.

## Steps to View the Stock Ledger Summary Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Stock Ledger Summary**.

* The Stock Ledger Summary Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-46-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Funnel Filter**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11 (3).png>) icon to access the available filtering options.

1. Select the required criteria to refine the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-46-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Use the **Store By** drop-down to organise the report based on store information.

1. Use the **Group By** drop-down to group the report according to your preferred criteria.

Step 4: After selecting the required filters, grouping options, and display mode, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Stock Ledger Summary Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 12-48-05.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1376).png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1377).png>)
