# Stock Reconciliation

**Stock Reconciliation** is the process of comparing the stock quantity recorded in the system with the actual physical stock available in the store or warehouse.The **Stock Reconciliation List Report** provides detailed information about stock reconciliation transactions, helping users verify inventory accuracy and monitor stock adjustments made during reconciliation activities.

## Steps to View the Stock Reconciliation List Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Stock Reconciliation List**.

* The **Stock Reconciliation List** will be displaye&#x64;**.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 16-02-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 10-43-11.png>)

1. Select the required filter criteria to refine the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 16-03-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Options** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 15-35-40.png>)

1. Select the required options according to your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 16-03-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filters and report options, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will generate and display the Stock Reconciliation List Report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 16-04-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Print or Export the Report

1. Once the Stock Reconciliation List Report is displayed, you can:
   * Click the **Print** button to print the report.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-05-11.png>)
   * Click the **Excel** button to export and download the report in Microsoft Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 11-05-32.png>)
