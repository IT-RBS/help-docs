# GRN- GST Details

**GRN-GST Details**

This report is for the information of Tax w.r.t to receipt you made from the supplier for GST. This report gives you all the details of a GRN and details of the Supplier GSTIN No. and Tax Amount separately for a better understanding.

Multiple Filters and sorting options are provided to prepare your own report.

**Group By:** Groups the content of the report based on the below selection. It can be GST % wise, Date wise etc.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (89).png>)

Shows the total amount of the Group

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (62).png>)

**Funnel Filter:** This helps you to search for a specific supplier and his related transactions, supply type wise etc.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (19).png>)

**Columns:** This filter, as it says adds columns of additional information in the report.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/4 (74).png>)

This report will be an all-in-one report for the transactions of GRN or for anything related to GSTIN and Taxes of the suppliers in this module.
