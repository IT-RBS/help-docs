# High Value Stock Items

The **High Value Stock Items** report provides a detailed view of inventory items that carry a high monetary value. These items are identified based on factors such as unit cost, total stock value, or their critical importance to business operations.

## Steps to View the High Value Stock Items Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Analysis** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **High Value Stock Items**.

* The **High Value Stock Items** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 12-47-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the report page, apply the appropriate filters to generate the required information.

* The report supports multiple filters, allowing users to customise the data based on their analysis needs.

1. Use the Funnel Filter.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 09-52-22.png>)

* The **Funnel Filter** allows users to refine the report by applying specific search criteria.

2. Use the Group By Option

* The **Group By** drop-down enables users to organise report data into meaningful categories for better analysis.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 12-47-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filters and grouping options, click **Display** button.

* The system will display the High Value Stock Items report based on the selected criteria.
