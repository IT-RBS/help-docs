# Month wise Item Consumption Summary

The **Month Wise Item Consumption Summary** report provides a summarised view of item consumption on a monthly basis for a specified period. It displays the total quantity of items consumed during each month and, where applicable, the corresponding consumption value.

## Steps to View the Month Wise Item Consumption Summary

Step 1: Navigate to the **Analysis** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Month Wise Item Consumption Summary**.

* The **Month Wise Item Consumption Summary** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 10-58-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the report page, choose the appropriate filters to generate the required data.

* The report supports multiple filters, allowing users to customise the results based on their specific analysis requirements.

Step 3: Use the Funnel Filter![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 09-52-22 (3).png>)

* The **Funnel Filter** enables users to further refine the report by applying specific search criteria.

1. Use the Group By Option

* The **Group By** drop-down allows users to organise report data in different formats for better analysis and reporting.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 10-58-42.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filters and grouping options, click **Display** button.

1. The system will display the Month Wise Item Consumption Summary report based on the selected criteria.
