# Non Moving Stock

The **Non Moving Stock** report provides information about inventory items that have not recorded any consumption, issue, transfer, sale, or stock movement during a specified period. These items remain inactive in inventory and may indicate excess stock, slow-moving stock, or obsolete inventory.

## Steps to View the Non Moving Stock Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Analysis** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Non Moving Stock**.

* The **Non Moving Stock** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 13-57-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the report page, apply the necessary filters to generate the report.

* The report supports multiple filters, allowing users to customise the output according to their analysis requirements.

1. Use the Funnel Filter![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 09-52-22 (4).png>)

* The **Funnel Filter** allows users to narrow down the report results by applying specific search criteria.

2. Use the Group By Option

* The **Group By** drop-down enables users to organise report data into meaningful categories for easier analysis.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 13-58-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filters and grouping options, click **Display** button.

* The system will display the Non Moving Stock report based on the selected criteria.
