# Cost Center Item Summary

The **Cost Center Item Summary** report provides a summarised view of items associated with each cost center during a specified period. It captures item-wise quantities and the corresponding costs incurred by individual cost center based on inventory consumption, issue transactions, or other recorded item movements.

## Steps to View the Cost Center Item Summary

Step 1: Navigate to the **Analysis** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Cost Center Item Summary**.

* The **Cost Center Item Summary** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 10-18-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the report page, apply the necessary filters to retrieve the desired information.

* The report supports multiple filters, allowing users to customise the report according to their analysis requirements.

Step 3: Use the Funnel Filter.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 09-52-22 (1).png>)

* The **Funnel Filter** allows users to refine the report further by applying specific search criteria.

1. Use the Group By Option

The **Group By** drop-down allows users to organise and summarise report data in different ways.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 10-18-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filters and grouping options, click **Display** button.

1. The system will display the **Cost Center Item Summary** report based on the selected criteria.
