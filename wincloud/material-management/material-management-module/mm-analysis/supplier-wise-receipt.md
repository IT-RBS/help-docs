# Supplier Wise Receipt

The **Supplier Wise Receipt** report provides a detailed record of goods, materials, or services received from suppliers, organised supplier-wise. It captures information such as received quantities, delivery dates, purchase order references, receipt numbers, and other relevant transaction details.

## Steps to View the Supplier Wise Receipt Report

Step 1: Navigate to the Analysis menu in the Inventory module.

1. Select **Supplier Wise Receipt**.

* The **Supplier Wise Receipt** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 14-16-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: After opening the report page, apply the necessary filters to generate the desired report.

* The report supports multiple filters, allowing users to customise the information according to their business requirements.

1. Use the Funnel Filter![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 09-52-22.png>)

* The **Funnel Filter** allows users to further refine the report by applying specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-03 14-17-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filters and grouping options, click **Display** button.

* The system will display the Supplier Wise Receipt report based on the selected criteria.
