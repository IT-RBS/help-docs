# Cost Center wise Product Group Summary

**Cost Center wise Product Group Summary**

This report gives a summarised view of total consumption in the cost centers for a date range.

Filter to select **Group By**

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (289).png>)

And a Funnel Filter is provided to filter a specific department and cost center, product or it's sub group.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (247).png>)

And the report will display based on Filter of **Group By**, This is grouped according  to Cost Center.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (102).png>)
