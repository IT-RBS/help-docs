# Item Stock Query

The **Item Stock Query** feature allows users to view the current stock details of a specific item available in the system. Follow the steps below to search and display item stock information.

Step 1: Navigate to the **Queries** menu in the **Inventory** module.

1. Select **Item Stock Query**.

* The **Item Stock Query** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 09-38-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Item Search** icon available on the Item Stock Query screen.

1. Select the desired **Item Name** from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-02 09-38-47.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the item, click the **Display** button.

* The system will retrieve and display the stock information for the selected item.
