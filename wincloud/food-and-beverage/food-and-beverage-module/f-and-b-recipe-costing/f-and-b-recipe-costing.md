# Recipe Costing

**Recipe Costing**

This report gives you the list of all or selected recipes for seeing the items or ingredients in it. You can filter it Outlet wise and Product group wise (from Menu) for ease.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (22).png>)

It shows the items used and their quantity. It reveals the cost of making the Menu item / portion and also recommends you at what rate it should be.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (272).png>)
