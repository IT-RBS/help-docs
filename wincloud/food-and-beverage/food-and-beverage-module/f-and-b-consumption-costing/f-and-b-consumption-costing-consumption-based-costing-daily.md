# Consumption Based Costing - Daily

**Consumption Based Costing - Daily**

This report gives you data based on the Sales and Material Posting in the Module to see and analyze the **Sales** against **Material Issue** and/ or consumed for the selected date range.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (220).png>)

It displays the sales of all the outlets, shows the cost center linked and consumption amount outlet wise and also shows you the NC (Non Chargeable) Sales and its cost.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (23).png>)
