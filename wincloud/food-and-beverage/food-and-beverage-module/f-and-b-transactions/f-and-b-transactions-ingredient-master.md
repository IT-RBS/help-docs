# Ingredient Master

**Ingredient Master**

The Ingredient Master is used to create the name of the Ingredient. For Example, if Ginger Garlic Paste is an ingredient and not a final item, but it is used to create the final item. Creating this Ingredient also needs a mix of multiple items which we will create in Ingredient Creation where we will show what items are used to create the Ingredient itself.

To create an Ingredient Master:

Click “**New**” button after opening the Ingredient Master, or “**Open**” to modify. And Fill in the **Ingredient id** (use initials) and enter the **Ingredient name**.

Use the lowest Unit of Measurement (**UOM**).

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (124).png>)

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (116).png>)

After that “**Save**” it.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (172).png>)

Now Ingredient Name is Saved. You can proceed with creating Ingredient Recipe (refer **Ingredient Creation- Help**).
