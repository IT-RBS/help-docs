# Ingredient Creation

**Ingredient Creation**

Ingredient Creation is used to create or insert the recipe of the “Ingredient” used in a Final Item or Sale Item or Menu Item. Ingredients such as Chutney, Mayonnaise or Pastes etc. which are or can be made in house to add to the final item.

To create an Ingredient Recipe:

1. Go to the Ingredient Creation and Click “**New**” for new or “**Open**” to Modify and to just see click “**View**”.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (73).png>)

1. Select the “**Ingredient**” which you created in **Ingredient Master**.
2. Select the “**Portion**” and “**Preparation time**”. Portion is based on UOM.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (287).png>)

You have two options where you can create the steps of making the Item in **Preparation** and enter the list of Items to be used in **Recipe**.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (128).png>)

1. Select the **Ingredient/Item**. You can select other ingredients, a purchased item (raw material) and even a finished item (another menu item) to make the recipe.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (190).png>)

1. Select the Items and enter the quantity used to create a “portion” and percentage of yield.
2. On the bottom it will show you the total **Wastage** and Recommended price of **Cost** and **Menu** based on the Item value of the Stock.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/4 (126).png>)
