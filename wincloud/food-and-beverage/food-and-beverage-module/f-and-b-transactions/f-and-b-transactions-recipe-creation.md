# Recipe Creation

**Recipe Creation**

Recipe Creation is the option to enlist items used in the Menu for determining the actual cost of making a menu item based on the stock price of the items which can be frequently updated based on the Posting.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (139).png>)

To create a Recipe for an Outlet or a common Recipe, the steps would be as follows.

1. Click “**New**” to create New Recipe or Open to modify or View to just see it.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (184).png>)

1. Select “**Outlet**” Name for a specific Recipe or Keep it “**None**” for a common recipe.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (104).png>)

1. Select the “**Final Item**” (This is the Menu Item or Sale Item in Restaurants) for which you want to insert the recipe.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (51).png>)

You have two options where you can create the steps of making the Item in **Preparation** and then enter the list of Items to be used in the **Recipe**.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/4 (23).png>)

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/5 (12).png>)

1. Select the **Ingredient/Item**. You can select other ingredients, a purchased item (raw material) and even a finished item (another menu item) to make the recipe.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/6 (24).png>)

1. Select the Items and enter the quantity used to create “portion” and percentage of yield.
2. It also shows you the cost %  w.r.t to the Menu rate and suggests to you a possible Menu price based on the Cost . It also displays Wastage based on yield.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/7 (65).png>)
