# Standard Cost Updation for Recipe & Ingredient

**Standard Cost Updation for Recipe and Ingredient**

This option helps you update the price of Purchased items linked in the Ingredients and proceeds to update the rates of Recipe with it to give you the latest Recipe Rate.

Open the option and click “**Update**”.
