# PR Short Close

The **Purchase Order Short Close** option is used to close Purchase Orders that are no longer required, partially used, or pending for a long period of time. This process helps maintain accurate purchase records and prevents further transactions against unused or incomplete Purchase Orders.

## Steps for Purchase Order Short Close

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Order Short Close**.

* After selecting the option, the Purchase Order Short Close screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-05-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-07-28.png>)

Step 3: Choose the required **Request Number** or Purchase Order that needs to be short closed.

* Verify the Purchase Order details carefully before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-10-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-13-52.png>)

* Once saved, the selected Purchase Order will be marked as **Short Closed** in the system.
