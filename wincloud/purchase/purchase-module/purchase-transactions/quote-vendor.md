# Quote Vendor

A **Quote Vendor** refers to the vendor or supplier who submits a quotation in response to a Quote Request or Request for Quotation (RFQ). This process helps organizations record and manage vendor quotations for comparison and procurement purposes.

## Steps to Create a Quote Vendor Entry

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Quote Vendor**.

* The Quote Vendor screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-31-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-47-53.png>)

Step 3: Choose the required **RFQ Selection** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1367).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The selected RFQ details will appear on the screen.
* Ensure the vendor details and quotation information are correct before saving.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-36-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-49-36.png>)

* The vendor quotation details will be saved successfully in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-37-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
