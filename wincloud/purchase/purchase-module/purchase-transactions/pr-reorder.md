# PR Reorder

The **PR Reorder** option is used to create a new Purchase Requisition (PR) for items that need to be reordered. Reordering is generally done when stock levels are low or when previously purchased items are required again.

* This feature helps organisations maintain stock availability and streamline the purchasing process.

## Steps for PR Reorder

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **PR Reorder**.

* After selecting the option, the PR Reorder screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-52-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-47-53.png>)

* A new entry screen for PR Reorder will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-55-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Search** icon![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-57-05.png>) next to the **Product Name** field.

1. Select the required item or product from the displayed list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-57-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the product details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-13-52.png>)

* The system will create a new Purchase Requisition for the selected item.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-00-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
