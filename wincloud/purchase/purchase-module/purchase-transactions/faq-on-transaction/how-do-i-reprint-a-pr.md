# How do I reprint a PR?

To reprint a Purchase Requisition (PR), follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Click on **Transaction > Purchase Requisition Reprint**.
3. Select the date, choose the status, and click the Display button.
4. Select the specific bill (requisition) from the list and click the Print button at the top right corner of the screen.

_(Note: If you ever need to reprint a finalized Purchase Order instead of a requisition, you can do so by navigating to_ Transaction > Purchase Order Reprin&#x74;_.)_
