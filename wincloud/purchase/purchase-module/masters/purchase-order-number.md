# Purchase Order Number

The **Purchase Order Number** screen is used to view and maintain purchase order numbering details within the Purchase module.

## Steps to View Purchase Order Number

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Purchase Order Number** from the sub-menu.

* The Purchase Order Number screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-20-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-20-57.png>)

* This screen displays the existing Purchase Order Number configuration.
* Review the displayed Purchase Order Number details.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-21-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-22-46.png>)

* The Purchase Order Number information will be saved successfully.
