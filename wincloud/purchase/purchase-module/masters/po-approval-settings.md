# PO Approval Settings

The **PO Approval Settings** feature is used to define and manage the approval workflow for Purchase Orders based on TM Type and PO Type. This helps ensure that Purchase Orders follow the required approval hierarchy before processing.

## Steps to View and Configure PO Approval Settings

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **PO Approval Settings** from the sub-menu.

* The PO Approval Settings screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-58-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-32.png>)

Step 3: Select the required **TM Type** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-59-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Select the required **PO Type** from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-59-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Enable the required **Approval Level**.

1. Select or configure the appropriate **Approval Procedure** as required.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-00-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Review all selected settings carefully.

1. Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-16.png>)

* The PO Approval Settings will be saved successfully.
