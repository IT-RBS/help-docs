# PO Tax

**PO TAX** is used to define and maintain tax details applicable to Purchase Orders in the system. It helps ensure that the correct tax ID and tax name are recorded and used during purchase transactions.

## Steps to Create PO TAX

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu in the system.

1. Click on **Masters**.
2. Select **PO TAX** from the list.

* The PO TAX entry screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-13-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-30.png>)

Step 3: In the PO TAX screen, enter the required **Tax ID** and **Tax Name.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-13-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering the Tax ID and Tax Name, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07.png>)

* The system will save the PO TAX details successfully.
