# Supplier Terms and Condition

The **Supplier Terms and Condition** feature is used to define and maintain specific terms and conditions for suppliers. These terms can be applied during Purchase Order processing and supplier transactions.

## Steps to Create Supplier Terms and Condition

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Supplier Terms and Condition** from the sub-menu.

* The Supplier Terms and Condition entry screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-48-23.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-32.png>)

Step 3: Select the required **Type** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-49-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Select the appropriate **Supplier Name**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-50-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* These details determine the supplier and category for which the terms and conditions will be applied.

Step 4: After selecting the supplier, enter the required **Terms and Conditions** in the provided text area.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-51-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Review the entered details carefully to ensure accuracy.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-52-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-16.png>)

* The Supplier Terms and Condition details will be saved successfully.
