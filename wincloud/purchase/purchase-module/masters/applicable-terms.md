# Applicable Terms

**Applicable Terms** are predefined conditions or rules that are applied to a purchase type in the system. These terms help standardize purchasing rules such as payment conditions, discounts, taxes, or any other agreed terms during procurement.

## Steps to Create Applicable Terms

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu in the system.

1. Click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Applicable Terms** from the list.

The **Applicable Terms** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 13-57-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-30.png>)

Step 3: In the **Applicable Terms** screen, locate the **Purchase Type** field.

* Click on it and select the required **Purchase Type** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 13-58-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-41-59.png>)

* Select the required Applicable Terms from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-01-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the Purchase Type and Applicable Terms, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07.png>)

* The system will save the selected Applicable Terms against the chosen Purchase Type.
