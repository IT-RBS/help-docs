# PO Terms Type

**PO Terms Type** refers to the predefined conditions or terms that are attached to a Purchase Order (PO). These terms define the rules, agreements, or conditions between the buyer and the supplier while placing a purchase order.

## Steps to Create PO Terms Type

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu in the system.

1. Inside the Purchase module, click on **Masters**.
2. From the drop-down options, select **PO Terms Type**.

Step 2: In the PO Terms Type screen, click on the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-29-26.png>)

* A new entry form will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-28-55.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: In the form, enter the following details:

* **PO Terms ID**: Enter a unique identification code for the PO terms.
* **PO Terms Name**: Enter a clear and meaningful name for the terms.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-29-55.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering the required details, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07.png>)

* The system will store the PO Terms Type successfully.
