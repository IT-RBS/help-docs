# Email Template

The **Email Template** feature is used to create and manage predefined email formats for Purchase-related communications. These templates help users send consistent and professional emails efficiently.

## Steps to Create an Email Template

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Email Template** from the sub-menu.

* The Email Template creation screen will appear.

Step 2: Click on the **New** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-29-26.png>)

Step 3: Select the required **Template Name**.

1. Select the appropriate **Type**.
2. Create the email content as per your requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 17-04-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Review the entered template details carefully.

1. Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-22-46.png>)

* The Email Template will be saved successfully.
