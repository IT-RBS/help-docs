# How to find the PO Item Rate Variance?

PO Item Rate Variance refers to the difference between the rate (price) of an item specified in a purchase order (PO) and its agreed or standard rate. This tool is primarily used by the purchasing department to identify pricing discrepancies, track cost variations, or monitor changes in supplier pricing.To view and analyze these price differences in the system, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Click on **Analysis > Po Item Rate Variance.**
3. Click on the Item Search icon to find and select the product you want to analyze.
4. Use the Advance Filter option to refine your search criteria.
5. Click the Display button to load the variance report on your screen.

If you want to perform further data analysis or share the details, you can download the report in Excel format by clicking the Excel button.
