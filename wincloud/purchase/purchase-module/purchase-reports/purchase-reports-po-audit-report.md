# PO Audit Report

**PO Audit Report**

This report is to show any change or modification (if) that has happened in a PO. It will show the data of any PO from creation to its operationally closed status or Completed or Cancelled status.

You can filter by a specific **User**:

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (53).png>)

You can also Search for a Specific PO you are looking for:

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (242).png>)

**Sort** the report based on the following:

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (73).png>)

The “**Critical Change**”, shown below usually means any rate change or cancellation.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/4 (17).png>)

And through **PO Operation**, You can select what kind of transaction status you are looking for:

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/5 (33).png>)

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/6 (35).png>)

So the report will reveal data based on the changes made and what was the data before the changes.
