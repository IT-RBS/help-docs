# Supplier Rate Agreement list

The **Supplier Rate Agreement List** report is used to view the agreed rates (prices) finalized with suppliers for supplying goods or services.

## **Steps to View Supplier Rate Agreement List**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Supplier Rate Agreement List**.

* After clicking **Supplier Rate Agreement List**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-37-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41.png>)option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report using specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-37-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08.png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting needs.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-38-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Supplier Rate Agreement List** report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-39-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21.png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and documentation.
