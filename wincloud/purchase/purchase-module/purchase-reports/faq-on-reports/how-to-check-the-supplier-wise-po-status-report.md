# How to check the Supplier-wise PO Status report?

The Supplier-wise PO Status report is a tracking tool that groups and displays your purchase order status separately for each supplier. This makes it easy to monitor active, pending, or completed orders on a vendor-by-vendor basis.To view this report, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Navigate to **Reports > Supplier Wise PO Status.**
3. Click on the Advance Filter option.
4. Select your desired criteria using the option buttons.
5. Click the Display button to load the report on your screen.

If you need to analyze or share this data, you can download the report in Excel format by clicking the Excel button.
