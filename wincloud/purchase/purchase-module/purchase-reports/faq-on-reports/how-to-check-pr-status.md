# How to check PR status ?

You can check the status of your Purchase Requisitions (PRs) using two different reports, depending on whether you need an overall requisition overview or a detailed item-by-item breakdown.

**1. Purchase Requisition Status Report**

Use this report to view the overall current status of any PRs raised within your organization.

* How to Access:
  1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
  2. Click on **Reports > Purchase Requisition Status Report.**
  3. Click on the Advance Filter option to narrow down your results.
  4. Click the option button to select your specific criteria.
  5. Click the Display button to view the report on your screen.
  6. _(Optional)_ Click the Excel button to download the report in Excel format.

**2. Item-wise PR Status Report**

If you want to track the status of each individual item listed inside your purchase requisitions rather than just the overall PR status, use this report.

* How to Access:
  1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
  2. Click on Reports > Item wise PR Status Report.
  3. Click on the Advance Filter option.
  4. Click the option button to select your criteria.
  5. Click the Display button to load the report.
  6. _(Optional)_ Click the Excel button to download the data.
