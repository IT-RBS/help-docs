# How to check Consolidated Po Status?

The Consolidated PO Status report is a summary report that combines the status of multiple purchase orders (POs) into a single, overall view.To check this status, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Click on **Reports > Consolidated Po Status.**
3. Click on Consolidated Po Status on the screen to open it.
4. Click on the Advance Filter option to narrow down your results.
5. Click the option button to select your specific filter criteria.
6. Click the Display button to view the report on your screen.

If you need to analyze this data further or share it, you can click the Excel button to download the report in Excel format.
