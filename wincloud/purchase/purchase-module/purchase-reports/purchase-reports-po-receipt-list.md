# PO Receipt List

**PO Receipt List**

This report shows you all the items received against all PO (Purchase Orders) generated. It shows the information of GRN such as Bill no. , Date, GRN date and transaction no. etc.

A **Funnel Filter** is provided to search for specific **Supplier** or store etc.
