# Reservation Amendment Audit

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **Reservation Amendment Audit.**

**Reservation Amendment Audit**

This report gives you information on the amendments made in the reservation by the users.  The report can be sorted based on the criteria below for a given time period.
