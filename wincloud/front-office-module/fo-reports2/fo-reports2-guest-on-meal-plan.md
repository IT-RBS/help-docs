# Guest On Meal Plan

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **Guest On Meal Plan**.

**Guest on Meal Plan**

This report gives you the list of Guests with reference to the Meal Plan they are on.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-07 100818.png>)

Multiple Easy to use options are also available to see the report of a specific **Date Range** and add in the **details of the plan** or **summarized** report of the same. You can **Exclude Departure** or **Include Arrivals** based on the requirement and see the **Plan Amount** charged.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-07 100919.png" alt="" width="248">

Option to sort the report based on **Plan** and/ or group with **Company**.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (5).png>)

You can also add multiple columns of information such as **Segment**, **Pax Details** and **Nationality** etc.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/3 (145).png>)

And a search lens to provide the information of specific or multiple plans.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-07 101006.png" alt="" width="251">
