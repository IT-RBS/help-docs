# Guest Bill Reprint Audit

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **Guest Bill Reprint Audit**.

**Guest Bill Reprint Audit**

This report provides you with the information of any reprint of the final bill, who took a printout and the reason for the reprint.
