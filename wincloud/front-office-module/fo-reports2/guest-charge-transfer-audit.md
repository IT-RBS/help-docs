# Guest Charge Transfer Audit

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **Guest Charge Transfer Audit.**

**Guest Charge Transfer Audit**

This report shows the Guest charges transfer audit report from selected date range.
