# InHouse Amend Audit

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **InHouse Amend Audit.**

**Guest Amendment Audit**

This report gives you information on the amendments made in the Guest profiles for In House guests by the users. The report can be sorted based on the criteria below for a given time period.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-07 111821.png" alt="" width="197">
