# Amend Guest Stay Audit

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Advanced Reports** menu.
3. Select **Amend Guest Stay Audit.**

**Amend Guest Stay Audit**

This report gives the information about any modifications or changes made in the stay of any guest by the staff (specific staff or multiple staff or all) within a selected date range. It also shows you the amendment reason.
