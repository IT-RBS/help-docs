# Paid Out Deletion

### **Paid Out Deletion**

Use this option to delete a paid-out transaction made to an in-house guest.

#### **Steps to Paid Out**

1. Go to **Front Office>>Cashier > > Paid Out.**
2. Click on the **view button**<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (54).png" alt="" data-size="line"> on the toolbar.
3. After clicking the _View_ button on the Paid Out screen, the _Voucher_ field displays a magnifier icon for selection.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (51).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Search for already Paid-out vouchers using the magnifier. Click on the desired voucher to be deleted.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (58).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (57).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Delete the voucher by clicking the trash can icon <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (55).png" alt="" data-size="line">on the toolbar.

**Note:-**&#x54;he system will allow modifying/void paid out vouchers of the account date only.
