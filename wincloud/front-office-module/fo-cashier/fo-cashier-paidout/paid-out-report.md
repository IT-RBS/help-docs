# Paid out report

1. Go to Front Office>> Cashier>> Paid out
2. Click on the Report button<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (66).png" alt="" data-size="line"> on the tool bar.
3. Clicking on the report button opens the list of paid outs made.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on the check box next to the desired voucher and click OK.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (67).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. The voucher opens in another window, ready to be printed

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (68).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
