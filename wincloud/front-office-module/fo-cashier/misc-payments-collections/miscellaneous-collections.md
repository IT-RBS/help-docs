# Miscellaneous collections

Miscellaneous collections refer to revenue received from guests on the sale of miscellaneous items or services provided.

* Go to **Front Office>> Cashier>> Miscellaneous payment/collections**.
* Select Miscellaneous Collections from the Type field.
* Enter the voucher number if available.
* Select the revenue account to which the revenue will be charged from the Revenue Account drop-down.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1066).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Enter the amount.
* Use the narration field to provide a detailed explanation of the transaction, especially when the receipt is not part of the regular transaction records
*   Select the payment mode.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1069).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Save ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1073).png>)the transaction.

These revenues find their way to the night audit list.

The toolbar has options to modify(Open​ ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1071).png>)),  and view ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1074).png>)(to delete), but this access is usually given only to( to delete). Still, usually this access is given only to the administrators.
