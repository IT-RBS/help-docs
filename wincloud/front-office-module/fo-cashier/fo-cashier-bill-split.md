# Bill Split

### **Bill Split**

This option is used to split the bills. By using this option, the user can make upto 9 bill splits as required.

#### **Steps to Bill Split**

1. Go to **Front Office>> Cashier>>Checkout>> Bill Split**
2. Then, select the guest either from the guest list or through the Calendar view.&#x20;
   1. Click on the room number field to open the guest list.
   2.  Select the guest.

       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1097).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  Or Select the guest from the Calendar View.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1101).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  Select the guest from the list.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1102).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5.  Click on the Bill Split  button from the bottom of the screen to display the bill charged to the guest.&#x20;

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1103).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6. If the user is allowed to split the amount using the [amount split](fo-cashier-amountsplit.md) functionality and save.
7.  Click next to go to bill settlement.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1104).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
8. Click on the Provisional button to create a [provisional bill](provisional-bill.md)
9. Click on the Bill & Settle button [to settle bills](fo-cashier-bill-settle-cancel.md).
10. Click on the Print Bill button to print bills.
