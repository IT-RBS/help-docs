# Check-out

### **Checkout**

Whenever a user wants to checkout of a room, then this option can be used. When the user selects the room number to check out in this window, then the system will display as shown below.

#### **Steps to Checkout**

1. Go to **Front Office>>Cashier >>Check Out.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (127).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Select the room number to be checked out using the magnifier icon in the Room Number field. The system will then display the corresponding reservation details.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (128).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. If you attempt to check out the room before the scheduled departure date, the system will display a warning message. Click “Yes” to proceed with the checkout or “No” to return to the previous screen.

<div align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-13 113722.png" alt=""></div>

If the rate has not been posted for the reservation, then the system asks for posting the rate. To continue, check yes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (129).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The system then dispalys all the pending bills and POS bills which needs settlement.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (130).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Clicking on the bill details button displays all the bill details of the guest.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (132).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. There are options like Bill Split, Bill details, Advance, Guest Charge, Refund, and Bar Post. These options can be used by the user based on the guest's requirements.
   1. If a guest wants to split the bill before billing, the bill split is an option.
   2. Bar posting can be used to effectively block posting other bills to a room once the final bill is produced.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (134).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* [Bar posting](../fo-frontdesk/fo-frontdesk-bar-posting/)&#x20;
* [Charges](guest-charges/fo-cashier-guest-charges.md)
* [Advances](advances-and-refunds/)
* [Refunds](advances-and-refunds/)
* [Bill Split](fo-cashier-bill-split.md)

8. Clicking on the 'I' button next to the 'checkout inst' field displays any instructions both the Housekeeping department and the front desk department were given during reservation & check-in time.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (994).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. To see the details of the bill, click on the bill details button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (995).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Clicking on the routing button helps you to route the bill according to the guest's preference.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (996).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. The user can edit splits, add or delete routing of the bill at this point.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (997).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9.  The user has to add group revenue to continue

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (998).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
10. &#x20;Click on the revenue to select the revenues to be grouped.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (999).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Click the OK button and then click Save. Click on the 'Back button' in the bill detail screen to go back to the 'Check-out' screen.

9\. Click **Bill Print** to Preview and print the Guest Bill or click **Bill & Settle** to print & settle the bill.\
The user can choose to **send an email and print,** or **send an email and skip print**

<div align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-13 114045.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

10\. Click **Save** to print the bill or **Cancel** to settle the bill.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-13 114304.png>)

11\. Select the **settlement mode** and click **Save** to complete the check out process

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-13 114739.png>)
