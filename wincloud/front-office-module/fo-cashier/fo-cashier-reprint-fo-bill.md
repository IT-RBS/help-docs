# Reprint Bill

### **Reprint the FO bill**

Allows for the reprint of checked-out guest bills.

#### **Steps to Reprint FO Bill**

1. &#x20;Go to **Front Office>>** **Cashier >> Reprint FO Bill.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (83).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Select a date range to display the checked-out bills that need reprinting, then click the display button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (84).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the bill that needs to be reprinted, and provide the reprint reason.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (85).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Double-click on the record to open the bill to recheck.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (86).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
