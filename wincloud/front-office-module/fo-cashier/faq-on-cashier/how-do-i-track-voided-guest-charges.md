# How do I track voided guest charges?

To track voided or cancelled guest charges, you can utilize specific audit reports designed to provide transparency into modifications and deletions within the system:

* **Void Bills Report:** This report is specifically used to see all bills that have been cancelled in the Front Office after a checkout bill or final bill was generated.
* **Cashier Transaction Audit:** This is a comprehensive tool for monitoring any changes made by a cashier. It shows all instances where a cashier has cancelled, modified, or transferred charges. To isolate voided items, you can select the specific checkbox to see only **"Cancelled"** or **"Amended"** transactions.
* **Verification Prior to Night Audit:** As part of the daily closing process, all settled and voided bill copies from all outlets and modules must be verified against system reports. You can use the "void bill report option" provided in the Front Office module for this purpose.
* **Audit Logs and Reasons:** When a guest charge is deleted through the Guest Charges menu **(Cashier > Guest Charges > Delete)**, the system requires the user to provide a mandatory reason for deleting the record. This reason, along with the identity of the staff member who performed the action, is stored in the logs and reflected in the relevant audit reports.
