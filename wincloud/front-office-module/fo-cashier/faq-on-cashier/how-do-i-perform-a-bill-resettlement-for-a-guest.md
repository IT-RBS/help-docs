# How do I perform a Bill Resettlement for a guest?

Bill Resettlement allows you to **modify the payment mode** of a bill that has already been settled. This is particularly useful if a guest decides to pay using a different method after the initial transaction has been closed.

To perform a Bill Resettlement, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the **Cashier Main Menu**.
2. Click on **Cashier > Bill Resettlement**.
3. Click on the **Bill No** field to view a list of all bills that have been settled in the system.
4. **Select the specific bill** that needs to be re-settled from the list.
5. Choose the new **settlement mode** and provide a mandatory **reason** for the change in the Reason box.
6. Click **Save** to complete the resettlement process.

It is important to ensure all necessary resettlements are completed during the **mandatory pre-audit checks**, as these cannot be performed once the business day is closed during the Night Audit.
