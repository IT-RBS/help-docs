# How do I perform a Bill Cancellation?

To perform a **Bill Cancellation**, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the **Cashier Main Menu**.
2. Select **Cashier > Bill Settlement / Bill Cancellation**.
3. Click the **Refresh** button and then click on the **Bill No** field to display a list of pending bills.
4. **Select the specific bill** you wish to cancel from the list.
5. Click the **cancel button**, which will trigger a **cancel reason box** to appear.
6. Provide a **valid reason** for the cancellation and click **Save** to complete the process.

#### **Key Considerations**

* **Purpose:** This function is specifically used to cancel a checkout bill that has already been created.
* **Access Control:** Permissions for performing bill cancellations can be **restricted on a per-user basis** to ensure security and accountability.
* **Audit Verification:** All cancelled or **voided bills** must be verified against system reports, such as the **Void Bill Report**, as part of the mandatory checks before performing a Night Audit.
* **Relation to Checkout:** If a bill was settled and the guest checked out, but you need to undo the process, you may first need to use the **Reinstate Checkout** option, which also involves cancelling the settlement and the bill.
