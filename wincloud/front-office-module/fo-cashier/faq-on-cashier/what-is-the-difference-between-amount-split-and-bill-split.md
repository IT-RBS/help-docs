# What is the difference between amount split and bill split?

The primary difference between an amount split and a bill split lies in whether you are dividing the cost of a single transaction or organizing multiple transactions into separate documents.

**Amount Split**

**Purpose:** This is used to divide the specific amount of a single revenue item.

**Functionality:** You can split the amount either percentage-wise or by a specific amount.

**Use Case:** It is ideal for situations where guests want to share the cost of a specific charge equally. Once the amount is divided, the user can proceed with a bill split.

**Bill Split**

**Purpose:** This option is used to generate multiple separate bill documents.

**Functionality:** The system allows for up to 9 different bill splits. You assign a bill number (e.g., "1" or "2") to each revenue item listed on the guest's account.

**Result:** The system generates separate bills based on these numbers; for example, all items marked as "1" will appear on the first bill, while items marked "2" will be on the second.

Both of these options are found in the **Cashier > Check Out** menu on the bill summary screen,.
