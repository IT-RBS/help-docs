# How to void a guest charge if I made a mistake

## **How Do I Void a Guest Charge if I Made a Mistake?**

To **void a guest charge** that was posted incorrectly, you must use the **Guest Charges Deletion process** within the **Cashier module**.

## **Steps to Void a Guest Charge**

### **1. Navigate to the Menu**

* Go to **Cashier Main Menu > Cashier > Guest Charges**.

### **2. View Posted Charges**

* Click the **View button** located in the **toolbar** to display all **guest charges that have already been posted**.

### **3. Select the Record**

* From the displayed list, select the **specific guest and charge** that you want to remove.

### **4. Execute Deletion**

* Click the **Delete button**.

### **5. Provide a Reason**

* When prompted, enter a **valid reason for deleting the record**.
* Click **OK** to finalize the **void process**.

## **Modifying vs. Voiding**

If you do not want to **delete the charge completely** and only need to **correct an error**, you can modify the bill instead:

* Click the **Refresh button** in the **Guest Charges window** to enter **Open mode**.
* Select the **bill**, make the required **adjustments**, and click the **Save button**.

## **Important Constraints**

* **Current Date Only:** You can only **post, modify, or delete guest charges** based on the **current accounting date**. The system does not allow selecting or changing charges from **previous days**.
* **Audit Trail:** All **amendments and deletions** are saved in a **log** and will be reflected in the relevant **audit reports**.
