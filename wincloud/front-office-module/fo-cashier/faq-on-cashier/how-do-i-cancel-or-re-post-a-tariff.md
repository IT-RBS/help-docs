# How do I cancel or re-post a tariff?

To cancel or re-post a tariff that has already been recorded, you must use the Post Tariff-Single option within the Cashier module.Follow these steps to manage the posted record:

1. **Access the Menu:** Go to Cashier Main Menu > Cashier > Post Tariff - Single.
2. **Select the Room:** Choose the specific room number for which the tariff has already been posted.
3. **Choose the Action:** The system will automatically display options to either Re-post or Cancel the tariff.

**To Cancel a Tariff**

* Click the Cancel button to delete the already posted record.
* In the pop-up message box that appears, provide a valid reason for why you are deleting the tariff.
* Click OK to complete the deletion of the record.

**To Re-post a Tariff**

* If you have modified the tariff and need to post it again, click the Re-post button.
* Enter the reason for re-posting the tariff in the pop-up message box.
* Click OK to finalize the update and re-post the tariff
