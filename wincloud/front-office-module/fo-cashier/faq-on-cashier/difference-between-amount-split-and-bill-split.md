# Difference between Amount Split and Bill Split

## **What Is the Difference Between Amount Split and Bill Split?**

The **primary difference between an Amount Split and a Bill Split** is whether you are **dividing the cost of a single transaction** or **organizing multiple transactions into separate bill documents**.

## **Amount Split**

* **Purpose:**\
  Used to **divide the specific amount of a single revenue item**.
* **Functionality:**\
  The amount can be split:
  * **Percentage-wise**
  * By a **specific amount**
* **Use Case:**\
  Ideal when guests want to **share the cost of a specific charge equally**.
* **Process:**\
  Once the amount is divided, the user can proceed with a **Bill Split** if required.

## **Bill Split**

* **Purpose:**\
  Used to **generate multiple separate bill documents**.
* **Functionality:**\
  The system allows up to **9 different bill splits**.\
  A **bill number** (for example, **"1" or "2"**) is assigned to each revenue item listed on the guest's account.
* **Result:**\
  The system creates **separate bills** based on the assigned numbers:
  * Items marked as **"1"** appear on the **first bill**.
  * Items marked as **"2"** appear on the **second bill**.

## **Location**

Both **Amount Split** and **Bill Split** options are available in:

**Cashier > Check Out > Bill Summary Screen**
