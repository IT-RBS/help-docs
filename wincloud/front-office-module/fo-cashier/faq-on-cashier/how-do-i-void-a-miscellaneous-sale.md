# How do I void a miscellaneous sale?

To void or delete a miscellaneous sale bill created for an outsider, follow these steps within the Cashier module:

**Access the Menu:** Go to the **Cashier Main Menu** and select **Cashier > Guest charges.**

**Locate the Bill:** Click the refresh button and then click the view button to browse and select the specific bill number you need to delete.

**Execute Deletion:** Once the bill is opened, click the **Delete** button.

**Confirm the Action:** A message box will appear asking to continue; click Yes to proceed with the process.

**Enter a Reason:** You must provide a valid explanation in the cancel reason box and then **click OK** to complete the deletion.
