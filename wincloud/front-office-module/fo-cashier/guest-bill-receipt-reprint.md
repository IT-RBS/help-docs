# Guest Bill Receipt Reprint

The Guest Bill Receipt Reprint option helps to reprint a guest bill.

1.  Go to Front Office>>Cashier>>Guest Bill Receipt Reprint

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1139).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  Select a date range from the calendars given on the menu bar.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1140).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  The user can also apply an advanced filter next to the from and to fields.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1141).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  Click on the Display button to open the guest list.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1142).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5.  Double-click on the bill to be reprinted. And the bill is displayed in detail for inspection.&#x20;

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1143).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6.  After inspection, click on the print button.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1144).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
