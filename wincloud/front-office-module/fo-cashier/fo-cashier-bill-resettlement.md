# Resettlement

### **Bill Resettlement**

Allows to resettle the payment mode. If the payment mode needs to be changed after the bill is settled, you can use the Bill Resettlement option to update the transaction

#### **Steps to Bill Resettlement**

1. Go to **Front** **Office>>** **Cashier > Bill Resettlement.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1019).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on the magnifier in the 'Bill no' field to view the list of bills that were previously settled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1018).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Click on the pencil icon to select the settlement mode. Make the necessary changes to the card and click save.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1020).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Once the changes have been saved, click the **Resettle button**. A pop-up box is displayed asking for the reason for resettlement. Enter the reason and click OK.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1021).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. &#x20;The system confirms the resettlement.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1022).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
