# Deleting an Advance

This option is used to **delete** the posted charges from the guest ledger.

### **Steps to Delete an advance**

1. Go to Front Office >> Cashier >> Advances.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (75).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on the **View button** <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (70).png" alt="" data-size="line">to see all the guest advances posted already.
3. Choose the room/reservation criteria from the 'For' field in the voucher search.
4. Select the voucher number to be deleted from the 'voucher No' field.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (74).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. After selecting the user, click the _Delete_ button to proceed

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (72).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Click the _Delete_ button.
8. Enter the reason for deletion.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (73).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Click _OK_ to confirm and delete the charges.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (76).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
