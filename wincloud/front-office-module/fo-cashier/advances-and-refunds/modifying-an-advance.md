# Modifying an Advance

This option is used to **modify** the posted charges from the guest ledger.

### **Steps to Modify an advance**

1. Go to Front Office >> Cashier >> Advances.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1032).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on the **open button** <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1033).png" alt="" data-size="line">to see all the guest advances posted already.
3. Choose the room/reservation criteria from the 'For' field in the voucher search.
4. Select the voucher number to be modified from the 'voucher No' field.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1035).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1034).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Edit the record and click save<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1037).png" alt="" data-size="line">.
6. The system asks for the amendment reason. Fill in the narrative and click OK.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1036).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
