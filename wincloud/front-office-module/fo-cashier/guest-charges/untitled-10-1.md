# Guest Charge transfer

### **Guest Charge transfer**

This option is used to transfer guest charges from one room to another room.

#### **Steps to Guest Charge Transfer**

1.Go to **Front Office>>Cashier >>Guest Charge Transfer.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (152).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Select a room number from the ''**From Room'** using the magnifier (the room from which the charge will be transferred).
  * The system will display the in-house guest list.
  * Choose the guest from the list
* Select a room number from the '**To Room'** using the magnifier(the room to which the charge will be transferred).
  * The system will display the in-house guest list.
  * Choose the guest from the list
* Select the **Revenue Item** to be transferred.
*   Add the reason of transfer in the reason field.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (153).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Now, click on the save button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (154).png>) on the top right corner of the window to confirm the transfer.
