# Provisional Bill

### **Steps to create a Provisional Bill**

1.Go to **Front Office>>Cashier >>Provisional Bill.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (976).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the Room No using the magnifier in the Room number field.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-13 111701.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click the magnifier icon to open the In-House Guest List. Double-click the required record to select the guest and room.
5.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (977).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6. Once the guest has been selected, click on the **Next button** to create a Provisional Bill

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (979).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. Click on the **Provisional button** to create a provisional bil&#x6C;**.**&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (980).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (107).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Click OK to continue creating the bill.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (108).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
