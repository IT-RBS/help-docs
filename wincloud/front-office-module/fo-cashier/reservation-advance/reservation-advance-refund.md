# Reservation Advance Refund

1. Go to the **Reservation Main Menu.**
2. Click on **Reservation> Reservation advance refund.**
3. Click Reservation No., then choose the required guest or reservation number from the displayed list, as shown below.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (356).png>)

4. Once the guest or reservation has been selected, enter the amount details and choose the appropriate refund payment mode, as shown below.

<div align="left"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (378).png" alt=""></div>

5. Click Save to complete the process. A confirmation pop-up will then appear, as shown below.

<div align="left"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (494).png" alt=""></div>
