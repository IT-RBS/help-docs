# Reservation Advance

1. Go to **Front office>>Reservation > Reservation advance**
2. Select the Reservation No. option, then choose the specific guest or reservation number from the Reservation Search screen displayed below.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (354).png>)

3. Once the guest or reservation has been selected, enter the amount details and choose the appropriate payment mode, as shown below.

<div align="left"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (435).png" alt=""></div>

4. Click Save to complete the process. A confirmation pop-up will then appear, as shown below.

<div align="left"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (758).png" alt=""></div>
