# Foreign Exchange Reprint

### **Foreign Exchange Reprint**

This option will help the user take a reprint of the Foreign Exchange.

#### **Steps for Foreign Exchange Reprint**

1.  Go to **Front Office**>>**Cashier >> Foreign exchange Reprint.**

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1136).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



2. Select the required transaction number (Trn No.) for reprinting. Then, provide a reason in the ‘Reprint Reason’ field located at the bottom before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1137).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on the print button to print.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1138).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
