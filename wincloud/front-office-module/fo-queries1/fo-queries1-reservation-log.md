# Reservation Log

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **Reservation Log**.

**Reservation Log**

This report is to see Modification or changes made to a reservation, who made it and the reasons for the modifications.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 120724.png>)

You can also filter Cancelled reservations in report.
