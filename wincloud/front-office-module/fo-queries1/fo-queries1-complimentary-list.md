# Complimentary Stay List

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **Complimentary Stay.**

**Complimentary List**

This report gives you the list of all the Complimentary guests staying with you **In House** by default, which includes both **House Use** and **Compliment**.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 115722.png>)

And By addition the list of guests stayed with you previously. The user can choose from the date range to the past days details

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 115941.png>)

As usual it features a **Funnel Filter** to sort or group the reports based on Selection.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 120056.png" alt="" width="375">

And an **Options filter** to add the columns of additional info.
