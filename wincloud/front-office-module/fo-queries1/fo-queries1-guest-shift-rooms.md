# Shifted Rooms

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **Shift Rooms.**&#x20;

**Guest Shift Rooms**

This report is to see the room shift that happened on the day or within a selected period.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 113747.png>)

Check box of **Date Range** shows you the specific date period and **Reason Summary** gives the direct no. of shifts that happened along with the reason.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (179).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (97).png>)

If you want to see the detailed report and information of shift, remove the checkbox of Reason Summary.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 114037.png>)
