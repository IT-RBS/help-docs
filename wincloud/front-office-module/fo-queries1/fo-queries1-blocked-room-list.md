# Blocked Room List

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **Blocked Room List**.

**Blocked Room List**

This report helps you to see all the rooms blocked currently, till recently and previously with filter of Out of Order or Out of Service or both with a few other features to filter and also to sort report Room No. wise or Room Type wise.
