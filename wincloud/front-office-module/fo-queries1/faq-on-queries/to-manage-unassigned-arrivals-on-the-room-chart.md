# To manage unassigned arrivals on the Room Chart

**How do I manage unassigned arrivals on the Room Chart?**

To manage unassigned arrivals on the **Room Chart** (also referred to as the **Calendar**), follow these steps:

1. **Access the Calendar:**\
   Navigate to **FRONT OFFICE → QUERIES → CALENDAR**.
2. **View Unassigned Arrivals:**\
   Click the **three dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner of the screen to view the list of unassigned arrivals for the current day.
3. **Open the Due-in Screen:**\
   Click the **Due-in** icon to access the list of guests who are due to arrive.
4. **Assign Rooms:**\
   From the **Due-in** screen, you can assign rooms by either:
   * **Manually** selecting a room number for each guest.
   * Clicking **Auto Assign** to let the system automatically allocate available rooms.

This feature enables you to quickly assign rooms and transition reserved guests to **In-House** status directly from the visual **Room Chart** interface.
