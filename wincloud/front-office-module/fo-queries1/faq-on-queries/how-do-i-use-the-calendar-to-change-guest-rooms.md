# How do I use the Calendar to change guest rooms?

To use the Calendar (also referred to as the Room Chart) to change guest rooms, follow these steps:

1\. **Access the Calendar:** Navigate to the **FRONT OFFICE** module, select **QUERIES**, and click on **CALENDAR**, or access it directly from the Front Office Homepage.

2\. **Move the Guest:** Locate the guest's current room on the chart. Drag and drop your cursor towards the right-hand side against the new room number and date to move the guest from one room to another.

This method provides a visual way to manage room assignments. Alternatively, you can use the Search Bookings option within the calendar to search for specific rooms or guests to perform operations like amending in-house information. If you need to perform more detailed operations on an occupied room, clicking the guest's block on the calendar will open a pop-up menu for amending reservations or viewing guest profiles.
