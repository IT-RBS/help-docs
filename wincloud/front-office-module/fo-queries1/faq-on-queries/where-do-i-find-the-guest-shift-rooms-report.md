# Where do I find the Guest Shift Rooms report

**Which reports help track the Average Length of Stay?**

To track the **Average Length of Stay (ALOS)** and related stay duration metrics, you can utilize several specific analytical and operational reports within the system:

**Primary Report: Average Length of Stay Report**

This dedicated analysis report indicates the average number of days a person remains at the property. It is a critical tool for management because it:

* **Analyzes** the effect of stay duration on revenue and tariffs.
* **Allows** segmentation by country, company, and room type to identify which guest profiles stay the longest.
* **Provides** search/filter functions to analyze nights, revenue, and ADR collectively.

**Operational Tracking and Forecasts**

* **Guest In House Report:** This report features a **"Duration of Stay"** filter, allowing you to generate a list of guests staying for a minimum specified number of days (for example, guests staying more than **X** days).
* **Package Revenue Forecast:** This forward-looking report estimates future income by analyzing several factors, including the length of stay derived from guest arrival and departure dates.
* **Arrivals Register:** When viewing expected arrivals, you can add a **"Room Night"** column to the report. This shows the total number of nights each guest is scheduled to stay before check-in.

**Related Analysis Reports**

While not exclusively for ALOS, these reports track the **Room Nights** data necessary to calculate stay averages:

* **Segment Analysis (MTD/YTD):** Displays total nights for different market segments for the current day, month, and year.
* **Room Wise Income Analysis:** Provides a report on Room Nights and ARR by room number or room type for a selected month range.
* **CMIS Report:** Analyzes total nights stayed categorized by guest nationality or country of residence, helping identify trends in stay patterns from different source markets.
