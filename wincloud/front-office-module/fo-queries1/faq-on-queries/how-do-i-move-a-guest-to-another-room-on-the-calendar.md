# How do I move a guest to another room on the calendar?

To move a guest to another room using the Calendar (Room Chart), you can utilise the visual drag-and-drop functionality.

**Moving a Guest via the Calendar**

1. **Navigate to the Calendar:** Go to **FRONT OFFICE > QUERIES > CALENDAR.**
2. **Execute the Move:** Locate the guest's current room on the chart. Drag and drop your cursor towards the right-hand side against the desired new Room number and Date to move the guest from one room to another.

**Alternative: Using the Change Room Menu**

If you prefer to perform the move through a standard menu process rather than the visual chart, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Front Desk Main Menu > Front Desk > Change Room Menu**.
2. **Select the Guest:** Choose the specific Guest Name for whom you would like to change the room.
3. **Choose the New Room:** Select the destination Room no.
4. **Provide a Reason:** Enter the reason for the move in the Narration field.
5. **Finalise:** Click Save to complete the process.

Regardless of which method you use, the system will record the change. You can later verify these movements by viewing the Shifted Rooms report, which lists all room shifts that occurred on a specific day along with the reasons provided.
