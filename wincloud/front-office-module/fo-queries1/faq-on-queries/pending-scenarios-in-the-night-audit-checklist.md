# Pending scenarios in the Night Audit Checklist

**What are the common pending scenarios in the Night Audit Checklist?**

The **Night Audit Checklist** identifies pending tasks that must be completed before the system allows the **Night Audit** to proceed. Common pending scenarios include:

#### **1. Tariff Not Posted**

* Indicates that room tariffs have not been posted for all in-house guests.
* Complete this task using the **Post Tariff All** option, typically after **10:00 PM**.

#### **2. Plan Not Posted**

* Appears when plan charges for in-house guests have not yet been posted or finalized.

#### **3. Overdue Departures**

* Occurs when guests scheduled to depart on the current date are still marked as **In-House**.
* These reservations must be:
  * Checked out, or
  * Extended if the guest is continuing their stay.

#### **4. Checked-Out Guest with Unpaid Bills**

* Indicates that a guest has been checked out and a final bill has been generated, but the bill has not yet been settled.

#### **5. Pending KOT (Kitchen Order Tickets)**

* The Night Audit cannot proceed if there are pending **KOTs** in the **Restaurant** or **Room Service** outlets.
* These KOTs must be:
  * Settled by generating a bill, or
  * Cancelled in the **POS** module.

#### **6. Pending Bills**

* Refers to bills created in the **POS** module that are still **unsettled** or **uncancelled**.

Clearing these pending items ensures that all guest transactions and revenue are accurately recorded before the system advances to the next accounting date during the **Night Audit**.
