# Which reports help track the Average Length of Stay

### Reports for Tracking Average Length of Stay (ALOS)

To track the Average Length of Stay (ALOS) and related stay duration metrics, you can utilize several specific analytical and operational reports within the system:

#### 1. Primary Report: Average Length of Stay

This dedicated analysis report indicates the average number of days a person remains at the property. It is a critical tool for management because it:

* Analyzes the effect of stay duration on revenue and tariffs.
* Allows for segmentation by country, company, and room type to identify which guest profiles stay the longest.
* Provides a search/filter function to analyze nights, revenue, and ADR collectively.

#### 2. Operational Tracking and Forecasts

* Guest In House Report: This report features a "Duration of Stay" filter, which allows you to generate a list of guests staying for a minimum specified number of days (e.g., guests staying more than "X" days).
* Package Revenue Forecast: This forward-looking report estimates future income by analyzing several factors, including the length of stay derived from guest arrival and departure dates.
* Arrivals Register: When viewing expected arrivals, you can add a "Room Night" column to the report. This shows the total number of nights each guest is scheduled to stay before they even check in.

#### 3. Related Analysis Reports

While not exclusively for ALOS, these reports track the "Room Nights" data necessary to calculate stay averages:

* Segment Analysis (MTD/YTD): Displays the total Nights for different market segments for the current day, month, and year.
* Room wise Income Analysis: Provides a report on Room Nights and ARR specifically by room number or room type for a selected month range.
* CMIS Report: Analyzes total nights stayed categorized by the guests’ nationality or country of residence, helping identify trends in stay patterns from different source markets.
