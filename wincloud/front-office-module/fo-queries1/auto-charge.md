# Auto Charge

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **Auto Charge**.

View the Auto Charges posted for Inhouse or Expected guest. Users can choose from the date range as shown below.
