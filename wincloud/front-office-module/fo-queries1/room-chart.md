# Calendar

**ROOM CHART**

A Calendar provides a complete overview of all defined events for a specific period of time. Hotels can manage and keep track of their Occupancies, Reservations, Check-ins and outs, posting of charges etc. from the calendar screen&#x20;

1. To view this, go to “FRONT OFFICE” module “QUERIES ” “CALENDAR” or from the Front office Homepage as shown below

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-28 101833.png>)
