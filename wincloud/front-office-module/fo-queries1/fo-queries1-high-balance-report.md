# High Balance Report

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Queries menu**.
3. Select **High Balance Report.**&#x20;

**High Balance Report**

This report shows you the balance amount for in house guests and their credit limits (if set). The report is to alert you if the outstanding amount is greater than their allowed limits.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 114257.png>)

Check boxes are provided to see the specific info. And/ or to add the info. In report:

**Guest Balance** is to see the current Balance pending from guest.

**Cut off Limit** is to see the cut off based on the amount you enter in the corresponding field of **Cut off Amount**.

**Credit Limit** is to see the Credit Limit given to the Guest

**CC Preauthorization** where guest has given Credit Card details and has authorized to use it up to a certain amount.

**PM** is to see Pay Master Rooms.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (170).png>)

To see Company Guests with **Company** Name or Individuals and **Credit Card Details**.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (110).png>)

**Funnel Filter** is provided to search for specific **Company, Group and Agent**. **Sorting Button** is provided to sort and sequence the report based on the requirement

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-31 114359.png>)

**Options button** is provided to add the information as columns as shown below.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/5 (24).png>)

.

Report will be generated as on date and will reflect the information based on your selection of filters.
