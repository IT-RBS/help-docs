# Add-On Reservations

### Creating an ADD on Reservation

**Accessing the Reservation System**

* Go to the **Front Office** module.&#x20;
* Select **Reservation**.&#x20;
* Click on **Reservation** again to open the full reservation menu.&#x20;

**Opening the Add-On Option**

* In the reservation menu, locate the **ellipsis/the three dots (⋮) menu icon**.&#x20;
* Click the icon to reveal additional options.&#x20;
* Select **Add-On** from the dropdown list.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (886).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Wincloud displays a comprehensive reservation list, allowing users to view all existing reservations. From this list, users can select a specific reservation to edit or update its details as required.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (887).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Entering Add-On Reservation Details

1. **Provide Required Information**
   * Enter the necessary details for the add-on reservation, including:
     * Dates&#x20;
     * Room type&#x20;
     * Special requests (if any)&#x20;
2. **Verify Accuracy**
   * Ensure that all entered information is **accurate, complete, and up to date** before proceeding.
3. **Save**
4. **Review the Rate Edit Screen**
   * Upon saving, the Rate Edit screen is displayed. Here, you can:&#x20;
   * Decide and confirm the applicable rates for the reservation.&#x20;
   * Review and verify the accuracy of other reservation details before proceeding.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (888).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. After entering the required details, save the Rate Edit screen to proceed. Once saved, the system will automatically navigate to the Reservation Add-On screen, where you can continue managing additional services or extras for the reservation.
6. Save again.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (889).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Sending Confirmation for Add-On Reservation
   * Send Confirmation
     * Send a confirmation email or message to the guest after creating the add-on reservation.
   * Include Reservation Details
     * Ensure the confirmation includes all relevant information, such as:
     * Check-in and check-out dates
     * Room type
     * Any additional services or add-ons included
