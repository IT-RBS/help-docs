# Reservation Check-in

1. Go to the Reservation Main Menu.
2. Click on Reservation>> Reservation Check-In.
3. Click on the Open Icon to <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (261).png" alt="" data-size="line"> open the reservation list.
4. Select the reservation to be checked by double-clicking the reservation row.

<div data-full-width="false"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-03 165652.png" alt=""></div>

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (401).png>)

5. Once the reservation is created or selected, navigate to the Room Selection option if a room has not already been assigned.
6. From the Room Selection screen, choose the appropriate room type and room number based on availability.
7. Confirm the selection to update the reservation with the assigned room.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-03 173839.png>)

8. Click on the Check-in icon to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (263).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Once the check-in is initiated, the following pop-up appears asking for a registration card to be printed.
10. Check the box if a registration card is to be printed.
11. Click OK to complete the Check-In process.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (264).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
