# How does the forecast track room nights and ADR?

The system tracks **room nights** and **ADR (Average Daily Rate)** through several forecasting and analytical reports by evaluating both existing reservations and future booking trends.

#### **Key Reports for Tracking Nights and ADR**

* **Business on Books Report:** This report specifically calculates revenue for a given month by considering three main factors: **Total Nights, Revenue Generated (or to be generated)**, and **ADR**. It can be filtered by room type and can exclude tentative bookings to focus only on confirmed business.
* **Forecast Report:** This tool tracks the **future contribution of various market segments** (such as corporate or individuals) for metrics including **Nights, Pax, ADR, ARP, and RevPAR** over a selected future date range.
* **Segment Analysis (MTD/YTD):** This report provides a detailed breakdown of **Nights** and **ADR** for different segments, showing data for the current day, month, and year to analyze performance trends.
* **History and Forecast Report:** It combines past performance **(rooms sold and revenue collected)** with **possible future sales** based on current bookings within a selected period, allowing for a comparison of actual vs. projected nights and revenue.

#### **Analytical Breakdowns**

* **Rate Code Analysis:** This provides information on **Room Nights** based on specific rates and their overall contribution to the property's revenue.
* **Average Length of Stay:** This metric indicates the average number of days a guest remains and analyzes how this duration affects both total revenue and **ADR**.
* **Room wise Income Analysis:** This gives a specific report on income generated by room number or type, showing **Room Nights, Revenues**, and **ARR (Average Room Rate)** for a selected month range.
