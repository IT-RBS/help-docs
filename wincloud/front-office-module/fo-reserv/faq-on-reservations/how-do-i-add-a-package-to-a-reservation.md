# How do I add a Package to a reservation?

To add a package to a reservation, follow these steps within the reservation screen:

1. **Open the Package Allotment Window:** Click on the **Packages button** located in the top right corner of the reservation window.
2. **Select the Package:** A list of pre-created packages will appear. These packages are established in the **Guest Specialities >> Packages** master. Choose the package you intend to apply and click **OK**.
3. **Add Additional Services:** If the guest requests extras that are not part of the standard package, such as a **taxi service, city tour, or extra bed**, click the **Revenue button** next to the selected package.
4. **Confirm Services:** Select the requested services from the **Revenue Search list** and click **OK** to add them to the guest's package.
5. **Save:** Click the **Save** button to finalize the package allocation for the reservation.

You can also view a detailed **Packages report** to track all packages assigned to in-house, expected, or departed guests by navigating to the queries section.
