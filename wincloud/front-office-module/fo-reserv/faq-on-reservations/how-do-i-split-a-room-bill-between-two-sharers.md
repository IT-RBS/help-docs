# How do I split a room bill between two sharers?

To split a room bill between two guests sharing a room (sharers), the system provides several methods depending on whether you want to set up the split at the beginning of the stay or handle it manually during checkout.

1. **During Reservation (The Sharer Button)**

When initially creating the reservation, you can designate the stay as a shared booking:

* **Enable Sharer Status:** While entering guest details, tick the Sharer button box to indicate the bill will be shared.
* **Master Configuration:** For this to work correctly, the room must be eligible in the backend; ensure the sharer room option is enabled in the Room Master for that specific room number.

2. **Automated Bill Splitting (Routing)**

If you want the system to automatically divide the charges (such as the room tariff) between the two sharers throughout their stay, use the Routing tool:

* **Access Routing:** Click the Routing button after the guests and room have been assigned.
* **Assign Splits:** In the "Assign to" field, enter 2 (for two guests).
* **Select Guests:** Use the Edit split button to assign each split to the respective guest's name.
* **Choose Revenues:** Double-click the Group/Revenue field to select the specific charges (e.g., Room Tariff, Taxes) that should be automatically divided between their folios.

3. **During Checkout (Manual Split)**

If the guests request to split the bill at the end of their stay, you can perform this in the Checkout screen within the Cashier module:

* **Amount Split:** Use this option if the guests want to divide a specific charge by a certain value. You can split the amount percentage-wise (e.g., 50/50) or by a specific amount. Select the revenue item, enter the split details, and click Split Amount.
* **Bill Split:** If you need to generate entirely separate physical bills, select Bill Split. You can assign different revenue items to different bill numbers (e.g., Bill "1" for Guest A and Bill "2" for Guest B), and the system will generate them accordingly.

4. **Group Reservations**

If the sharers are part of a group booking, you can use the Split button within the Group Reservation screen. Select the reservation line, click Split, and enter the number of splits required to divide the booking between all guests.
