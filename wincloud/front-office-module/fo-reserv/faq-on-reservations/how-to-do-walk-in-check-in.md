# How to do walk-in check-in ?

To perform a Walk-In Check-In, you should navigate to the Reservation Main Menu and select the Walk In Check-In option.

**Follow these steps to complete the process:**

1. **Enter Stay Details:** Choose the Room type, number of Nights, number of Adults, and the Rate type.
2. **Select a Room:** Click on the Room No. field to assign a specific room to the guest.
3. **Guest Information:** For a new guest, type the name directly into the system; for a repeat guest, search for and select their existing guest profile from the list.
4. **Analytical and Billing Details** (Optional): If required, you can choose specific details such as Guest type, Guest status, Reserve Mode, Billing instructions, Payment Mode, and Business Source. Providing the Business Source is especially important for the hotel's analytical reports.
5. **Finalise Check-In**: Click the **CheckIn** button to complete the process. A pop-up window will appear confirming that the walk-in check-in has been successfully completed.

**Note on Registration Cards:** If you need to provide the guest with paperwork, you can print a Plain Registration Card specifically for walk-in guests by selecting **"Plain Reg. Card"** and clicking the Print icon during the check-in process.
