# How do I create a new Guest Profile?

To create a new guest profile, you typically initiate the process during the reservation or check-in workflow. Follow these steps based on the sources:

### **During Reservation Creation**

* **Standard Reservation:** When creating a new reservation, go to the guest name section and **type the names** of the individuals in the designated columns by clicking the pencil icon or double-clicking the row.
* **Quick Reservation:** If you are using the Quick Reservation window (accessed via the calendar), enter the required guest details and check the **Create as new Guest** button before saving the booking.

### **Adding Detailed Information**

* **Guest Details & Instructions:** To include specific reservation instructions or other important guest-relevant notes, click the **Others** button within the guest profile area to input this information.
* **Advanced Profile Data:** For guests who are already in-house, you can enrich their profiles by navigating to **Front Desk > In-House Guest Amend**. From this screen, you can fill in:
  * **Personal Details:** Including likes, dislikes, and allergies.
  * **Documentation:** Passport and other official document details, which are often mandatory for foreign guests.
  * **Guest Statistics:** Such as guest type (VIP, repeat, etc.) and billing instructions

### **Managing Profiles**

* **Returning Guests:** If a guest has stayed before, you can use the **magnifier or search icon** to find and select their existing profile instead of creating a new one.
* **Guest Search:** You can view and manage current profiles by going to **Front Desk > Guest Search,** where clicking on a guest's name will open a window with their full details.
