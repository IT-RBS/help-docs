# How to delete the In-House /Reservation Advance?

Overview

The Advance Deletion option allows authorized users to delete an advance associated with an in-house or reservation transaction. The process requires selecting the specific voucher number and providing a reason for the deletion.

## Steps to Delete an Advance :

#### Log in to the System

Ensure you have the required user privileges to perform advance deletions.

Navigate to the Advance Deletion Screen

&#x20;**Path:** _Cashier Main Menu → Cashier → Advance_

&#x20;**To View the List of Advances :** Click on the View button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1371).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select the transaction for RESERVATION / TO ROOM.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1372).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Reservation:** will display the list the Reservation advance for the accounting date.

**To Room:** will display the list of In-House advance made on the accounting date.

Identify the specific advance voucher number you wish to delete.

#### Select and Delete the Advance:&#x20;

Highlight the desired voucher number.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1373).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click the Delete button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1374).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Provide a Cancellation Reason:

A message box will appear prompting you to provide a reason for the deletion.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1375).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Enter the reason for cancellation and click OK to confirm.

**Confirmation:**&#x54;he selected advance will be deleted, and the system will update accordingly.

**Notes:**

**Reservation Advance:** It can be deleted on the same accounting date and before Check-in.

**In-House Advance:** It can be deleted on the same accounting date and before checkout.

**User Privileges:** Ensure your user role allows you to access and perform advance deletions.

**Audit Trail:** Deleted advances may be logged for auditing purposes.

**Cancellation Reason:** Providing a valid and clear reason for deletion is mandatory.
