# How to Re-Instate the NO SHOW reservation ?

To reinstate a No Show reservation, you must navigate to the specific reinstate tool within the Reservation module. Follow these steps:

1. **Access the Menu:** Go to the Reservation Main Menu.
2. **Open Reinstate Tool:** Click on the ellipsis (the three vertical dots) located on the menu bar and select ReInstate NoShow.
3. **Select the Reservation:** A window will appear listing all current no-show reservations. Double-click on the specific reservation row you wish to restore.
4. **Finalize the Reinstatement:**

* Click the **Save** button.
* A pop-up window will appear prompting you to enter a reason for reinstating the booking.
* Enter the reason and click **OK** to complete the process.

Once reinstated, the reservation will no longer appear in the No Show list and will be returned to an active status. You can track these actions later in the No Show/Cancelled Reservation Audit report, which lists all reinstated reservations within a selected period.
