# How do I create a Booker Profile?

To create a **Booker Profile**, you can follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Booker Master:** Go to the **Front Office** module, select **Masters**, and then click on **Booker Profile**. Alternatively, you can initiate this from the **Booker Search Screen**.
2. **Add New Profile:** Click the **New** or **Add** button located at the bottom of the screen.
3. **Enter Booker Details:** Input the necessary information, including the **Booker Name, Title, Company, Telephone, Email, and Mobile**. You may also provide the **Address and City**.
4. **Save the Profile:** Click the **OK** or **Save** button to complete the process.

If you need to **modify a profile**, navigate back to the **Booker Search Screen**, select the specific record, and click the **Amend** button to update the details.
