# What is the difference between a New and Repeat guest?

The primary difference between a New Guest and a Repeat Guest lies in how their information is entered into the system and how the hotel utilizes their historical data for service and reporting.

1\. **Information Entry and Selection**

* **New Guest:** You must manually type the guest's name into the designated columns by clicking the pencil icon or double-clicking the row. A new profile is created by entering all required personal details.
* **Repeat Guest:** Instead of manual entry, you click the magnifier (search) icon to search the existing list by the guest's last name. Once found, you double-click the name to automatically populate the reservation with their existing profile data.

2\. **Operational Significance**

* **Advance Recognition:** Categorizing a guest as a **"Repeat Guest"** under Guest Type serves as a clue for hoteliers to provide specific treatment or personalized service in advance.
* **Analytics:** The system tracks repeat business through the Business Source field, which is compulsory because it indicates opportunities for repeat business and has financial implications.

3\. **Reporting and Analysis**

* **Repeaters Definition:** In the Guest In House report, **"Repeaters"** are specifically identified as guests who have stayed at the hotel at least twice.
* **Detailed Analysis:** There is a dedicated Repeat Guest analysis report that allows you to filter and view specific details like mobile numbers, email, company affiliation, and nationality for these returning individuals.
* **Emerging Markets:** The system also generates reports like "New Guest Arrivals and Guest Nights by Nationality," which helps the hotel identify emerging markets by tracking guests who have never stayed at the property before.
