# How do I print a registration card for walk-ins?

To print a registration card specifically for a walk-in guest, you can complete the task during the check-in process by following these steps:

1\. Navigate to the **Reservation Main Menu.**

2\. Select the **Registration Card Print** option.

3\. Choose the **“Plain Reg. Card”** option, which is specifically intended for walk-in guests.

4\. Click the **“Print”** icon to generate and print the card in the system's designated format.

Additionally, during the general check-in flow, the system typically generates a pop-up window asking if a registration card is to be printed. You can check the box in this pop-up and click **OK** to initiate the print as you complete the check-in process.
