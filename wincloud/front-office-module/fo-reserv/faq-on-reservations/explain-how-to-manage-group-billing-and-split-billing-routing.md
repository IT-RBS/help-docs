# Explain how to manage group billing and split-billing routing?

Managing group billing and split-billing routing involves using the Group Master record for centralised control and the routing tool for automated charge distribution.

**Centralised Group Billing**

To manage billing for an entire group, you must first create a Group Master record. This serves as the primary record that consolidates all individual reservations, allowing for centralised billing and reporting for the entire group.

**Split-Billing Routing**

The Routing feature is used to automatically split bills among guests, which saves the effort of manual splitting. Routing can be applied at four different stages:

* While creating the reservation.
* While checking in.
* After check-in.
* While checking out.

**Steps to Create a Routing:**

* **Initiate:** Click the Routing button after the guest and room creation is complete.
* **Assign Splits:** Use the Assign to field to specify the number of splits required for the reservation.
* **Specify Recipients:** In the Edit Split button, specify the Guest name or Company Name, as well as details for payments, collections, and tips.
* **Select Revenues:** Click on the **Group/Revenue** field to select the specific group and the particular revenues (such as room charges, telephone, or laundry) that need to be split.
* **Save:** Save these preferences to ensure charges are automatically directed to the correct folio.

**Manual Billing Splits at Checkout**

If billing needs to be handled manually during the checkout process, the system offers two additional options:

* **Amount Split:** This allows you to split the amount of any revenue either percentage-wise or by a specific amount (e.g., splitting a single charge equally between two guests).
* **Bill Split:** This option allows you to create up to 9 different bill splits. You can assign specific revenue items to different bill numbers (e.g., Bill 1 for room charges and Bill 2 for incidental charges).

**Additional Billing Tools**

* **Temporary Folios:** These are used to record charges before a guest officially checks in or to accommodate third-party service charges and complex split-billing scenarios.
* **Link/De-link Rooms:** You can link **"child rooms"** to a **"parent room"** so they have a single bill during checkout. In this setup, the system will not allow child rooms to be checked out individually; they must be checked out through the parent room.
