# Reservation Confirmation

1. Go to the **Reservation** main menu and click **Reservation Confirmation.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (92).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Define the required period by selecting the dates from the calendar icons in the _From_ and _To_ fields

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (94).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on the display button, and the reservation that falls within the preview of the dates will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (95).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. The user can also apply additional filters by clicking the funnel icon next to the _Display_ button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (96).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Double-click on the **Reservation** to generate a reservation confirmation.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (99).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (98).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On the top right corner of the confirmation window, the user can either print or send the confirmation as an email using the print and send mail button.
