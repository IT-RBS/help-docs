# Reservation Cancellation

### To Cancel a Reservation, follow the steps:

* Go to Front Office >> Reservation>> Reservation screen
* Go to the sub menu bar >> Click open <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (8).png" alt="" data-size="line">
* Select the reservation to be cancelled.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (890).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Double click to open the reservation.
* Select the cancel button <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (9).png" alt="" data-size="line">. When you hover over the cancel button, it turns red.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (891).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* A cancel detail screen pops up inquiring the reason for cancellation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (892).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Fill in the cancellation reasons and the person who requested cancellation. Click OK.
* The cancellation of the reservation is completed, and the system gives a cancellation number.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (893).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Click the OK button to complete cancellation.
