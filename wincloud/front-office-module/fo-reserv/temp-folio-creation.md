# Temp Folio Creation

A temporary folio is utilized to record charges against a guest or reservation before check-in and to accommodate split billing scenarios or third-party service charges.&#x20;

This functionality can be used to handle temporary billing scenarios where a trusted guest may be allowed limited credit. As Wincloud does not have a built-in option to extend credit to guests, this feature serves as a workaround. (In this scenario, only the front desk is responsible for the credit. The accounts department is never connected to this credit)

To create a Temporary folio in Wincloud:

* Go to Reservations >> Temp folio creation
*   &#x20;Provide a name for the temp folio in the given field.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1145).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Specify the date range during which the folio will be active.&#x20;
* Enter an appropriate narration.
* Select the room type from the drop-down list. Preferably select the type as 'PM connect'.
* Choose the country.
* Select the business source and Market segment from the corresponding drop-down lists
* Select the company and tag the guest's name.
*   To tag the guest's name, double-click on the guest dropdown.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1146).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Save ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (1147).png>)the folio.
* Once saved, the folio number will be automatically generated.
* To **modify** the temp folio, click on the open button ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (1148).png>)on the toolbar.
* Edit the folio and then save.
* To **Delete** the temp folio, click on the view button![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png>)on the toolbar.
* Select the folio to delete and click on the delete button, which appears on the toolbar.
