# Reinstate Checkout

Reinstate Checkout allows the user to undo the checkout.

### **Steps to Reinstate Checkout.**

1. Go to **Front Office>>Front Desk>> Reinstate Checkout.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (953).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Enter the checked-out room number in the 'Room No' field if the user knows the room number. If the room number is not known, search for the checked-out guest to be reinstated from the guest field using the magnifier.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (954).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. Enter the reason to Reinstate the Checkout. Click on the check boxes under Cancel Settlement and Cancel Bill.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (955).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. Save ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (956).png>)the reinstation.

Note:- The room that you are going to reinstate should be vacant.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (957).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
