# What are the different types of Room Blocking

## **What are the different types of Room Blocking?**

There are **three primary types of Room Blocking** used to **restrict room availability for specific purposes:**

### **1. Guest Room Block**

This type is used for the **pre-blocking of a particular room for future arrivals.**

* **Basis:** It is strictly based on **existing reservations.**
* **Functionality:** When you search for and select a reservation, the system automatically selects the **Block Start Date (Arrival Date)** and **Block End Date (Departure Date)** for that guest.

### **2. Maintenance Room Block**

This category is used for **physical room upkeep** and is divided into two distinct statuses depending on the **duration and impact on hotel statistics:**

* **Out of Order (OOO):** Used for **blocks of long duration.** Rooms marked as **Out of Order** are **not considered for ARR (Average Room Rate) calculations** in the room count.
* **Out of Service (OOS):** A **temporary block for a short duration.** Unlike OOO, these rooms are **considered for ARR calculations.**

### **3. Management Room Block**

The system also allows for a **Management Room Block**, though specific details on its **statistical impact** were not provided in the sections describing the other types.

## **Managing and Releasing Blocks**

* **Execution:** Room blocking is performed by navigating to **Front Desk > Room Blocking**, selecting the **block type**, choosing the **room**, and providing a **mandatory narration.**
* **Identification:** In the **Room Search screen**, blocked rooms are visually identified by **color codes** (e.g., **"Blocked for maintenance"**).
* **Release:** To **unblock a room**, you must select the **required block type and number**, then click the **Release Button** in the **Block Release section.**
