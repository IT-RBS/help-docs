# How to change the Guest Room?

To change a guest's room, you can use the dedicated Change Room menu or the interactive Calendar screen.

**Method 1: Via the Change Room Menu**

This is the standard procedure for moving a guest who is already in-house:

1. Navigate to the **Front Desk Main Menu** and click on **Front Desk > Change Room** Menu.
2. Select the specific guest for whom you want to change the room in the Guest Name option.
3. Choose the destination **Room no.** to which the guest would like to move.
4. Enter the reason for the move in the Narration field.
5. Click **Save** to complete the process. A pop-up message will appear confirming the change was successful.

**Method 2: Via the Calendar (Room Chart)**

You can also perform a room change visually through the room chart:

1\. Go to the **Front Office** module and select **Queries > Calendar**.

2\. Locate the guest on the chart and simply drag and drop your cursor to move the guest from one room number to another.

**Monitoring Room Changes**

If you need to review room moves that have occurred over a specific period, you can use the Guest Shift Rooms report. This report can be generated as a Reason Summary to show the direct number of shifts and their associated reasons, or as a detailed report for in-depth information on each shift.
