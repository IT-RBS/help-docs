# What is the difference between OOO and OOS Blocks

**What is the difference between OOO and OOS maintenance blocks?**

&#x20;

In the system, the primary differences between **Out Of Order (OOO)** and **Out Of Service (OOS)** maintenance blocks relate to the duration of the block and their impact on hotel financial statistics:

· **Out Of Order (OOO):** This status is intended for maintenance issues that require a **long duration** to resolve. When a room is marked as OOO, it is **not considered** in the total room count used to calculate the **Average Room Rate (ARR).**

· **Out Of Service (OOS):** This is a **temporary block used** for maintenance tasks of a **short duration**. Unlike OOO rooms, OOS rooms **are considered** in the room count for **ARR** calculations.

Both types of blocks are managed through the **Front Desk > Room Blocking** menu and are visually identified by a specific color code on the **Room Search Screen**. To remove either block, you must select the specific block number and use the **Release Button** under the Block Release option.
