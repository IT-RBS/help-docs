# How do I release a room block in the system?

To release a room block in the system, you must navigate to the same module used to create the block. Follow these specific steps:

1\. **Select the Block Type:** Click on the required ROOM block type (such as Guest Room block, Maintenance Room block, or Management Room block) displayed in the type options.

2\. **Locate the Specific Block:** Select the required block number from the list of currently blocked rooms.

3\. **Execute the Release:** Click on the Release Button that appears in the right corner of the window under the Block Release option.

As a reminder from our previous discussion, rooms blocked for **Maintenance** can be categorized as **Out Of Order (OOO)** for long duration or **Out Of Service (OOS)** for short duration. Rooms marked as Out Of Order are not included in the room count for **Average Room Rate (ARR)** calculations, whereas Out Of Service rooms are included.
