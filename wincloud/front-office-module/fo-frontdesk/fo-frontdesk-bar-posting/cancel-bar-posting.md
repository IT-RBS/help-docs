# Cancel Bar Posting

### **What is Bar Posting?**

&#x20;**Bar Posting** is a feature where the front office team can block any charges or credits being posted to the room of a guest. Once the room is bar-posted during the stay, whatever a guest consumes or whatever services they use, the guest has to pay immediately.&#x20;

### **Steps to Cancel the Bar-Posted Room**

1. Go to **Front Office>>Front Desk >>** **Cancel Bar Posting.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (965).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Once the user has clicked on **Cancel Bar Posting,** the list of guests who have bar-posting applied is displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (966).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (967).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (968).png>) save to complete the process. A confirmation pop-up is displayed, and clicking the OK button will remove the Bar posting on the reservation.
