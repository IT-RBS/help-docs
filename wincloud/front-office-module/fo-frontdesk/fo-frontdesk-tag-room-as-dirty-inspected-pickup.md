# Room Status Tagging

1.Go to **Front office>>** **Front Desk >>** **Room as Room Status Tagging**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (951).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. The screen displays a list of rooms. You can filter the rooms based on their status, such as Occupied, Vacant, or Guest Block.
3. The supervisor can inspect the rooms and mark them as dirty if required by selecting the checkbox under the “Tag Dirty” field.

4\. Once the room is marked or tagged as dirty, choose the priority level, and enter the narration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (186).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. Click save ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (188).png>)to complete the process. A confirmation message pops up to confirm the change of status.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (187).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Once the rooms are tagged as dirty, if the user wants, he can list out all the dirty rooms by clicking the dirty room filter.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (190).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
