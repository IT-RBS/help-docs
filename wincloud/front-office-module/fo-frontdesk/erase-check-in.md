# Erase Check-In

### **Erase Check-in**

This option is used to remove a Checked-In Guest from the record without any trace, provided if no charges are posted to the Guest's folio.

### **Steps to Erase Check-in**

1.  Go to **Front Office>>Front Desk >>Erase Check-in**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (183).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



3. Clicking on the magnifier displays all the checked-in rooms. Select the room to erase from the list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (182).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Select the room and enter the reason in the narration box.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (184).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



5. Click save, and a confirmation pop-up appears.
