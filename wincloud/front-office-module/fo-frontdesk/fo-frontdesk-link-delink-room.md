# Link / De-link Room

**Link / De-link Room**

### Room Linking (Single Bill for Multiple Rooms)

This option allows occupied rooms to be linked together so that a **single consolidated bill** can be generated at checkout.

* One room must be designated as the **Parent Room**
* The other rooms will be treated as **Child Rooms**
* During checkout:
  * Child rooms **cannot be checked out individually**
  * Checking out the **Parent Room** will automatically check out all linked child rooms

### **Steps to Link Room**

1. Go to the **Front Desk >>Link / De-link Room.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (227).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Clicking the Link / Delink Room button opens the link/delink screen.
4. Double-Click on the magnifier in the room number field for the list of occupied rooms.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (908).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the room number you want to designate as the **Parent Room** from the displayed room list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (911).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click on the plus button next to the table to select rooms for linking to the parent room.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (912).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. After selecting the parent room, choose the rooms you want to link to it. These rooms will be treated as the **Child rooms**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (913).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Once the parent and child rooms are linked, the child rooms will be visible in the table under the **Link/De-link Room** section.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (914).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Click the **Save** button to save the changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (917).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Note:- Rooms already linked with some other room will not be allowed to link again.

### **Steps to Delink Room**

1. Go to the **Front Desk**.
2. Click on **Front Desk > Link / Delink Room.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (916).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on the open button to select the rooms to be delinked. Select from the list provided. Here, room number 320 is the linked parent room.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (918).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Double-click on the room number to be de-linked. The screen displays the child rooms along with the parent room.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (921).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click the **Delete** option to remove the room from the linked rooms list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (922).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Click on the save button to save the changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (923).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
