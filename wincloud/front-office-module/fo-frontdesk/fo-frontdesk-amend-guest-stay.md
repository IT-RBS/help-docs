# Amend Stay

1. Go to **Front Office** >>**Front Desk >> Amend stay.**
2. Click on the **Amend Stay Menu**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (925).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the magnifier icon in the Room Number field to search for the room that requires amendment.
4. When a room is selected, the reservation details populate automatically. The original departure date appears highlighted with a pink flag.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (926).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Choose the new departure date using either the calendar or the date field on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (927).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\. Once the new departure date is selected, provide the reason for the amendment in the Reason field located at the bottom of the Amend screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (928).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. Select Save ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (931).png>)to complete the process. A confirmation message indicating that the amendment was successful will be displayed.
