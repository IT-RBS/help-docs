# Change Room

1. Go to **Front Office**>>**Front Desk >> Change room.**
2. Click the magnifier icon in the Room Number field to search for the room to be changed.  &#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (197).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. When a room is selected, the reservation details populate automatically.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (198).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Select the destination Room No. to which the guest will be moved, and enter the reason for the room change in the Narration field, as shown below.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (199).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click the “To Room No.” field to open the Room Search screen and select a new room for the guest.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (201).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6.  The newly assigned room number appears in the ''To room no " field.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (202).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. Click Save ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (203).png>)to complete the process. A pop-up message will appear to confirm the change of rooms.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (932).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
