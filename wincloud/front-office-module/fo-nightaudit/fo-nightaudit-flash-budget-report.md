# Flash Budget Report

### **Flash Budget Report**

This report gives you the comparison of Actual Sales and Budget Defined (in NA Masters) for the selected period with details of Rooms Sold, ARR and ARP included in the same as rows.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (457).png>)

You can also filter to see **YTD** or See **Last Year** Sales & Budget Comparison and also **Average/Year**.
