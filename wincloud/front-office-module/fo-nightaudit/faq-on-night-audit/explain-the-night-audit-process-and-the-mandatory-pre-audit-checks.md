# Explain the night audit process and the mandatory pre-audit checks?

The **Night Audit** is a critical day-end process (typically started after 23:59) that finalizes all transactions, closes the current account date, and opens a new one. Once completed, the system prevents any further modifications or postings to the closed date.

### **Mandatory Pre-Audit Checks**

Before executing the audit, it is mandatory to verify and complete several processes to ensure data integrity:

* **In-House Guest & Registration Verification:** Ensure all expected arrivals have been checked in and assigned registration numbers. You can use the **Guest Information Amendment** window to update guest or tariff details if needed.
* **Tariff and Rate Code Accuracy:** Verify that the correct room tariffs and rate codes are applied to all in-house guests. The **Room Tariff Report** can be used for this verification.
* **Settlement and Check-Out Details:** All settlement bills and checked-out guest details must be cross-referenced with printed bills and system reports. This includes verifying voided bills through the **Void Bill Report**.
* **Revenue Posting:** Ensure all miscellaneous revenues, such as room service and telephone calls, are properly posted to the correct guest ledgers.
* **Clear Pending Bills and KOTs:** All outlet bills must be settled, and any pending **Kitchen Order Tickets (KOTs)** in restaurants or room service must be cleared.
* **Resolve Overdue Departures:** Guests scheduled to leave but still showing as in-house must either be checked out or have their stay extended.
* **Mandatory Tariff Posting:** Room tariffs for all in-house guests must be posted before the audit can begin. This is done via the **Post Tariff All** option, which is available after 10:00 PM.

### **The Night Audit Process**

You can verify your preparedness using the **Night Audit Check List**, which will display any pending jobs (like unposted plans or unpaid bills) that would block the audit. Once the list is empty, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate:** Go to the **Night Audit Main Menu >> Night Audit**.
2. **Create Night Balance:** Click **Create Night Balance** and select **Proceed**; the system will show "Completed" once finished.
3. **Open New Day:** Click **Open New Day**, enter the new accounting date, and re-confirm.
4. **Completion:** Click **Proceed** to finalize the audit and open the new business day.
5. **GL Posting:** After the audit is finished, use the **NA GL Posting** option to transfer the night audit revenue data to the **General Ledger.**
