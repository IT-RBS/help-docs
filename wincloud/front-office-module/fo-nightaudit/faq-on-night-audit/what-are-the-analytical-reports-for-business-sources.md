# What are the analytical reports for Business Sources?

The **Business Source** is a compulsory field during reservation because it tracks the origin of a referral (e.g., OTAs), which is critical for identifying repeat business and financial implications. Several analytical reports utilize this data to provide insights into hotel performance:

* **Contribution Analysis:** This report analyzes **Revenue Contribution and Room Nights Contribution** based on various aspects, including **Business Contribution**, for any selected period.
* **Market Analysis:** Similar to contribution analysis, this report tracks **Market Revenue and Room Revenue** categorized by the origin of the business.
* **Guest Analysis:** This provides a detailed breakdown where users can filter guest data by **Business Source**, allowing for deeper segmentation of the guest profile.
* **Occupancy Statistics:** By using the **Sources filter**, you can add a dedicated column to this report that indicates exactly where each booking originated for in-house or checked-out guests.
* **Occupancy Forecast:** This forward-looking report includes an **Options Filter** to add Business Source columns and a **Funnel Filter** to sort future reservations by a specific booking origin.
* **Manager Report:** While not a dedicated source report, it provides a consolidated overview of rooms and related revenues, including those collected from companies and travel agents, which are often linked to business sources.
