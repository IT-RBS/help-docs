# How do I use the Occupancy Analysis report?

The Occupancy Analysis report is a management tool designed to provide a focused look at room occupancy and sales performance. It allows you to analyse room utilisation across different time frames and supports multi-property viewing, enabling you to select specific properties or a group of properties for analysis.

**Key Metrics Tracked**

The report breaks down data by Room Type and includes the following performance indicators:

* **Rooms (Available & Revenue)**: Displays the number of rooms available for sale and the total revenue they generated.
* **Sold Rooms:** Shows the total number of rooms sold and the Room% (the percentage of total available rooms that were sold).
* **Occupancy Breakdown:** Categorises sold rooms by their specific occupancy type, such as Single, Double, Triple, and Quad.
* **Pax (Person):** Tracks the total number of guests residing in the sold rooms.

**Financial Statistics**

The report automatically calculates critical financial ratios that reflect the division of average sales per room and per person:

* **ARR (Average Room Rate)**: This is the revenue generated divided by the number of sold rooms.
* **ARP (Average Revenue per Pax)**: This is the revenue generated divided by the total number of guests (Pax).
* **RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room)**: This is the average revenue generated for all available rooms, calculated as Revenue Generated divided by Total Available rooms for sale.

**Time frame Analysis**

The report provides collective data for three distinct periods based on the last closed accounting date:

* **For the Day:** Current daily performance.
* **For the Month:** Cumulative performance for the current month.
* **For the Year:** Cumulative performance for the current year.
