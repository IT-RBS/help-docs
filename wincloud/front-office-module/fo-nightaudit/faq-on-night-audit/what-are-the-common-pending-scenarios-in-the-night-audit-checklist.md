# What are the common pending scenarios in the Night Audit Checklist?

The Night Audit Checklist identifies specific pending tasks and "jobs" that must be completed or cleared before the system allows the Night Audit to proceed. The most common pending scenarios listed in the checklist include:

* **Tariff Not Posted:** This indicates that the room tariff for all in-house guests has not yet been posted. This must be done using the "Post Tariff All" option, typically after 10:00 PM.
* **Plan Not Posted:** This scenario appears when plan charges for in-house guests have not been finalised.
* **Overdue Departures:** The system blocks the audit if guests scheduled to depart on the current date are still showing as in-house. These guests must either be checked out or have their stay extended.
* **Checked Out Guest Unpaid Bills:** This occurs when a final bill has been created for a guest, but the settlement has not yet been recorded in the system.
* **Pending KOT (Kitchen Order Tickets):** The audit cannot run if there are pending KOTs in the Restaurant or Room Service outlets. These must be either settled with a bill or cancelled in the Point of Sale (POS) module.
* **Pending Bills:** This refers to bills generated in the POS module that remain unsettled or not cancelled.

Clearing these pending items ensures that all revenue is accounted for and that the system can accurately transition to the new accounting date.
