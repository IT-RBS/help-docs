# Occupancy Analysis

### **Occupancy Analysis**

The Occupancy Analysis Report shows you the information of Rooms focusing on the occupancy and sold rooms. This report also supports Multi-Property features, where users can select the property from a group of properties.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 111409.png>)

**Room Type:** Types of Rooms Available for the day/month/year.

<div align="center"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 111254.png" alt="" width="149"></div>

**Rooms:** _Available_ - Available no. of rooms for sale.

&#x20;_Revenue_ - Revenue generated by the available rooms.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (548).png>)

**Sold:** _Rooms_ - Total Rooms sold out of available rooms.

&#x20;_Room%_ - Percentage of Rooms sold from available rooms.

With Occupancy of Single, Double, Triple, and Quad.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (454).png>)

**Pax (Person):** No. of Pax in sold rooms (People in the room).

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (528).png>)

**ARR**: Average Room Revenue of the sold rooms (Revenue Generated / Sold Rooms= ARR).

**ARP** : Average Revenue generated per Pax (Revenue Generated / PAX= ARP).

**RevPAR**: Revenue per available room, Avg. Revenue Generated for all available rooms (Revenue Generated / Total Available rooms for sale= RevPAR).

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (368).png>)

This report will show you the collective data of Rooms Available and Sold for the day, for the month, and for the year as of now (Closed date).
