# Transaction Journal

### **TRANSACTION JOURNAL**

Transaction journal shows the complete transactions which happened for the day. These are the revenue-wise statistics. This report even shows the bill numbers of the transaction. Filters are available to view revenue-wise transactions

1.To view the report, go to “FRONT OFFICE” module “NIGHT AUDIT” “TRANSACTION JOURNAL”

2.Select the date required and click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/0 (69).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-27 111649.png>)

3.To filter revenue group-wise, disable ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (241).png>) and click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/3 (99).png>) the icon

4.To view only specific revenue group data, select the required revenue from the pop-up and click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/4 (96).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (323).png>)
