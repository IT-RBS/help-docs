# Night Audit Report

### **Night Audit Report**

The Night Audit Report shows you the revenues generated in the Revenue Centres (Outlets/Facility/Rooms) with Collections and City Ledger settlements of the day. This happens only when you close the accounting date for a previous day.

**Revenue:**

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/0 (140).png>)

**Collection:**

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (42).png>)

The Night Audit Report will feature the Revenue names and options as follows : today’s (closed day), Month till date and Year till date Sales as default.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 104631.png>)

In “Particulars” column the Revenue Main Groups are **Bold** and below this  are Sub-Group which is Revenue Heads are given.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (781).png>)

In All three columns of Today’s, Month till date (MTD), and Year till date (YTD) Sales and Collections, there are three sub-division of columns.

**Amount**: Which shows the total revenue/ collection generated.

**Allowance**: Allowance (if any) against the Revenues.

**Net**: Net Amount after Allowance (Amount- Allowance= Net).

By Default this report will generate the data of the last closed day (as today’s column). If you want to see a cumulative amount of a **specific period** you will have the filter to generate a “**From and to” report.** This gives a collective amount report against the revenues generated within that period

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 105102.png>)

The user, by selecting **Datewise** will get the report for each date from the selected date range as shown below.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 105339.png>)

“Net Revenue” check box provides an additional row of Net Revenue generated.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/6 (47).png>)

**Last Year Comparison:** A filter is provided to see the comparison of Revenues and Collections generated for Today’s date, MTD and YTD with Today’s date Last Year, MTD LY and YTD LY which gives an insight on the business to the management.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 105530.png>)

The user can also view the Night Audit Report for **Multi-Property** by selecting the property from top left hand side, as shown below. Also by selecting **All Properties,** the system provides a consolidated reports inclusive of all properties.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 110916.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Excess/Shortage:** It means any kind of discrepancy in Revenues and Collections not matching, it shows in the very last row of the NA report which can show because of usually one reason

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (556).png>)

Revenues not linked properly in the NA Master. This issue can be easily resolved and doesn’t change any data it just displays the missing data and removes the excess/shortage amount.

**Note:** Excess/Shortage row must always show ‘0’ amount.
