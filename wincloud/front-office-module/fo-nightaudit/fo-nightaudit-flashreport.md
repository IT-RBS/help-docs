# Flash Report

### **Flash Report**

The Flash Report gives you the group-wise information revenue, based on NA Report and its master linking. It also shows you the percentage of contribution of revenue/s w.r.t to Total Revenue. By default it will show as below :

<div align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 123402.png" alt=""></div>

Columns are divided as Particulars (Revenue Group and Sub-Group or Revenue heads) and Amount (Today, MTD and YTD) and also the contribution percentage of the total.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-07-21 123507.png>)

There are also many Filters provided for briefly modifying and changing the report as per user requirements.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (646).png>)

**Statistics:** If you click on this checkbox, it adds on a section of Rooms Statistics and related info.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (542).png>)

**Collections:** If you click on this checkbox, it adds on a section of Collection of amount and city Ledgers.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (447).png>)

**Comparison with Previous Year:** It adds columns to compare the current report with the previous year’s revenue data at the same time, but if you keep Statistics and Collections box checked it will **not** show you that. So, only for comparison of Revenues and not for Room Statistics and Collections.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (617).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (619).png>)

**Month wise:** Month wise filter adds on 2 more filters to see the data Month wise in a Summarised format (Total Revenue of the month)&#x20;

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (580).png>)

And also in Avg. per day (for that month) format.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (312).png>)
