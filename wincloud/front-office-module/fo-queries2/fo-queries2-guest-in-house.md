# Guest In House

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Advanced Queries**.
3. Select **Guest In House**.

**Guest In House**

This report as the name suggests shows the information of the Guest Staying with the hotel at present. By default it will show the basic information of all the guests.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 120751.png>)

There are multiple filters available to add more information. Columns and sort to rearrange the sequence based on your requirements.

**Repeaters:** This will show the Current In House Guests who have been coming frequently or at least twice and so on.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 122337.png>)

**Scanty Baggage:** This will show you the in house guests who have light baggage with them, only if you have marked their profile as scanty baggage.

**Duration of Stay:** This will generate a report to see (based on no. of days entered) the information of those guests who are going to stay for a minimum of “X” days.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (65).png>)

**Guest Profile:** This will give a report with the personal info. Of in house guests such as their likes, dislikes and allergies or any hotel comments provided they are filled in the guest profile.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/4 (38).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/5 (65).png>)

**News Paper:** This will give you a report of the guests who are eligible for newspapers and which newspapers to be exact.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/6 (43).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/7 (69).png>)

**Bar Posting:** To see the list of guests for whom posting of specific Charges are Barred or prohibited or banned into their rooms. They have to pay for those charges right away.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/8 (55).png>)

**Arrival Today and Departure Today:** To see the list of In House Guests who arrived today and who are going away today.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/9 (32).png>)

**Past Days:** Information of the Guests who stayed with you in the Past within a provided (Date selection) period with detailed information.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/10 (2).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/11 (9).png>)

Other options are also available as filters to add on the info. Columns to sort the report based on requirements.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/12 (3).png>)

**Funnel Filter:** To Search Specific Segments, Group, Agent and Company etc. also for sorting.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 121041.png" alt="" width="375">

**Options Filter:** To add more information. Columns which were filled in detail for all the guests.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 121140.png" alt="" width="174">

**Sort On:** For Sorting or arranging the sequence based on requirement.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/15 (8).png>)
