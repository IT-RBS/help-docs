# History and Forecast

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Advanced Queries**.
3. Select **History and Forecast**.

**History and Forecast**

This report provides you an insight on all Rooms sold and collected revenues with information. And possible Sales in the future based on Bookings within the selected period.
