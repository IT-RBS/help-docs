# Can I view a month-wise summary report?

Yes, you can view month-wise summaries and monthly reports across several different modules in the system. Many analytical and operational reports provide specific filters or default views for monthly data.Key Month-Wise Reports

* **Business on Books:** This report defaults to a month-wise date format, displaying months in columns and specific dates in rows to show both collected and expected revenue.
* **Flash Report:** You can apply a Month-wise filter to this report to see total revenue for the month in a summarized format or as an average per day for that month.
* **Monthly Night Audit Report:** This version of the Night Audit report allows you to view revenues, collections, and city ledger settlements on a monthly basis, including comparisons with the previous year.
* **Monthly Manager Report:** This provides insights into room statistics, revenue, and occupancy specifically from selected months.
* **Room-wise Income Analysis:** This report generates data on room nights, revenue, and ADR (Average Daily Rate) for a selected month range.

**Month-Till-Date (MTD) Tracking**&#x20;

Several reports include a Month-Till-Date (MTD) column as part of their default display to help you track current monthly progress:

* **Night Audit Report:** Features Today’s, MTD, and YTD (Year-Till-Date) sales and collections as a default view.
* **Occupancy Analysis:** Shows collective data for available and sold rooms for the day, the month, and the year.
* **Manager Report:** Displays statistics, revenue, and occupancy data across four primary columns: Statistics, Day, MTD, and YTD.
* **Segment Analysis:** Provides specific details for nights, revenue, and pax (pax details) for the day, month, and year.

**Forecast and Analysis Summaries**

For future planning and historical analysis, you can toggle the view of several reports:

* **Contribution and Forecast Reports:** Users can choose to view contribution and forecast data for different segments in a Daywise, Quarterlywise, or Monthwise format.
* **Night Audit Monthly Budget:** This master tool allows you to view and distribute the monthly budget for specific sub-groups.
* **Guest Birthday Report:** This allows you to view a complete list of birthdays for a selected month
