# What is the use of early/extended departure ?

The Early/Extended Departure feature is primarily used as a report to track and view all instances where guests have either left the hotel before their scheduled departure date or have lengthened their stay beyond their original booking. This report allows staff to monitor these changes across a selected date range.From an operational and system-integrity standpoint, managing extended stays is critical for the following reasons:

* **Night Audit Requirement:** The system will not allow the night audit process to execute if there are any guests with an overdue departure. To proceed with the audit, staff must either complete the checkout for those guests or officially extend their stay in the system.
* **Stay Amendments:** To officially record an extended stay, users navigate to the Amend Stay menu, select the specific room, enter the new departure date, and provide a reason for the amendment.
* **Operational Awareness:** Monitoring early and extended departures helps the hotel maintain accurate occupancy and availability records, ensuring that rooms are correctly accounted for in future reservation blocks and daily housekeeping schedules
