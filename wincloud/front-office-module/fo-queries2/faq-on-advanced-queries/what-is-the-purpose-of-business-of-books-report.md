# What is the purpose of "Business of Books" report ?

**Purpose and Overview**

The Business on Books report shows the revenue amount collected (when "Past Days" is selected) and the business expected to be generated from future reservations. It provides a real-time view from the current moment up to a selected month in the future.By default, the report uses a month-wise date format, displaying months in columns and dates in rows.

**Revenue Considerations**

To calculate the revenue for a given month, the report takes three primary factors into consideration:

* Total Nights.
* Revenue Generated (or expected to be generated) from bookings.
* ADR (Average Daily Rate).

**Report Filters and Customization**

The report includes multiple filters to refine the data based on operational needs:

* **Room Type Wise:** Displays the report based on specific room types, allowing for single, multiple, or all types to be selected.
* **With Compl/HU:** Includes rooms for complimentary bookings, house use, or management bookings in the totals.
* **With Day Use:** Includes bookings for day-use guests.
* **Exclude Tentative:** Removes all tentative bookings to show only confirmed business.
* **Occ%:** Adds a column for each selected month to show the percentage of occupancy on that day.
* **RevPAR:** Adds a column showing the average revenue generated (and to be generated) across all available rooms within the selected period.
* **Funnel Filter:** Allows users to sort the report by specific market segments or date-wise criteria
