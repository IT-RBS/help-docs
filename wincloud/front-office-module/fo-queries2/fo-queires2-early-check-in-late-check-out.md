# Early Check-In Late Check Out

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click **Advanced Queries**.
3. Select **Early Check-In Late Check Out**.

**Early Check In / Late Check Out**

This report is to see the Guests who checked in earlier in the day or Guests who checked out late as per their check out timing, Time Period can be defined in the report manually to see the Early Check In or Late Check Out.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 150242.png>)

You can select to see Early Check In or Late Check Out.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (166).png>)

Enter the **Time Period** and **Date Range** you require to see and then click on **Display** Button to see the data.
