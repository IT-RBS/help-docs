# APPLICABLE REVENUE CHARGES

APPLICABLE REVENUE CHARGES

The Applicable revenue charges allow the user to set the rates for the chargeable items as per the charge type created. Different rates are accepted as per each charge type. The rates entered here must be exclusive of the taxes.

1.To update the rates of the items, go to “**FRONT OFFICE**” module MASTERS CHARGE ITEM APPLICABLE REVENUE CHARGES OPEN

2\. Select the revenue which must be already linked with the corresponding outlet in the “OUTLET REVENUE” option

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (239).png>)

3\. Then select the charge type from the drop down nearby the title ‘NAME’

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (146).png>)

4\. Once the charge type is selected all the created charge items will be displayed, enter only the basic rates of the items which is related to the revenue selected. For e.g. refer screenshot

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/2 (42).png>)

5\. Click ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/3 (167).png>) once the rates are updated
