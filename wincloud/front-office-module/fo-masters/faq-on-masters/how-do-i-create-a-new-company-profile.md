# How do I create a new Company Profile?

To create a new Company Profile, also known as a **Company Master**, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Company Master:** Go to the **Front Office** module, select **Masters**, then **Company Profile**, and click **New**.
2. **Fill in Address Details:** Under the **Address tab**, enter the company's location and contact details.
3. **Enter Account and GST Information:** Switch to the **Accounts tab** to fill in the **GST details** for the company.
4. **Select Company Type:** It is critical to select the **correct company type** so that settlements reflect accurately under that company.
5. **Enable Approval Status:** By default, a new company is marked as "unapproved." You must manually **change the status to "approved,"** or the system will not allow settlements to be taken for that company, even if it appears in the list.
6. **Save:** Click the save button to finalize the profile.

If you need to **deactivate** a company later, you can do so by opening the company record, selecting the company name, and disabling the active tick-box.
