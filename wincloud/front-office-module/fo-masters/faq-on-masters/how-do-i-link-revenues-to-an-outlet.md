# How do I link revenues to an outlet?

To link revenues to a specific outlet, you must use the **Outlet Revenue** master tool. This allows you to group one or more revenues under a single outlet for more efficient charging.

**Steps to Link Revenues to an Outlet**

1. **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to the **FRONT OFFICE** module, select **MASTERS**, and then click on **OUTLET REVENUE**.
2. **Access the Master:** Click the **OPEN** button.
3. **Select the Outlet:** Choose the desired outlet from the **Outlet ID** drop-down menu.
4. **Add the Revenue:**
   1. Click the **search icon** under the revenue heading to find and select the corresponding revenue.
   2. To link **additional revenues** to the same outlet, click the **plus icon (+)** to add a new row and repeat the search process.
5. **Configure Sales Type:** If you want the revenue to be available for **miscellaneous sales** (bills for non-staying guests), ensure you enable the corresponding checkbox for that row.
6. **Remove a Link:** If you need to remove a revenue from the outlet, click the **delete icon** next to the specific revenue row.
7. **Finalize:** Click the **edit/save icons** to confirm and save your changes.

Once linked, these revenues will automatically appear under the selected outlet name when you are posting **Guest Charges** or performing **Miscellaneous Sales**.
