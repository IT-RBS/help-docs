# How do I define a Paymaster room type?

To define a **Paymaster room type**, you must configure it within the Room Type master under the Front Office module.

**Steps to Define a Paymaster Room Type**

1. **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to the **FRONT OFFICE** module, select **MASTERS**, then **ROOMS**, and click on **ROOM TYPE**.
2. **Create New Record:** Click on the **NEW** button to begin defining the category.
3. **Enter Category Details:** Provide a **room type ID and name** for the category.
4. **Enable Paymaster Type:** Locate the **"Paymaster type" checkbox** and **enable it**. This identifies the room type as being for internal, temporary folio, or clearing purposes rather than for standard guest accommodation.
5. **Finalize:** Click the **Save** icon to update the system with the new room type.

**Context and Use Cases**

* **Internal Accounting:** Paymaster (PM) rooms are often used to manage **temporary folios**, which record charges against a reservation before check-in or accommodate split billing and third-party service charges.
* **Monitoring:** You can specifically filter for Paymaster rooms in various reports, such as the **High Balance Report**, to monitor outstanding balances in these internal accounts.
* **In-House Tracking:** In the **Guest Folio query**, there is a dedicated **PM button** that allows users to view only the ledger details for Paymaster rooms.
