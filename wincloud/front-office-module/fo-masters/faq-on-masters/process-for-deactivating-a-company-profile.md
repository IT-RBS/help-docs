# Process for deactivating a company profile

**What is the process for deactivating a company profile?**

To deactivate a **Company Profile**, follow these steps:

#### **Steps to Deactivate a Company Profile**

1. **Navigate to the Company Master:**\
   Go to **Front Office → Masters → Company Profile**.
2. **Select the Company:**\
   Click the **search** icon next to **Company Name** and select the company you want to deactivate.
3. **Disable Active Status:**\
   Clear the **Active** checkbox to deactivate the company profile.
4. **Save the Changes:**\
   Click **Save** to apply the changes.

#### **Important Considerations**

* **Approval Status:**\
  Deactivating a company makes it unavailable for active use. In addition, a company must be marked as **Approved** to be available for settlements. By default, newly created company profiles are **Unapproved** and cannot be used for settlements until they are approved.
* **Company Type:**\
  Ensure the correct **Company Type** (such as **Corporate**, **Travel Agent**, or **Credit Card**) is selected, as it affects billing, settlements, and reporting.
* **Sales Executive Linking:**\
  Company profiles can be linked to **Sales Executives** to help track corporate accounts, performance, and revenue contributions.
