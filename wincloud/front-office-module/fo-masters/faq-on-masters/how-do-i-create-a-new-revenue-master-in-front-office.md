# How do I create a new revenue master in Front Office?

To create a new Revenue Master in the Front Office module, which is used to store and track the total value of sales collected through the software, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to the **FRONT OFFICE** module, select **MASTERS,** and then click on **REVENUE**.
2. **Enter Revenue Details:** Create the revenue entry as required for your property's needs.
3. **Assign Tax Structure:** You can assign a specific tax structure to each revenue item. This ensures that taxes are automatically calculated whenever a corresponding transaction takes place.
4. **Link Outlets:** You have the option to link specific outlets to the revenue item within this master.
5. **Finalise:** Click the **Save** icon to store the new revenue.

**Key Features of the Revenue Master**

* **Universal Applicability:** Once a revenue is created in the Front Office masters, it is applicable for use across all modules of the software.
* **Modifying Existing Entries:** If you need to change details for a revenue that has already been created, click the **OPEN** button, select the revenue you wish to amend, make your changes, and then click the **Save** icon.
