# How to modify the PLAN amount in master ?

To modify the PLAN amount (referred to as Plan Charges in the system) within the master settings, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the menu: **FRONT OFFICE > MASTERS > RATE > PLAN CHARGE**.
2. Click the Open button to view the list of defined plan charges.
3. Click on the Update Amended Plan Rate option to modify the rates.
4. _(Alternatively, to configure a new plan charge rate)_ Click on the New button and select the appropriate **Plan ID, Currency, Plan name, and Tax structure**.
5. Click the **Save** button to apply and save your changes successfully
