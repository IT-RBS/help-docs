# How do I view the Occupancy Forecast report?

The **Occupancy Forecast** report is a forward-looking tool found within the **Analysis** section of the **Front Office** module. It is designed to provide visibility into future occupancy levels and anticipated revenue based on existing reservations, including those marked as tentative.

**How to Use and Customise the Report**

**Select a Date Range:** When viewing the report, you can select a specific period of future dates to analyse upcoming business.

**Include Specific Stay Types:** You can adjust the report to include Complimentary bookings, House Use, and Management bookings by selecting the **"With Compl/HU"** checkbox. Similarly, you can enable **"With Day Use"** to factor in day-use guest forecasts.

**Add Detailed Columns:** The **Options Filter** allows you to add specific columns to the report in a predefined sequence, such as Business Source, Segment, and Room Type, providing a more granular view of all future reservations.

**Filter and Sort Data:** The **Funnel Filter** is used to refine the results based on specific requirements, such as focusing on a single booking source or a specific Company or Travel Agent.

This report is essential for identifying potential high-demand periods and tracking the revenue to be generated from future bookings.
