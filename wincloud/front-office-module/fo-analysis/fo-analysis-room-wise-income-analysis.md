# Room wise Analysis

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Analysis** menu.
3. Select **Room wise Analysis.**

**Room wise Income Analysis**

Room wise Income Analysis gives you the report of sales specifically on how much income (sale) you have got from rooms (**Room No. wise** or **Room Type wise**). It shows you the Room wise **Room Nights**, **Revenues** and **ARR** etc. for a selected Month range.
