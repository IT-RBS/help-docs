# Complaints/Requests

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Analysis** menu.
3. Select **Complaints/Requests**.

**Guest Complaints/Suggestions Audit**

This report gives you list of Guest Complaints, Suggestions and Requests  On the basis of the below filters you can see the report for all types of Guests either In House or Checked out for the selected time period.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/0 (101).png>)

And it has on option to see the person (Staff) who entered or created the Requests/Complaints in the system on behalf of the guests.
