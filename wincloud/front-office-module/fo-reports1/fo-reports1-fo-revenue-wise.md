# Entries Revenue-Wise

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Entries Revenue Wise**.

**Entries Posted at FO- Revenue wise and User wise**

This report shows you each and every posting in the the Front office module (Rooms and Miscellaneous) while focusing on the Revenues and Users of these postings. It will also give you multiple options to sort and filter based on your requirement. Choose from the left hand side on Revenue Wise or User Wise.
