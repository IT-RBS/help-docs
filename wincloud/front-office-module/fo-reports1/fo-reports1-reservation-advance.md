# Reservation Advances

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Reservation Advances**.

**Reservation Advance**

The Reservation advance report shows you the advance given by guest/s against their future bookings with the hotel within the selected period.

By default it will show the Total Tariff (probable) and Advance with the mode of payment both in the same report for understanding the amount received.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 122217.png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 122249.png>)

Reservation Advance Refund

The Reservation advance refund report shows you any transaction made as a refund against the collected advance for booking. From the following, the user can choose from advance and refund

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 122757.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



Different formats of reports are also available based on the selection of checkboxes for the data.

**Materialization:** It shows the amount received and/ or Materialized (billed against booking or final bill) with details of the advance and the type of settlement.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 122400.png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/3 (200).png>)

**Pending Advance:** This shows the amount received through the Advance but has not been used or materialized or billed just yet. This shows all the pending advances not specific to a period.
