# Reservation-Advance refund

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Reservation Advances Refund.**

**Reservation Advance Refund**

The Reservation advance refund report shows you any transaction made as a refund against the collected advance for booking.
