# Guest Settlement

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Guest Settlement**.

**Guest Settlement**

This report helps you to see the total charges and settlement done on the day with checkout and with the Bill no. if the Bill has been generated.
