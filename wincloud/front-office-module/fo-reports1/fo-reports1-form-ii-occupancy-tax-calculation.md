# Form II Occupancy, Tax Calculation

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Form II Occupancy, Tax Calculation.**

**Form II Occupancy & Tax Calculation**

This report shows you the information of the guest with the rack rate, rate charged and the cumulative amount to be charged at the end of the stay with the total Tax to be collected.

You can sort the report based on **Room No. and Guest Name** and / or with **departure date**.
