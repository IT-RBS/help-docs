# Company Bill Printing

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Company Bill Printing.**

**Company Bill Printing**

This option helps you see the Bill Printed in the name of any Company or separates the reprint option for you to print the Company Bills generated within selected period.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-04 094952.png>)

You can Filter **Party Type** wise, **Booker** wise and also by the already printed Bills. This report will show all the bills generated for a single party name and you can print them all together or see the total for the selected Party.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-04 095331.png>)
