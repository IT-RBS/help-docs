# C Form(Foreigner Stay Report)

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **C Form(Foreigner Stay Report).**

**C Form (Foreigners Stay Report)**

As the name suggests, it shows the list of foreign individuals and groups staying with you along with their details such as Visa and Passport details of all the FFIT (Foreign-Free Individual Traveller).

Multiple columns can also be added to see further details such as their **Company Name, Booker and Address** etc. and you can filter to see only P.M Room.

**Arrival Only** gives you the list of people who have arrived within the selected date period.

As the name itself clarifies, **Filter Checked out Guest**, filters out all the Checked out Guest and shows you in house guests.
