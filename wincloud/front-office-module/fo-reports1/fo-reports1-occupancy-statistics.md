# Occupancy Statistics

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Occupancy Statistics**.

**Occupancy Statistics**

The Occupancy Statistic Report shows you the information of Current guest, Checked-Out Guest, Day Use Guest, Complimentary Guest as on the last closed date with the Room No., Registration No., Name, Company Name, their Arrival Date & Time , Departure Date & time, Pax in the Room, their plan and Tariff with comparison to Rack Rate and Extra Bed Charges(if any) as columns as default data on the screen.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 143751.png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 143859.png>)

We have multiple filters available to use in the report.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 143939.png>)

**Retention Charge Room:** If you click on this checkbox It will add a column to see if any of the guests have been charged any retention against the room.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/3 (135).png>)

**Walk In:** It will show you the guests which directly walked-In instead of pre-reserved rooms.

**Segment:** If you click on this, it will add a new column called Market Segment which indicates where the booking came from.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 144844.png>)

**Sources:** If you click on this, it will add a new column of Business Source which indicates where the booking came from.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 145849.png>)

**Room Type:** If you click on this, it will add a column of type of room (it will show ID of that room type) after the room no. column.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 150009.png>)

**Difference %:** If you click on this, it will add a column for the analysis. It shows the percentage in diff. of amount charged for the room (Tariff Charged) compared to the Rack rate.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-03 150055.png>)
