# Guest Ledger

**Navigation Path:**

1. Navigate to the **Front Office** module.
2. Click the **Reports Menu**.
3. Select **Guest Ledger**.

**Guest Ledger**

The Guest Ledger report shows, by default, the current day’s Ledger of all the guests processed today (includes in house and checked out guests both).

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 171535.png>)

You will have options to check both **Current Guest and Checked out Guest** separately and/ or together in the report.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (11).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2023-08-01 171646.png>)

If you want to see the **Arrival and Departure Date** of the Guests there is a provided checkbox to add that to the report as well. Choose it from the options as shown below
