# Past Advances Adjusted

**Past Advances Adjusted** refers to the process of utilising advance payments received from customers in previous periods against current or subsequent invoices. This ensures that previously received advances are correctly applied, outstanding balances are reduced accurately, and customer accounts are properly maintained.

## Steps to View Past Advances Adjusted

Step 1: Go to the main menu of the application and click on **Settings**.

1. From the Settings menu, select **GST India**.
2. Click on **Past Advances Adjusted** to open the screen.

Step 2: Select the required **From Date** and **To Date** to define the reporting period.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/a17.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will display the list of **Past Advances Adjusted** for the selected date range.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/a17.1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Export and Print Options.

1. Click on the **Excel** button to download the report in Excel format.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-21 15-55-05.png>)
2. Click on the **Print** button to generate the report in PDF format or open it in print view for printing.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-21 15-54-24.png>)
