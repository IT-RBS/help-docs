# GST Excel Export

**GST Excel Export** is a feature that allows users to generate and download GST-related data in Excel format from the system. This functionality helps in easy reporting, analysis, reconciliation, and submission of GST information to statutory authorities.

### Steps to Export GST Data to Excel

Step 1: Go to the main menu of the application and click on **Settings**.

1. From the Settings menu, select **GST India**.
2. Click on GST Excel Export to open the screen.

Step 2: Select the required **From Date** and **To Date** to define the reporting period.

Step 3: Choose the appropriate **Unit** from the available options.

1. Ensure the correct unit is selected before generating the report.

Step 4: Click on the **Excel Export** button.

* The system will generate the GST report in Excel format.
* The Excel file will be automatically downloaded to your system.
