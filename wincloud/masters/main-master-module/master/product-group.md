# Product Group

The **Product Group Master** is used to define and manage a collection of related products that share similar characteristics. It helps in organising products efficiently and supports better **inventory management, reporting, pricing, and business analysis** within the system.

## Steps to Create Product Group

Step 1: Go to the **Settings** section&#x20;

1. Open the **Master** module. From there, select **Product Group Master**.

Step 2: Click on the **Add** button to create a new product group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s4 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: A form will appear after clicking the Add button. Enter the required details based on user requirements, such as:

* **Product Group Id**
* **Product Group Name**

Ensure the product group is defined clearly to represent related products accurately.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s4.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required details, click the **Save** button to create the product group in the system.
