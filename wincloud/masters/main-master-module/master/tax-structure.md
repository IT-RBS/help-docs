# Tax Structure

The **Tax Structure** defines the framework for applying multiple taxes to transactions in a systematic and compliant manner. It specifies how individual taxes from the Tax Master are combined, calculated, and applied to sales, purchases, or other financial transactions.

## Steps to Create a Tax Structure

Step 1: Log in to the application.

1. Go to the **Settings** section from the main menu.
2. Under **Masters**, click on **Tax Structure**.

* The Tax Structure screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s14 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Add** button available on the screen.

* A new Tax Structure entry form will open.

Step 3: Enter the required details in the respective fields.

1. Create the tax structure as per user requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s14.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Once the Tax Structure is created, click on the **Save** button.

* The system will save the Tax Structure successfully.
