# Supplier Master

The **Supplier Master** is a centralised repository that stores all important information related to vendors or suppliers from whom goods or services are procured. It helps maintain accurate and standardised supplier records, ensuring smooth **procurement, accounting, payment processing, and reporting** within the system.

## Steps to Create a New Supplier

Step 1: Go to the **Settings** section&#x20;

1. Open the **Master** module. From there, select **Supplier Master**.

Step 2: Click on the **Add** button to create a new supplier record.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s8 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: A supplier creation form will appear after clicking the Add button. Enter all required supplier information based on user needs.

* Ensure all entered details are accurate and complete for proper supplier management and transaction processing.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s8.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required details, click the **Save** button to create the supplier record in the system.
