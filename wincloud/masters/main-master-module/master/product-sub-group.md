# Product Sub Group

The **Product Sub Group Master** is used to define sub-classifications within a **Product Group**. It helps in organising products in a more detailed and structured manner. This enables better **product management, reporting, pricing control, inventory tracking, and accounting accuracy** within the system.

### Steps to Create Product Sub Group

Step 1: Go to the **Settings** section &#x20;

1. Open the **Master** module. From there, select **Product Sub Group Master**.

Step 2: Click on the **Add** button to create a new product sub group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s5 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: A form will appear after clicking the Add button. Enter the required details based on user needs, such as:

* **Product Sub Group Name**&#x20;
* **Product Group**&#x20;
* **Product Sub Group Id**
* **Default Account Name**

Ensure that the correct Product Group is selected to maintain proper hierarchy and classification.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/s5.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required details and selecting the appropriate Product Group, click the **Save** button to create the product sub group in the system.
