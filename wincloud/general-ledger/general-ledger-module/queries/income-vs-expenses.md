# Income vs Expenses

The **Income vs Expenses** report is a comparative financial statement that displays total income and total expenses for a selected period. This report helps users evaluate financial performance, monitor cost control, and identify net profit or loss (surplus or deficit) over time.

## Steps to Generate the Income vs Expenses Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Queries** from the menu.
2. Select **Income Vs Expenses** option.

* The Income vs Expenses screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 14-07-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the **Division** from the available drop-down list.

1. Choose the **From Date** and **To Date**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 14-08-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required filters, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate the Income vs Expenses report based on the selected criteria.
