# Transfer Ledger to Another Group

**Transfer Ledger to Another Group** option is used to transfer a ledger, along with all its transactions, from one accounting group to another within the same type of account. For example, a ledger can be transferred from **Assets Group A to Assets Group B** or from **Income Group A to Income Group B**. It cannot be used to transfer ledgers between different account types, such as from **Assets to Income**.

## Steps to Transfer a Ledger

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Settings** from the menu.
2. Select **Transfer Ledger to Another Group**.

* The Transfer Ledger to Another Group screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-32-37.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the left side, select the **Source Group**.

1. From the list, select the required **Ledger** to be transferred.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-29-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: On the right side of the screen, select the **Destination Group**.

* Ensure that the destination group belongs to the same account type as the source group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-30-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter the **Reason** for transferring the ledger.

* The reason helps maintain audit trail and proper documentation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-30-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review the selected source ledger and destination group.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-31-30.png>)

* The system will transfer the ledger to the selected group along with all associated transactions.
