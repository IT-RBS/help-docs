# Cash Flow

The **Cash Flow Report** provides a summary of cash movement within the organization for a selected **financial (fiscal) year**. It helps users track how cash is received and spent over a period of time and determine the closing cash position.

## Steps to View the Cash Flow Report

Step 1: Go to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Analysis** from the menu.
2. Select **Cash Flow**.

* The **Cash Flow Report** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-35-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click **Display**. The report will be displayed, showing the **opening balance**, **inflow**, and **outflow** for the selected period, along with the **closing balance** as of the last selected date.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-37-30.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After generating the report :&#x20;

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
