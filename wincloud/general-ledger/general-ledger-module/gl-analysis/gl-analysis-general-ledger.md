# General Ledger

The **General Ledger Report** provides a detailed view of all transactions recorded for a selected ledger. It displays complete transaction history, including debit and credit entries, within a chosen period.

## Steps to Access the General Ledger Report

Step 1: Go to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Analysis** from the menu.
2. Select **General Ledger**.

* The **General Ledger Report** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-46-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Search** icon.

1. Choose the required date range or reporting period from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-46-47.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-55-53.png>)

1. Select the required filter criteria from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-47-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Three-Dot option** ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-50-43.png>)available on the screen.

1. Select the available options as per your requirement.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-48-48.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Display** button.

* The **General Ledger Report** will be generated and displayed on the screen.
