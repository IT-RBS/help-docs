# Profit & Loss

The **Profit & Loss Report** provides a summary of the organisation's financial performance during a selected period. The report is generated based on the balances available under the **Income** and **Expense** ledger groups and displays the resulting profit or loss for the selected period.

## Steps to Access the Profit & Loss Report

Step 1: Go to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Analysis** from the menu.
2. Select **Profit & Loss**.

* The **Profit & Loss Report** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-05-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2:Click the **Search** icon.

1. Select the required reporting period from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-06-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the preferred **Report Style**.

* The report layout will be updated according to the selected format.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-07-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-55-53.png>)

1. Select the required filtering criteria from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-07-37.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Once the required **reporting period**, **report style**, and **filter options** are selected, the **Profit & Loss Report** will be generated and displayed according to the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 10-11-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
