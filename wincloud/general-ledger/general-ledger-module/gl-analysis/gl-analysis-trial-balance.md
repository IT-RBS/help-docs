# Trial Balance

The **Trial Balance Report** provides a summary of all ledger balances within the selected accounting period. It displays ledger balances group-wise and helps users verify the accuracy of accounting records by showing opening balances, transaction amounts, and closing balances.

## Steps to Access the Trial Balance Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Analysis** from the menu.
2. Select the **Trial Balance** option.

* The Trial Balance screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-53-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Use the available **check boxes** and **Advanced Filter** ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-55-53.png>)options to customise the report view according to your requirements

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-55-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the **From Date** and **To Date**.

1. Click **Display** button.

* The **Trial Balance Report** will be generated and displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 09-57-05.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58 (2).png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08 (2).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
