# Bills Aging Summary-Monthwise

The **Bills Aging Summary – Monthwise** report provides a consolidated month-wise summary of outstanding bills based on their aging. This report helps users track unpaid amounts, understand overdue status, and analyse payment patterns over different months.

## Steps to Generate the Bills Aging Summary – Monthwise Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Bills Aging Summary – Monthwise** option.

* The bills aging summary – Monthwise screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-56-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Group** drop-down.

1. Select the required group from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-57-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Party** selection field.

1. Select the required party from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-57-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advance Filter** option.

1. Select the required filter options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-58-41.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Verify all selected filters, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Bills Aging Summary – Monthwise report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-59-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the report :&#x20;

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
