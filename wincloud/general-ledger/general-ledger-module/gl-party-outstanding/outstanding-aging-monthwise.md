# Outstanding Aging-Monthwise

The **Outstanding Aging – Monthwise** report provides a detailed view of unpaid balances categorised month-wise based on their due dates or transaction dates. This report helps users analyse outstanding receivables and payables, identify overdue amounts, and monitor aging trends over time.

## Steps to Generate the Outstanding Aging – Monthwise Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Outstanding Aging – Monthwise** option.

* The outstanding aging-monthwise screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-33-47.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Group** drop-down.

1. Select the required group from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-34-27.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Party** selection field.

1. Select the required party from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-35-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advance Filter** option.

1. Select the required filter criteria from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-36-57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the required filters, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Outstanding Aging – Monthwise** report based on the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 12-37-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the report :&#x20;

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
