# Bill Ageing(Custom)

The **Bill Aging (Custom) Report** is used to analyze outstanding dues in ledger accounts and view aging details on a group-wise and account-wise basis. Unlike the standard Bill Aging Report, this report allows users to customize the aging periods according to their business requirements.

## Steps to Generate the Bill Aging (Custom) Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Bill Aging(Custom)** option.

* The Bill Aging(custom) screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-02-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Group** field.

1. Select the required group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-03-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Parties Search** option.

1. Search for and select the required account name or party from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-04-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-50-34.png>)

1. Apply the required filters to refine the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-05-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Options** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-48-41.png>)

1. Select the required report settings and display preferences.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-06-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Review all selected criteria, filters, and options.

1. Click the **Display** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-08-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Use the **Add Column** option to include additional fields in the report.

1. Use the **Delete Column** option to remove unnecessary fields from the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-09-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58.png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08.png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
