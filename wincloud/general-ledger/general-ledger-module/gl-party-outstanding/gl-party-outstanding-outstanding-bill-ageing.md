# Bill Aging

The **Bill Aging Report** is used to analyze outstanding dues in ledger accounts based on the age of pending bills. This report provides detailed information on receivables and payables, helping users monitor overdue balances and manage collections or payments effectively.

## Steps to Generate the Bill Aging Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Bill Aging** option.

* The Bill Aging screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-39-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Group** field.

1. Select the required group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-40-17.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Parties** option.

1. Search for and select the required account name or party from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-41-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Search Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-50-57.png>)

1. Apply additional filters to refine the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-43-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Option** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-48-41.png>)

1. Select the required report settings and display preferences.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-44-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Review all selected criteria and filters.

1. Click the **Display** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-47-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: After generating the Bill Aging Report:

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58.png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08.png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
