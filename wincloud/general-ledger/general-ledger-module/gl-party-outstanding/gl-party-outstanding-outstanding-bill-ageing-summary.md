# Bill Aging (Summary)

The **Bill Aging (Summary) Report** provides a summarized view of outstanding dues in ledger accounts on a group-wise and account-wise basis. This report helps users analyze pending receivables and payables according to their aging period without displaying detailed bill-level information.

## Steps to Generate the Bill Aging (Summary) Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Bill Aging (Summary)** option.

* The Bill Aging (Summary) Report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-30-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Group** field.

1. Select the required group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-31-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Parties Search** option.

1. Search for and select the required account name or party from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-32-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-50-57 (1).png>)

1. Apply the required filters to refine the report data.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-33-27.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review all selected groups, accounts, and filters.

1. Click the **Display** button.

* The Bill Aging (Summary) Report is generated and displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-34-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the Bill Aging Report:

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58.png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08.png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
