# Party Bills

The **Party Bills Report** allows users to view **party-wise bill details**. All receivable and payable transactions are consolidated and displayed under a single **Bill Amount** column, making it easier to review outstanding and completed bill transactions for selected parties.

## Steps to Generate the Party Bills Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Party Bills** option.

* The party bills screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 09-42-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-50-57.png>)

1. Select the required transaction type based on your reporting needs.

* Available options may include **Receivable, Payable** or **Both.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 09-43-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Group** drop-down or Lens Group selection field.

1. Select the required group from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 09-44-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Parties Search** button.

1. Search for the required party account.
2. Select the required account name from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 09-45-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the required filters, verify all selected criteria.

1. Click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Party Bills Report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 09-46-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
