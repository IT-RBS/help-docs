# Party Bills and Settlements

The **Party Bills and Settlements Report** provides a detailed view of all bill-related transactions and settlements for a selected party. The report displays receivable transactions, payable transactions, or both, including debit (DR) and credit (CR) entries recorded against the selected account.

## Steps to Generate the Party Bills and Settlements Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Party Bills and Settlement** option.

* The party bills and settlement screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 18-22-42.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Group** field.

1. Select the required group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 18-23-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Parties Search** option.

1. Search for and select the required account name or party from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 18-24-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.

1. Select the required transaction type based on your reporting needs.

* Available options may include **Receivable, Payable** or **Both.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 18-25-42.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review all selected filters and criteria.

1. Click the **Display** button.

* The Party Bills and Settlements Report is generated and displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 18-27-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After generating the report :&#x20;

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1382).png>)to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1383).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
