# Bill Aging Summary(Custom)

The **Bills Aging Summary (Custom) Report** provides a summarized, group-wise view of outstanding dues in ledger accounts. This report enables users to analyze receivables and payable based on customized aging periods without displaying detailed bill-level transactions.

## Steps to Generate the Bills Aging Summary (Custom) Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Party Outstanding** from the menu.
2. Select **Bills Aging Summary (Custom)** option.

* The Bills Aging Summary (Custom) screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-47-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Group** field.

1. Select the required group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-48-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Parties Search** option.

1. Search for and select the required account name or party from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-49-42.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-50-57.png>)

1. Apply the required filters to refine the report data.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-51-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review all selected groups, accounts, and filters.

1. Click the **Display** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-52-41.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Customize Report Columns

1. Click the **Add Column** option to include additional fields in the report.
2. Click the **Delete Column** option to remove unwanted fields from the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 17-09-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58.png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08.png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
