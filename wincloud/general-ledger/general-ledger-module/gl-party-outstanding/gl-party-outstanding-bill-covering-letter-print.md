# Bill Covering Letter Print

The **Bill Covering Letter – Print** feature allows users to print previously generated covering letters for **Receivable (Accounts)** transactions. Users can search for covering letters based on a specific party, date, or pending bill status and print the required covering letter whenever needed.

## Steps to Print a Bill Covering Letter

Step 1: Navigate to the General Ledger module.

1. Click Party Outstanding from the menu.
2. Select **Bill Covering Letter – Print** option.

* The **Bill Covering Letter – Print** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-03-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Group** drop - down.

1. Select the required group from the available list.
2. The system will display the parties associated with the selected group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 10-37-20 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Party** selection field.

1. Select the required party from the list.

* The system will retrieve the covering letters previously generated for the selected party.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-06-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Select the required  date range.

1. Click **Display** to view covering letters generated within the selected period.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-09-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* If you want to print covering letters only for outstanding bills:

1. Enable or click the **Pending Bills** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-11-01.png>)
2. The system will display covering letters associated only with pending bills.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-12-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To print a covering letter:

1. Select the required covering letter from the displayed list.
2. Verify the covering letter details.
3. Click the **Print** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 11-14-36.png>)
