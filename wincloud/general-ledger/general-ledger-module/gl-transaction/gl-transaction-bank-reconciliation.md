# Bank Reconciliation

**Bank Reconciliation** is the process of comparing and matching transactions recorded in the accounting software with transactions reflected in the bank statement. This ensures that the bank balance in the system is accurate, up-to-date, and synchronized with the actual bank records.

## Steps for Bank Reconciliation

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Bank Reconciliation** option.

* The Bank Reconciliation screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-29-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the bank account that you want to reconcile.

* The selected bank account details are loaded for reconciliation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-30-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the required **From Date** and **To Date** for the reconciliation period.

1. Define the time range for which transactions need to be compared with the bank statement.
2. Click the **Display** button.

* The system displays all transactions for the selected bank account and period.

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Search** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-11-58.png>)

1. Various filtering and sorting options will be available to help locate specific transactions.
2. Use the filters as required to narrow down the transaction list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-33-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review the displayed transaction list carefully.

1. Verify all transaction details, including Debit/Credit type and reference details
2. The Closing Balance and the Balance as per Bank Statement should match to ensure accurate reconciliation.

Step 6: Identify transactions that have been recorded in the accounting software, and successfully reflected in the bank statement.

1. Select the corresponding voucher details from the transaction list.
2. Mark the transaction as **Reconciled**.

* The selected transaction is prepared for reconciliation.

Step 7: Enter the date on which the transaction was cleared or realised by the bank.

1. Verify the selected voucher details and reconciliation date.

* The reconciliation date should match the date on which the transaction appears in the bank statement.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-35-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: After selecting all required transactions and entering the reconciliation date, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40.png>)

* The selected transactions are successfully reconciled and updated in the system.
