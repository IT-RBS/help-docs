# Fiscal Period Closing

**Period Closing** is a control mechanism used to close a specific accounting period (month) and prevent further transactions from being entered, modified, or deleted for that period.

Once a period is closed, users cannot create, edit, or post transactions within the selected month. This helps maintain the integrity of financial records and ensures that finalised accounting data remains unchanged.

## Steps for Period Closing

Step 1: Navigate to the General Ledger module.

1. Click Transaction from the menu.
2. Select **Period Closing** option.

* The Period Closing screen is displayed

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-55-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-56-21.png>)

* Review the list of available months displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-57-00.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the month that need to be closed.

* Carefully review the selected month(s) before closing, as this action restricts transaction processing for the chosen period.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-58-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40.png>)

* The selected month(s) are successfully closed.
