# Post-Dated Cheques

**Post-Dated Cheques (PDCs)** are cheques issued with a future date. These cheques cannot be presented for payment until the date specified on the cheque.

The Post-Dated Cheques module enables users to record, manage, and monitor future-dated cheque transactions accurately. This helps organisations maintain proper control over upcoming payments and receipts while ensuring that transactions are processed according to the cheque date.

## Steps for Recording a Post-Dated Cheque

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Post-Dated Cheques** option from the menu.

* The Post-Dated Cheques entry screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-08-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click **Add** button. ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-09-46.png>)

1. Select the Transaction Type and Transaction Date

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-10-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the transaction type and date, click the **Search** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-16-38.png>)available in the **Account Name** field.

1. Use the **Parties** list to locate the required account.
2. Search or browse through the available party records.
3. Select the appropriate account name.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-11-55.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter the cheque amount in the appropriate field.

1. Specify whether the amount is a **Debit** or **Credit** transaction based on the nature of the cheque.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-15-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40 (5).png>)

* The post-dated cheque transaction is successfully saved in the system.
