# Inter Company Transaction

An **Inter Company Transaction** is a financial transaction that occurs between two or more entities within the same corporate group. These transactions may involve the transfer of goods, services, loans, expenses, revenues, or cost allocations between related companies.

## Steps for Recording an Inter Company Transaction

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Inter Company Transaction** option.

* The Inter Company Transaction screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-26-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-27-33.png>)

1. Select the appropriate **Voucher Type** from the available options.
2. Enter or select the **Voucher Date**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-28-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Search** icon available in the **Account Name** field.

* The account selection window opens, displaying the list of available accounts.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-29-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Select the appropriate account name representing the related company involved in the transaction.

Step 5: Enter the required transaction information.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-33-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Confirm that the selected account and transaction details are correct.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40 (6).png>)

* The inter company transaction is successfully recorded in the system.
