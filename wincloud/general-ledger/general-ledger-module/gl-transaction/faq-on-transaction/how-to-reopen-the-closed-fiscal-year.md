# How to reopen the Closed Fiscal Year ?

To reopen a closed fiscal year in the system, use the Fiscal Year Reversing feature:

1. Navigate to Fiscal Year Reversing.
2. Enter a Narration (the reason for reopening/reversing the fiscal year).
3. Click the Processed button to reverse the closure.

_(Reversing a closed fiscal year allows you to make required corrections or adjustments to ledger transactions.)_

***

**Reopening a Closed Month / Period:**

If you need to reopen a closed monthly accounting period rather than an entire year, use Period Closing Reversing:

1. Click the Open button to view Period Closing Reversing.
2. Select the Months you want to reopen.
3. Click the Save button
