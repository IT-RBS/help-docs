# Voucher Approval

**Voucher Approval** is the formal authorisation process through which a designated approver reviews and validates a voucher before it is processed. This process ensures that the voucher is accurate and complete, supported by the required documents, compliant with organisational policies and procedures.

## Steps for Voucher Approval

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Voucher Approval**.

* The **Voucher Approval** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-00-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Locate the **Purchase Type** field on the Voucher Approval screen.

1. Select the required purchase type from the drop-down list.
2. Click the **Display** button.

* The system displays all vouchers related to the selected purchase type.

Step 3: Select the appropriate **Voucher Type**.

1. Verify the details once again.
2. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40.png>)

* The selected voucher is successfully approved, and the system displays a confirmation message.
