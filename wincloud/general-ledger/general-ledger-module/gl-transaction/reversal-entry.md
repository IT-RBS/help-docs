# Reversal Entry

A **Reversal Entry** is an accounting entry used to cancel or negate a previously recorded transaction by creating an equal and opposite entry. Reversal entries are commonly used to correct accounting errors, reverse accruals, or remove transactions that were posted incorrectly.

The reversal process helps maintain accurate financial records while preserving the audit trail of the original transaction.

## Steps for Creating a Reversal Entry

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Reversal Entry** option.

* The Reversal Entry screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-55-57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the appropriate options available on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-56-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Choose the required **Voucher Type** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-57-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Enter the **From Date** and **To Date** to define the transaction period.

1. Alternatively, click the **Search** icon to select the required period from the date selection window.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 14-59-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Advance Filter** option.

1. Apply additional filters to locate specific transactions more efficiently.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 15-04-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Select the Transaction for Reversal

1. Review the displayed transactions carefully.
2. Identify the transaction that needs to be reversed.
3. Select the required transaction.

Step 6: After selecting the transaction, verify all details.

1. Click the **Save** button.

* The system creates a reversal entry that offsets the original transaction.
