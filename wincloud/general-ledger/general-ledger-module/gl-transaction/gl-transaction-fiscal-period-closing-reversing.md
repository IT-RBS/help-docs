# Fiscal Period Closed Reversing

**Period Closing Reversing** is the process of reopening a previously closed accounting period (month). This option allows authorised users to reverse the period closure when additional transactions, corrections, or adjustments need to be entered for a closed month.

Once the period closing is reversed, users can create, modify, and process transactions within the reopened accounting period.

## Steps for Period Closed Reversing

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Period Closed Reversing** option.

* The Period Closed Reversing screen is displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 12-10-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-56-21 (1).png>)

* Review the list of closed months displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 12-12-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the month that need to be reopened.

1. Verify your selection before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 12-14-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40 (4).png>)

* The selected month(s) are successfully reopened, and the period closing is reversed.
