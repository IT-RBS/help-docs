# GL Transaction

The **GL Transaction** option is used to generate various accounting vouchers for financial transactions such as receipts, payments, journal entries, contra entries and other accounting vouchers.

* This guide explains how to create a **Payment Voucher**. The process for other voucher types is similar, although different accounting knowledge may be required depending on the transaction type.

## Steps to Create Payment Voucher

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **GL Transaction**.

* The GL Transaction screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-52-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **New** icon/button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-53-15.png>)

1. A new transaction entry screen will appear.

Step 3: Select the **Transaction Type**.

1. Select the required **Transaction Date**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-54-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Account Name** field.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-56-01.png>)

1. Select the required Supplier/Customer or Party Account from the **Chart of Accounts**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-56-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The selected account will be displayed in the transaction entry.

Step 5: Enter Credit/Debit Information

1. After selecting the account, enter the appropriate **Credit Name** as required.
2. Verify that the selected account and transaction type are correct before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-58-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Enter the payment amount in the **Amount** field.

1. Press the **B** key on your keyboard.

* The system will open the bill selection window.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 17-58-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Note:** The **B** key is used to link the payment against a bill or reference.

Step 7: Select Bill Reference Details

1. In the bill reference window, enter or select the following details such as **Reference Type, Reference Name (Bill Number), Bill Date, Due Date** and **Amount.**
2. After entering the required information, click **Done**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 18-02-05.png>)

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 18-01-48 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: If you select **Reference Type** as **Against Bill Reference**, the system will display all pending bills for the selected party.

1. To make a payment, select the bill(s) against which the payment is being made.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1380).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Enter the payment amount.
2. You can pay the bill in full or make a partial payment. The outstanding balance will remain pending against the same bill until it is fully settled.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 18-17-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 9: The system provides options to:

* **Add Row** – Create additional debit or credit entries.
* **Delete Row** – Remove unwanted entries.

Use these options whenever multiple ledger accounts are involved in the same transaction.

Step 10: For a Payment Voucher:

* The party account is generally entered on the Debit side.
* The corresponding Credit side should be the **Bank Account** or **Cash Account** from which the payment is made.

Step 11: Review all entered information and click **Save**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 18-25-02.png>)

Step 12: After saving the transaction:

* The system automatically generates a **Voucher Number**.
* Voucher numbers are generated based on the selected **Transaction Type**.
