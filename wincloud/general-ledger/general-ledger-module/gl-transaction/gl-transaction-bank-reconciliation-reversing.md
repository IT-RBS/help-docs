# Reconciliation Reversing

**Reconciliation Reversing** is the process of undoing or canceling a previously completed bank reconciliation. This feature allows users to correct errors, make adjustments, or reprocess transactions that were reconciled incorrectly.

When a reconciliation is reversed, the selected transactions are reopened and become available for reconciliation again. This ensures that transaction records remain accurate and aligned with the bank statement.

## Steps for Reconciliation Reversing

Step 1: Navigate to the General Ledger module.

1. Click **Transaction** from the menu.
2. Select **Reconciliation Reversing** option.

* The Reconciliation Reversing screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-02-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the bank account for which the reconciliation needs to be reversed.

* The selected bank account is loaded for reconciliation reversal processing.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-03-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the appropriate **From Date** and **To Date** corresponding to the period in which the transactions were originally reconciled.

1. Ensure that the selected date range includes the transactions you want to reverse.
2. Click the **Display** button.

* The system displays all reconciled transactions within the selected period.

Step 4: Click the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-11-58.png>)

1. Apply the required search criteria.

* The transaction list is filtered based on the selected criteria.

Step 5: Review the displayed transactions carefully.

1. Verify the transaction details before proceeding with the reversal.

* You can review **Credit (CR)/Debit** (DR) transactions and Foreign currency transactions.
* Ensure that the **Closing Balance** and **Balance as per Bank Statement** are verified for accuracy before making any changes.

Step 6: Identify the transaction(s) that were reconciled incorrectly.

1. Select the required reconciled transaction(s) from the list.
2. Mark the selected transaction(s) for reversal.

* The selected reconciled entries are prepared for reversal.

Step 7: Confirm the selected transactions.

* The reconciliation status of the selected transaction(s) is removed.
* The transaction(s) become unreconciled.
* The transaction(s) are made available again for future reconciliation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 11-07-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: If required, use multiple filters to locate specific transactions more efficiently( example: bill-wise or amount-wise search).

Step 9: After confirming the reversal of the selected transactions, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 10-03-40 (2).png>)

* The reconciliation reversal is successfully saved, and the selected transactions are available for reconciliation again.
