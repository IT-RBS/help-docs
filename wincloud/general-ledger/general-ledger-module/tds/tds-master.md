# TDS Master

The **TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Master** is used to define and manage TDS-related configurations within the accounting system. It records tax deductions made at the time of payment or credit, in accordance with applicable tax regulations.

This module ensures accurate tracking of deducted tax amounts, applicable rates, and related details for statutory reporting, compliance, and reconciliation purposes.

## Steps to Create TDS Master

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **TDS** from the menu.
2. Select **TDS Master.**

* The TDS Master screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-39-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-40-23.png>)

1. Enter the required TDS transaction details&#x20;

* Ensure all details are entered correctly as per tax requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-42-05.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Review all entered TDS details carefully.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 17-41-18.png>)

* The TDS Master configuration will be created and stored in the system.
