# Next Fiscal Year Creation

The **Next Fiscal Year Creation** option is used to create a new accounting fiscal year before closing the current fiscal year. This process ensures a smooth transition of financial data from the current year to the next year.

## Steps to Create Next Fiscal Year

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Next Fiscal Year** option.

* The next fiscal year screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 13-03-19.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **date range** for the new fiscal year.

1. Enable **New Fiscal Year** option.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 13-03-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After creation, click on **Proceed** butto&#x6E;**.**

* This action activates the new fiscal year for accounting operations.
