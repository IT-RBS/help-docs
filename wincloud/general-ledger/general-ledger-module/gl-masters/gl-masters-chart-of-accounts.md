# Chart of Accounts

The **Chart of Accounts** is used to create and manage accounting ledgers and groups within the system. It allows users to directly create ledgers (except Debtors and Creditors), modify existing ledger or group names, link analysis levels, and enter opening balances.

This module forms the foundation of the accounting system and ensures proper classification of financial transactions.

## Steps to Create Chart of Accounts

&#x20;Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Chart of Accounts** option.

* The **Chart of Accounts** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-19-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On the left side of the screen, the system provides four main accounting groups: **Assets, Liabilities, Income** and **Expenses.**

* These primary groups are pre-configured in the system. In addition, multiple subgroups are also available by default. Users can further:

1. Create new subgroups under existing groups
2. Create custom groups as required.

On the right side of the screen, users can manage ledgers and groups. The system provides options to create, modify, or manage accounting entries efficiently.

**Toolbar Options**

The following buttons are available at the top of the screen:

* New - Used to create a new **ledger or group**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-21-21.png>)
* Open - Used to modify an existing ledger or group name.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-22-15.png>)
* Delete - Used to remove the selected ledger or group from the system.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-22-42.png>)
* Refresh - Used to reload the screen and update the latest data.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-23-13.png>)
* Save - Used to save newly created or modified ledger or group details.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-23-46.png>)

Step 2: Select the **Ledger Type.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-25-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Choose the appropriate **Main Group**.
2. Select the **Subgroup**, if required.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-26-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **New** icon.

1. Choose whether you are creating a **Ledger** or a **Group** by selecting the appropriate option.

Step 4: Enter the **Ledger or Group Name**

1. Link **Analysis Level** if required
2. Enter **Opening Balance.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-28-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click **Save** to create the ledger or group.

1. The new entry will be added under the selected group structure.
