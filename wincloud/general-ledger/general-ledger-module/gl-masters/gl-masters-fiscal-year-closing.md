# Fiscal Year Closing

The **Fiscal Year Closing** option is used to close the current accounting year after ensuring that the **Balance Sheet is properly matched and finalised**. During this process, all ledger balances are transferred to the next fiscal year as opening balances.

## Steps for Fiscal Year Closing

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Fiscal Year Closing** option.

* The Fiscal Year Closing screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Enter the **Narration** or description for the fiscal year closing process.

Step 3: Click the **Proceed** button.

* The system will initiate the fiscal year closing process.
