# Company Settings

The **Company Settings** module is used to configure company-specific parameters that control the overall functioning of the system. These settings include **Accounts, Currency, Ledger Settings, Accounts Receivable/Accounts Payable (AR/AP), and other system options**.

## Steps to Configure Company Settings

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Company Settings**. option.

* The Company Settings screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-48-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-22-15.png>)

1. The available configuration options will be displayed.
2. Select the required settings based on your requirements.

Step 3: The system provides multiple configuration sections. You can edit and adjust settings in the following areas:

* **Accounts -** Used to configure general accounting rules and financial processing settings.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-49-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Currency -** Used to define base currency, currency format settings and Multi-currency handling (if enabled)

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-49-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Ledger Settings -** Used to manage ledger creation rules, behaviour & structure and control settings.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-50-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **AR/AP Settings -** Used to configure Accounts Receivable (AR) settings, Accounts Payable (AP) settings and credit terms and payment controls.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-51-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Others -** Includes additional system settings based on organisational requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-50-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Make the required changes in each relevant section.

1. After completing all changes, click the **Save** button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 12-23-46.png>)located at the top-right corner of the screen.

* The system will save and apply the updated company settings.
