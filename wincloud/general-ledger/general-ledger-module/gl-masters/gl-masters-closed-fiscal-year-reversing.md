# Fiscal Year Reversing

The **Fiscal Year Reversing** option is used to reverse a previously closed fiscal year in case corrections or adjustments are required. This feature allows authorized users to reopen a closed fiscal year for necessary modifications.

## Steps for Fiscal Year Reversing

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Fiscal Year Reversing** option.

* The Fiscal Year Reversing screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Enter a **Narration** explaining the reason for reversing the fiscal year.

* Ensure the narration is clear and properly documented for audit purposes.

Step 3: Click the **Proceed** button.

* The system will initiate the reversal process for the selected fiscal year.
