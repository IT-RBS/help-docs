# Fiscal Year Closing- Selective Ledger

The **Fiscal Year Closing – Selective Ledger** option allows users to close and carry forward only specific ledger accounts during the fiscal year-end process, while leaving other ledger accounts open.

## Steps to Process Fiscal Year Closing for a Selective Ledger

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Masters 1** from the menu.
2. Select the **Fiscal Year Closing -Selective Ledger** option.

* The Fiscal Year Closing - Selective ledger screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Select the required **Group** from the drop-down list.

* The system will display the ledgers associated with the selected group.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 16-21-44 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Search** icon and select the **Ledger Account** that needs to be closed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1386).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter a **Narration** describing the purpose of the selective ledger closing.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-10 16-22-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review the selected group, ledger, and narration.

1. Click the **Proceed** button.

* The system will process the closing for the selected ledger.
