# Opening Balance List

The **Opening Balance List** report displays the opening balances of ledger accounts at the beginning of a financial period. It serves as the starting point for tracking financial transactions and helps ensure accurate accounting and financial reporting.

## Steps to Generate the Opening Balance List Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Reports** from the menu.
2. Select **Opening Balance List** option.

* The opening balance list screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 15-44-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Group Name** drop-down.

1. Select the required group from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 15-45-17.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Advance Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 14-53-48.png>)

1. Select the required filter settings.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 15-46-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required group and filter options, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Opening Balance List report based on the selected parameters.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 15-50-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58.png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08.png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
