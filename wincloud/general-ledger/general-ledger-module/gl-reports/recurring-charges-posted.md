# Recurring Charges Posted

The **Recurring Charges Posted** report displays all recurring charges that have been automatically or periodically posted within a selected date range. These charges may represent recurring income, expenses, subscriptions, maintenance fees, rent, service charges, or other scheduled transactions configured in the system.

## Steps to Generate the Recurring Charges Posted Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Reports** from the menu.
2. Select **Recurring Charges Posted** option.

* The recurring charges posted screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 16-31-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the **From Date** and **To Date**.

Step 3: After selecting the required date range, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Recurring Charges Posted report for the selected period.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 16-32-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After generating the report :

* Click the **Print** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-58 (2).png>) to generate the report in a printable document format.
* Click the **Excel** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-08 16-37-08 (2).png>) to export the report to Microsoft Excel format for further analysis and reporting.
