# Income vs Expenses List

The **Income vs Expense List** report provides a detailed list of income and expense transactions for a selected period. It enables users to compare revenues against expenses, monitor financial activities, and evaluate overall financial performance.

## Steps to Generate the Income vs Expense List Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **General Ledger** module.

1. Click **Reports** from the menu.
2. Select **Income vs Expenses List** option.

* The income vs expenses list screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 16-12-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: .Click the **Search** icon.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 16-13-02.png>)

* Select the **From Date** and **To Date**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-09 16-13-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required date range, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Income vs Expense List report based on the selected period.
