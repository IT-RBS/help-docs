# PO Item Query

The **PO (Purchase Order) Item Query** feature is used to view and check the details of items included in a Purchase Order.

## **Steps to Access and Use PO Item Query**

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu on your dashboard.

1. In the Purchase menu, click on **Queries**.
2. From the drop-down, select **PO Item Query**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-40-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the PO Item Query screen, you can refine your search using the following filters:

1. **Date Range** – Select the start and end date to view POs within a specific period.
2. **Supplier Name** – Enter the supplier’s name to filter items by supplier.
3. **Item Selection** – Click the **search icon** next to the item field to select a specific item.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-42-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Once you have entered all required filters, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the list of Purchase Order items that match your criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-42-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After the results are displayed, you can export the data for further analysis:

1. Click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21.png>)

* The PO Item Query will be downloaded in **Excel format**, which you can save or print for reporting purposes.
