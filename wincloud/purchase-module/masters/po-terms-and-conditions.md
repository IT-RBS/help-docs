# PO Terms & Conditions

The **PO Terms & Conditions** master is used to define and manage standard purchase terms that apply to Purchase Orders (POs).

## **Steps to Create PO Terms & Conditions**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Masters**.
2. From the Masters menu, select **PO Terms & Conditions**.

* The **PO Terms & Conditions** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-30 (1).png>)

Step 3: Select the required **Purchase Terms,** From the available drop-down list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-49.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* After selecting the purchase terms, the system will display the selected terms on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-41-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step &#x34;**:** Click on the **Add** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-41-59 (1).png>)

1.  Enter the **Terms Name** in the provided field.

    * Ensure the terms name is clear, meaningful, and relevant to the purchase condition.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-48-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After entering the required details, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07 (3).png>)

* The system will successfully save the PO Terms & Conditions.
