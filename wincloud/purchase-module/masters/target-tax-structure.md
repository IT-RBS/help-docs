# Target Tax Structure

A **Target Tax Structure** is used to define and assign a specific tax structure to a supplier based on a selected transaction type. It helps ensure that the correct taxes are automatically applied during purchase transactions, improving accuracy and consistency in tax calculations.

## Steps to Create Target Tax Structure

Step 1: Go to the main menu of the application.

1. Click on the **Purchase** module.
2. Inside the Purchase module, click on **Masters**.
3. Select **Target Tax Structure** from the menu options.

Step 2: In the Target Tax Structure screen, click on the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-30 (4).png>)

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-45-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Type** field.

* Choose the required type from the available list shown on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-46-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Supplier** field.

1. Select the required supplier from the displayed supplier list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-47-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click on the **Tax Structure** field.

1. Choose the required tax structure from the available options.
2. After selecting the tax structure, click on the **Add** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-41-59 (3).png>)
3. The selected tax structure will be added to the configuration list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 14-49-08.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After verifying all entered details, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07 (7).png>)

* The Target Tax Structure will be saved successfully.
