# Approved Supplier Item

The **Approved Supplier Item** feature is used to map or assign approved products to a supplier. This helps ensure that purchases are made only from authorised suppliers for specific items.

## Steps to Create an Approved Supplier Item

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Approved Supplier Item** from the sub-menu.

* The Approved Supplier Item screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-02-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-02-33.png>)

Step 3: Click on the **Supplier Search** icon.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-11-03.png>)

1. Select the required supplier from the list.

* The selected supplier details will be displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-08-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Add** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-03-56.png>)

1. Click on the **Product Search** icon.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-10-15.png>)
2. Select the required product from the list.

* The selected product will now be linked to the supplier.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-04-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Verify that the correct supplier and product are selected.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-06-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-06-26.png>)

* The Approved Supplier Item will be saved successfully.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-12-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
