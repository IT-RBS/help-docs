# Approval Authority

The **Approval Authority** feature is used to assign users who are authorised to approve Purchase Order transactions based on TM Type, PO Type, and Request Category.

## Steps to View and Configure Approval Authority

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. In the **Purchase** menu, click on **Masters**.
2. Select **Approval Authority** from the sub-menu.

* The Approval Authority configuration screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-12-11.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-32 (2).png>)

Step 3: Configure Approval Authority Details

1. Select the required **TM Type** from the available list.
2. Select the appropriate **PO Type**.
3. Choose the required **Request Category**.
4. Select the user who will act as the **Approval Authority** for the selected configuration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 16-15-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Verify all selected details carefully.

1. Click on the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 15-39-16 (2).png>)

* The Approval Authority configuration will be saved successfully.
