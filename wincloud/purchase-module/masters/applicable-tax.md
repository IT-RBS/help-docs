# Applicable Tax

The **Applicable Tax** master is used to define and map taxes that are applicable for different types of purchases in the system.

## **Steps to Create Applicable Tax**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Masters**.
2. From the Masters menu, select **Applicable Tax**.

* The Applicable Tax screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 13-04-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-40-30 (2).png>)

Step 3: Select the required **Purchase Type** from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 13-05-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Choose the required **Applicable Tax** from the available options.

* Ensure the correct tax rate or tax category is selected.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 13-05-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After selecting the Purchase Type and Applicable Tax, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 12-19-07 (4).png>)

* The system will successfully save the applicable tax configuration.
