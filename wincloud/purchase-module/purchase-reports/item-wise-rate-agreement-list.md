# Item Wise Rate Agreement list

The **Item Wise Rate Agreement List** report is used to view the agreed prices (rates) for individual items based on the rate agreements created with suppliers.

## **Steps to View Item Wise Rate Agreement List**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Item Wise Rate Agreement List**.&#x20;

* After clicking **Item Wise Rate Agreement List**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-22-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41 (2).png>) option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report using specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-23-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08 (2).png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-24-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Item Wise Rate Agreement List** report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-24-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (3).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and documentation.
