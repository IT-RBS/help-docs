# Supplierwise PO Status

The **Supplier Wise PO Status** report is used to view the purchase order (PO) status grouped separately for each supplier.This report helps users monitor supplier-wise purchase activities, track order status, and analyze procurement details efficiently.

## **Steps to View Supplier Wise PO Status**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Supplier Wise PO Status**.

* After clicking **Supplier Wise PO Status**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-07-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41 (1).png>)option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report based on specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-07-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08 (1).png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-08-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Supplier Wise PO Status** report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-09-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (2).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and documentation.
