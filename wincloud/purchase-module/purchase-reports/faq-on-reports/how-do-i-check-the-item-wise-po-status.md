# How do I check the item-wise PO status?

To view the Item Wise PO Status report—which shows the status of each individual item listed in a purchase order rather than one overall status for the entire PO—follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Click on **Reports > Item Wise PO Status.**
3. Click on the Advance Filter option.
4. Click the option button to select your desired filtering options.
5. Click on the Display button to view the report on your screen.
6. _(Optional)_ Click on the Excel button to download the report in Excel format.
