# PO Audit Report

The **PO Audit Report** is used to review and verify Purchase Orders (POs) to ensure accuracy, compliance, and proper authorization.

## **Steps to View PO Audit Report**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **PO Audit Report**.

* After clicking **PO Audit Report**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-39-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **PO Type** from the available options.

* The PO Type helps users filter the report based on different categories of purchase orders.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-40-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41 (7).png>) option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report using specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-40-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **PO Audit Report** based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-43-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (8).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and audit documentation.
