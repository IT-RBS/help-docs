# Consolidated PO Status

The **Consolidated PO Status** report is used to view the combined status of multiple Purchase Orders (POs) in a single report.

## **Steps to View Consolidated PO Status**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Consolidated PO Status**.

* After clicking **Consolidated PO Status**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-28-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter** ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41 (6).png>)option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report using specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-28-57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08 (6).png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-29-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Consolidated PO Status** report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 11-30-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (7).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and documentation.
