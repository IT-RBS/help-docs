# PR Status Report

The **Purchase Requisition Status Report** is used to view the current status of purchase requisitions (PRs) raised within the organization.

## **Steps to View Purchase Requisition Status Report**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Purchase Requisition Status Report**.

* After clicking **Purchase Requisition Status Report**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-50-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41 (4).png>) option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps users refine the report using specific search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-51-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08 (4).png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-51-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Purchase Requisition Status Report** based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 10-52-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (5).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for further analysis and documentation.
