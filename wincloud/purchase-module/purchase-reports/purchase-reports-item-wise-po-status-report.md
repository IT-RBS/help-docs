# Item Wise PO Status

The **Item Wise PO Status** report is used to view the status of each individual item included in a Purchase Order (PO).Instead of showing one overall status for the entire purchase order, this report displays the status separately for every item.

## **Steps to View Item Wise PO Status**

Step 1: Navigate to the **Purchase** main menu from the dashboard.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. From the Reports menu, select **Item Wise PO Status**.

* After clicking **Item Wise PO Status**, the report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-53-41.png>) option available on the screen.

* The Advanced Filter helps you refine the report based on specific conditions.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-54-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-08.png>)

* Select the required filter options based on your reporting needs.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-55-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Item Wise PO Status** report based on the selected filters.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-56-27.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click on the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 09-43-21 (1).png>)

* The report will be downloaded in **Excel format** for reporting and analysis purposes.
