# Purchase Request Reprint

The **Purchase Requisition Reprint** transaction is used to view and print previously created Purchase Requisitions. This feature helps users reprint requisition documents whenever required for verification, approval, documentation, or record-keeping purposes.

## Steps to Reprint a Purchase Requisition

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Requisition Reprint**.

* After selecting the option, the Purchase Requisition Reprint screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-24-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **Date** range or transaction date.

1. Choose the appropriate **Status** from the available options.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-27-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will display the list of Purchase Requisitions based on the selected date and status.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-29-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: From the displayed list, select the required Purchase Requisition or bill that needs to be reprinted.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-32-49.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Print** button available at the top-right corner of the screen.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-35-13 (1).png>)

* The selected Purchase Requisition will open in print format and can be printed or saved as required.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-33-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
