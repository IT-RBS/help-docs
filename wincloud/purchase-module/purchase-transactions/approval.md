# Approval

The **Approval** transaction is used to review and approve Purchase Requisitions or purchase-related requests created by users. This process ensures that all purchase requests are verified and authorized before further procurement activities are carried out.

## Steps to Approve a Purchase Request

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Approval**.

* After selecting the Approval option, the Approval screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-48-01.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: The system will display the list of purchase requests or requisitions that are pending approval.

* Review the displayed details carefully before approving.

Step 3: Select the required purchase request(s) or item(s) that need approval.

* Ensure the selected requests are valid and authorized for processing.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-49-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Approve** button![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-51-04 (1).png>) located at the top-right corner of the screen.

* Once approved, the request status will be updated in the system and forwarded for further purchase processing.
