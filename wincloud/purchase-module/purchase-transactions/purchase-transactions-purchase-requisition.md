# Purchase Requisition

A **Purchase Requisition (PR)** is an internal request document used to inform the Purchase Department about the requirement for materials or services. It is created by department users, store personnel, or authorized staff whenever items need to be purchased.

## Steps to Create a Purchase Requisition

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Requisition**.

* After clicking **Purchase Requisition**, the Purchase Requisition entry screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 09-48-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Basis** field.

1. Select either **Goods** or **Service**

* Choose the appropriate option based on the requirement.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 09-50-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: In the **Request Name** field, enter the name of the requisition.

Step 4: Select the appropriate **Cost Center** for which the items are required.

1. The Cost Center field is displayed based on the Purchase Type settings configured in the system.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 09-51-31.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Select the **Purchase Order Date,** then select the required **Need By Date**.

Step 6: Choose the required **PO Type** from the available options.

* The PO Type defines the category or purpose of the purchase.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 09-57-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Select the appropriate **Requisition Category** below the PO Type.

Step 8: Click the **Add** button to add item details.

* Ensure that all item details are entered correctly before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-00-49.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 9: After entering all required details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-04-18.png>)

* The Purchase Requisition will be created successfully.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-02-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
