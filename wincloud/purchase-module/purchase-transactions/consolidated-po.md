# Consolidated PO

A **Consolidated Purchase Order (PO)** is a single Purchase Order created by combining multiple purchase requisitions or item requests into one PO. This process helps organizations simplify procurement activities by reducing the number of individual Purchase Orders issued to vendors.

* The Consolidated PO feature is useful when multiple items or requests are being purchased from the same supplier.

## Steps to Create Consolidated PO

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Consolidated PO**.

* The Consolidated PO screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-27-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **New** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-47-53 (1).png>)

1. Select the required **PO Type**.
2. Enter or select the **Vendor Details**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-34-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Add** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-36-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. To select products, click the **Search Icon** available next to the item or product field.

* The product selection list will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-38-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Choose the required items from the displayed product list.

* The selected products will be added to the Consolidated PO.

Step 4: Enter the required **Quantity** for each product.

1. Enter the applicable **Rate** or price.

* Verify the entered details carefully before saving.

Step 5: After entering all item details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-49-36.png>)

* The system will create the Consolidated Purchase Order successfully.
