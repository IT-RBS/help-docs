# PR Cancel

The **PR Cancel** option is used to cancel an existing Purchase Requisition (PR) that has already been created in the system. This feature allows users to stop further processing of a Purchase Request that is no longer required.

## Steps to Cancel a Purchase Requisition

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **PR Cancel**.

* After selecting the option, the PR Cancel screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-29-47.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-07-28 (2).png>)available in the PR Cancel screen.

Step 3: Click on the **Request No** field.

1. Choose the required Purchase Requisition (PR) that needs to be cancelled.

* Verify the selected PR details carefully before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-35-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the Purchase Requisition, click the **Save** button.

* Once saved, the selected Purchase Requisition will be marked as **Cancelled** in the system.
