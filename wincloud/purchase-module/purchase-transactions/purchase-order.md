# Purchase Order

A **Purchase Order (PO)** is an official commercial document issued by a buyer to a supplier for purchasing products or services. It contains details such as item names, quantities, prices, delivery terms, and other purchase-related information.

* The Purchase Order serves as a formal agreement between the buyer and the supplier and helps control the procurement process within the organization.

## Steps to Create a Purchase Order

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Order**.

* After selecting the option, the Purchase Order screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-17-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **PR** button![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-18-14 (1).png>)available on the Purchase Order screen.

* The system will display the list of available Purchase Requisitions.

Step 3: Select the required Purchase Requisition (PR) from the displayed list.

1. Verify the requisition details carefully.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-19-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **OK** button to confirm the selected Purchase Requisition.

* The item details from the PR will automatically appear in the Purchase Order entry screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-23-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Review all Purchase Order details carefully.

1. Click **Save** option to create the Purchase Order.

* Once saved, the Purchase Order will be generated successfully in the system.
