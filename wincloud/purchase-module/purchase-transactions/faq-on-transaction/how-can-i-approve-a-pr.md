# How can I approve a PR ?

We can approve a Purchase Requisition (PR) in the system.To approve a PR, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Click on **Transaction > Approval.**
3. Click the Open button and choose the relevant request number.
4. Click on the Save button to complete the approval.

**Approval Rules and Settings**

If you need to configure how these approvals are routed or who has permission to approve them, you can manage these rules in the Masters section:

* PO Approval Settings: Navigate to **Masters > PO Approval Settings** to select your transaction (Tm) type and PO type, then enable the appropriate Approval Level and Approval Procedure.
* Approval Authority: Navigate to **Masters > Approval Authority** to assign specific users as the authorized approvers based on transaction type, PO type, and request category.
