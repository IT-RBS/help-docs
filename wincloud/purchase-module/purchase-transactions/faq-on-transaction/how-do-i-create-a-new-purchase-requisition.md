# How do I create a new Purchase Requisition?

To create a new Purchase Requisition (PR) in the system, follow these step-by-step instructions:

1. Navigate to the Menu: Go to the Purchase main menu.
2. Open the Screen: Click on **Transaction > Purchase Requisition**.
3. Select Basis: Click on Basis and select either Goods or Service.
4. **Enter Request Details:**
   * Input the Request Name according to your requirements.
   * Select the Cost Center for which the items are required (the visibility of this field depends on your Purchase Type Settings).
   * Select the purchase order date and the required "Need By" date.
5. **Set the Category:** Select the Purchase Order (PO) type and choose the Req Category directly below it.
6. **Add Items:** Click the Add button to add a PO item. Enter the item name, category, product name, details, quantity, rate, and amount.
7. Save: Once everything is configured, click the Save button to finalize and create the purchase requisition.
