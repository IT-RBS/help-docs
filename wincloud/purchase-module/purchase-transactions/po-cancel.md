# PO Cancel

The **Purchase Order Cancel** option is used to cancel an already created Purchase Order (PO). This feature allows users to stop further processing of a PO when it is no longer required or was created incorrectly.

* Once a Purchase Order is cancelled, it cannot be used for any further procurement activities.

## Steps to Cancel a Purchase Order

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Order Cancel**.

* The Purchase Order Cancel screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-33-55.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-07-28.png>)in the PO Cancel screen.

Step 3: Click the **PO No Search Icon**.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-10-26.png>)

1. A list of Purchase Orders will be displayed.
2. Select the required **PO**.
3. Choose the appropriate **PO Type** and **Vendor Name.**

* Ensure the correct Purchase Order is selected before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-34-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **PR button**.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-18-14.png>)

1. The PR item selection window will appear.
2. Select the required **Purchase Requisition (PR)** linked to the PO.
3. Click **OK**.

* The selected PR items will be loaded into the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-41-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-13-52 (3).png>)

* The system will cancel the selected Purchase Order successfully.
