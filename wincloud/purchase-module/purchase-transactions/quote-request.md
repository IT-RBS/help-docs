# Quote Request

A **Quote Request** is a request sent to a vendor or supplier asking for pricing details, terms, and availability of specific items or services. It helps organizations compare vendor quotations before creating a Purchase Order.

## Steps to Create a Quote Request

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Quote Request**.

* The Quote Request screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-05-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **PR** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-18-14 (2).png>)

1. The list of available Purchase Requisitions will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1366).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Choose the required items or Purchase Requisition from the displayed list.

* The selected items will be loaded into the Quote Request screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-21-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After verifying the details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-49-36 (2).png>)

* The Quote Request will be created successfully and can be sent to vendors for quotation.
