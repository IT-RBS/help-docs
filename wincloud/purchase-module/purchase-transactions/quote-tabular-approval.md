# Quote Tabular Approval

**Quote Tabular Approval** is a process used to review and approve vendor quotations displayed in a tabular format. This method allows users to compare multiple vendor quotations side by side for better evaluation and approval decisions.

## Steps for Quote Tabular Approval

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Quote Tabular Approval**.

* The Quote Approval screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-59-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 17-24-13 (1).png>)

* The system will display the list of available RFQ (Request for Quotation) numbers.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 16-59-47.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the required **RFQ No** from the displayed list.

1. Verify the RFQ details carefully.
2. Click the **OK** button.

* The quotation comparison screen will appear in tabular format.

Step 4: Review the displayed vendor quotations.

1. Select the required quotation by enabling the **Tick Mark** or selection option.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 17-02-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 15-49-36 (3).png>)

* The selected quotation will be approved successfully.
