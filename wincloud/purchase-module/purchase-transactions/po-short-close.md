# PO Short Close

The **PO Short Close** option is used to close Purchase Orders that are partially used, not used, or pending for a long period. This helps ensure that inactive or unnecessary Purchase Orders are properly closed in the system.

## Steps for PO Short Close

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **PO Short Close**.

* The PO Short Close screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-07-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-07-28 (3).png>)

Step 3: Click the **Search icon**![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-10-26 (1).png>) to view available Purchase Orders.

1. The list of Purchase Orders will appear.
2. Double-click on the required **PO** that needs to be short closed.

* The selected Purchase Order details will be loaded into the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 14-12-32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the PO, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 11-13-52 (2).png>)

* Once saved, the Purchase Order will be marked as **Short Closed** in the system.
