# Purchase Order Reprint

The **Purchase Order Reprint** option is used to view and print previously created Purchase Orders. This feature helps users retrieve Purchase Orders for documentation, supplier communication, verification, and audit purposes.

* Users can search Purchase Orders based on date range and PO Type, then print the required Purchase Order directly from the system.

## Steps to Create Purchase Order Reprint

Step 1: Go to the **Purchase** main menu.

1. Click **Transaction**.
2. Select **Purchase Order Reprint**.

* After selecting the option, the Purchase Order Reprint screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-42-37.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the **From Date** and **To Date**.

1. Choose the required **PO Type**.

* The selected criteria will help filter the required Purchase Orders.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-47-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will display the list of Purchase Orders matching the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-47-48.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: From the displayed list, select the required Purchase Order (PO).

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-48-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Print** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-35-13 (2).png>)

* The selected Purchase Order will open in print format and can be printed or saved as required.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 12-51-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
