# Banquet Advance

The Banquet Advance report is used to view advance payment details collected for banquet bookings. This report helps track all advance transactions within a selected date range and allows users to filter and view specific records using advanced search options.

## Steps to view Banquet Advance&#x20;

Step 1: Go to the **Reports** menu in the **Banquet** module.

* Click on **Banquet Advance**.

Step 2: After opening the report, select the required date range.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m12 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Advanced Search** button available on the screen.

* This option allows you to filter records based on specific criteria.
* Select the required search criteria in the Advanced Search fields.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m12.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering the details, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will display the Banquet Advance report.
