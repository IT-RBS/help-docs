# Banquet Sale Day Book (Monthly)

The **Banquet Sales Day Book (Monthly)** report is used to view and analyse banquet sales on a daily basis, summarized month-wise. It helps in tracking all banquet-related transactions for each day within a selected month.

## Steps to access Banquet Sale Day Book (Monthly)

Step 1: Go to the **Reports** menu in the **Banquet** module.

1. Click on **Banquet Sales Day Book (Monthly)** from the list of available reports.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m10 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the report screen, locate the Search option.

1. Click on the Search option to open the filter criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m10.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After entering the required search criteria, click on the Display button.

* The system will generate and show the Banquet Sales Day Book (Monthly) report on the screen.
