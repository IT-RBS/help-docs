# Discount List

The **Discount List report** is used to view all discounts applied within a selected date range. This helps in tracking discount usage, analysing revenue impact, and reviewing promotional or approved discounts.

## **Steps to View Discount List Report**

Step 1: Go to the **Reports** menu in the Banquet module.

1. Click on **Discount List.**

Step 2: After opening the report, select the required date range.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m14 (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Advance Filter** option.

* The filter section will open, allowing you to customise the search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m14.1 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filter criteria, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will display the Discount List.
