# NC-KOT Summary

The **NC KOT (No Charge Kitchen Order Ticket) Summary Report** is used to view all complimentary or no-charge food and beverage orders generated in the system. This report helps track items issued without billing, which is useful for audit control and management review.

## **Steps to View NC KOT Summary Report**

Step 1: Log in to the system and navigate to the **Banquet Module**.

1. Click on the **Reports** menu.
2. Select **NC KOT Summary** from the list of available reports.

Step 2: After selecting **NC KOT Summary**, the system will open the report page.

* By default, the system displays the summary based on a **predefined day/date range**.
* This default view shows all NC KOT entries within that period.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m18 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the required **From Date** and **To Date**.

* Ensure the correct date range is selected to filter the report accurately.

Step 4: After selecting the date range, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and show the **NC KOT Summary Report** based on the selected dates.
