# How can I view a report of all advance refunds?

To view a report of all advance refunds, follow these steps in the system:

1\. Navigate to the Reports menu and click on Banquet Advance Refund.

2\. The system will display the Banquet Advance Refund screen, which is designed to show refunds within a specific Day Range.

3\. If you need to refine your search, click on the Advance search button to see additional filter options.

4\. Click the Display button to generate and view the report.

This report provides a comprehensive view of all processed refunds, complementing the Advance Refund transaction found in the Cashier menu
