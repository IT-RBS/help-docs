# Banquet Sales Day Book

The **Banquet Sales Day Book** is a report used to view all banquet-related sales recorded for a specific day. It helps track daily banquet revenue, bookings, and transaction details in an organized manner.

### Steps to Access Banquet Sales Day Book

Step 1: Go to the **Reports** menu in the **Banquet** module.

1. Click on **Banquet Sales Day Book**.

Step 2: After selecting the report, the system will display all **banquet sales for the selected day**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m9 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Use Advanced Search Option

* Click on the **Advanced Search** option available on the screen.
* This option allows you to filter and refine the report based on specific criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 11-16-42.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After applying the required filters, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and show the updated **Banquet Sales Day Book report** based on your selected criteria.
