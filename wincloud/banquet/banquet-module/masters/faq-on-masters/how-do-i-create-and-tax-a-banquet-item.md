# How do I create and tax a Banquet Item?

To create and apply tax to a Banquet Item, you must use the Masters menu. Follow these specific steps:

1\. **Navigate to Banquet Item:** Go to the Masters menu and select Banquet Item.

2\. **Initiate New Item:** Click the New button.

3\. **Enter Identification Details:** Provide a unique Item Id and the Item Name.

4\. **Assign Groups and Tax:**

&#x20;   ◦ Click the Group button to categorize the item.

&#x20;   ◦ Click on Tax Structure to select and apply the appropriate tax settings for the item.

5\. **Configure Additional Links:**

&#x20;   ◦ Click on Venue to associate the item with specific locations.

&#x20;   ◦ Click on Analysis Group to link it for reporting purposes.

&#x20;   ◦ Click on Link Revenue and choose the correct category from the Revenue Search.

6\. **Finalize:** Once all details are entered, click the Save button to create the item.

If you are setting up other types of billable items, such as Plan Items or Open Items, the process is similar:

• For Plan Items, you navigate to Masters > Banquet Settings (or the specific Plan Items master if available) and enter the Plan Id, Name, Rate, and Tax Structure before linking to a Revenue Group and saving.

• For Open Items, you select the Banquet Group, enter the item name and Sale Rate, and then click Tax Structure to define its taxation before saving.
