# How do I add market segments in the Master settings?

To add market segments in the Master settings, follow these steps:

1\. Navigate to the Masters menu and click on Market Segment.

2\. Click on the New button.

3\. Enter the required details, specifically the Market Segment Id and Market Segment Name.

4\. Click on the Save button to complete the creation of the Market Segment.

Once created, these segments can be used throughout the system. For instance, when creating a Function Type in the Master settings, you can associate it with a specific Market Segment. These segments also become available as filters in Analysis reports, such as the Banquet Sales Performance report, to help track business from different sectors.
