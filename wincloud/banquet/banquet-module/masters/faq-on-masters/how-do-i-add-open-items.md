# How do I add open items?

To add and tax open items within the system, you must configure them in the Master settings by following these steps:

1\. Navigate to Open Item: Go to the Masters menu and click on Open Item.

2\. Select a Group: Click on Banquet Group and choose the appropriate group from the list.

3\. Enter Item Details: Once the group is selected, enter the Item Name, the Sale Rate, and the Sale Cost.

4\. Apply Taxation: Click on the Tax Structure button to define and apply the relevant taxes for that specific open item.

5\. Assign Sub Group: Click on Sub Group to further categorize the item if necessary.

6\. Save: Click the Save button to finalize the creation of the open item.

These items then become available for analysis in reports like Item Wise Sales, where you can specifically filter by Open Item to track their performance.
