# How do I link a Banquet Item to specific venues?

To link a Banquet Item to specific venues, you must configure this association within the Banquet Item master settings. Follow these steps:

1\. Navigate to the Masters menu and click on Banquet Item.

2\. Click the New button to create a new item, or select an existing one to edit.

3\. In the item details screen, click on the Venue button.

4\. Select the specific venue or venues you wish to associate with this item.

5\. Once you have completed the configuration, click the Save button to finalize the link.

By linking items to venues in the Master settings, you ensure that these items are correctly associated with the appropriate locations when creating Reservations or recording Kot/Charges
