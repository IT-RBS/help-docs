# How do I create and manage venue groups?

To create and manage Venue Groups, you use the Masters menu. Based on the sources, follow these steps:

1\. Navigate to the Masters menu and click on Venue Group.

2\. Click the New button to initiate a new entry.

3\. Enter the required identification details for the group:

&#x20;   ◦ Group Id

&#x20;   ◦ Group Name

&#x20;   ◦ Group Short Name

&#x20;4\. Click the Save button to finalize the creation of the Venue Group.

While these steps cover the initial creation, "management" typically involves associating these groups with specific venues. While the detailed steps for linking them are not explicitly itemized in the "Venue Group" section, you can define individual venues through Masters > Venue, where you enter details like the Venue Id, Name, and Type.
