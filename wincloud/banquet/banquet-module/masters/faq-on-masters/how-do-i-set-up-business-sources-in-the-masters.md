# How do I set up business sources in the masters?

To set up Business Sources in the Master settings, follow these steps:

1\. Navigate to the Masters menu and click on Business Source.

2\. Click on the New button.

3\. Enter the required information, specifically the Business Source Id and the Business Source Name.

4\. Click the Save button to complete the setup.

Once these sources are established in the Masters, they become available for selection in the Business Source dropdown menu when you are blocking a venue or creating a new reservation
