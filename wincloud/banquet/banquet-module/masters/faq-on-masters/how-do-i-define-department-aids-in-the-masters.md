# How do I define department aids in the masters?

To define Department Aids in the system, you must configure three interconnected settings in the Masters menu: the Department, the Aids Group, and finally the Aid itself.

1\. **Set up the Department**

Before creating an aid, ensure the relevant department exists.

• Navigate to Masters and click on Department.

• Click the New button.

• Enter a Department Id and Department Name.

• Click Save.

2\. **Create an Aids Group**

This allows you to categorize different types of aids.

• Navigate to Masters and click on Aids Group.

• Click the New button.

• Enter an Aids Group Id and Aids Group Name.

• Click Save.

3\. **Define the Aid**

Once the department and group are ready, you can define the specific aid.

• Navigate to Masters and click on Aids.

• Click the New button.

• Enter the Aids Id and Aids Name.

• Select the appropriate Aids Group List and Depart Name from the entries you created in the previous steps.

• Click Save to complete the process.

After these are defined in the Masters, they will be available for selection in the Department Aids field when you are creating a Reservation under the Transactions menu.
