# Banquet Settings

The **Banquet Settings** section is used to configure and manage various operational settings related to the Banquet Module. These settings help users define system preferences, revenue configurations, cashier controls, reservation options, and other operational parameters required for banquet management.

## **Steps to Access Banquet Settings**

Step 1: Log in to the system and navigate to the **Banquet Module**.

1. Click on the **Masters** menu.
2. Select **Banquet Settings** from the available options.

Step 2: After selecting **Banquet Settings**, the system will open the settings page.

* The screen displays different configuration sections such as Main, Revenue, Cashier, Others, Reservation.
* These sections allow users to configure various banquet-related settings.

Step 3: Click on the **Main** button.

* The **Main Settings** screen will appear.
* The Main Settings section is used to configure the primary operational settings for the Banquet Module.

Step 4: Click on the **Revenue** button.

* The **Revenue Settings** screen will appear.
* The Revenue Settings section is used to configure financial and revenue-related options.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 16-11-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click on the **Cashier** button.

* The **Cashier Settings** screen will appear.
* The Cashier Settings section is used to configure billing and cashier-related controls.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 16-12-38.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Click on the **Others** button.

* The **Other Settings** screen will appear.
* The Others section contains additional operational settings required for banquet management.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 16-13-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Click on the **Reservation** button.

* The **Reservation Settings** screen will appear.
* The Reservation Settings section is used to configure banquet reservation and booking preferences.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 16-13-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: After completing all configurations, click the Save button.
