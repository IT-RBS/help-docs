# Banquet Status

The **Banquet Status** menu is used to view and generate banquet booking status reports for a selected date range.

## Steps to View Banquet Status

Step 1: Navigate to the **Banquet** module and select **Queries** menu.

1. Click on **Banquet Status** from the menu list.

* After selecting the menu, the Banquet Status screen will open.

Step 2: Select the required **Date** or **Date Range**.

1. Click on the **Display** button.

* The system will display the banquet status details for the selected date.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Use Advanced Search Filters

1. Click on the **Advanced Search** button.
2. The filter section will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m2 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After applying the required filters:

1. Click on the **Display** button.
2. The system will generate and display the report on the screen.

* The report will contain all banquet booking details based on the selected criteria.

Step 5: Once the report is displayed:

* Click on the **Print** button to print the report.
* Click on the **Excel** button to export the report in Excel format.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m3 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This allows users to save, share, or print the banquet status report for future reference.
