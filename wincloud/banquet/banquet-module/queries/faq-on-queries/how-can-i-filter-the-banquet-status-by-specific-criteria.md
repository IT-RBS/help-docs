# How can I filter the Banquet Status by specific criteria?

To filter the Banquet Status query by specific criteria, follow these steps within the system:

1\. Navigate to the Queries menu and select Banquet Status.

2\. Select the desired date and click the Display button to load the initial results.

3\. To apply specific filters, click on the Advance search button, which will reveal the available filter options.

4\. Once you have applied your criteria, you can view the results on the screen or export them by clicking the Print or Excel buttons to get the report in your preferred format.

While the sources note that the Advance search button is the primary way to filter this specific query, other related reports offer more specific filtering capabilities you might find useful:

**• Reservation Short Close:** Allows filtering by Function Days, Bill Printed, and Pending Advance Booking.

**• Banquet Settlement:** Offers a User filter to view settlements by specific staff members.

**• Banquet Sales Performance:** Includes filters for Market Segment and other Advance Filter options.

**• Business On Book:** Provides filters for Property, Venue, and Business.
