# Where can I view all cancelled bills and KOTs?

To view all cancelled bills and KOTs, you can use the following locations in the system:

1\. Cancelled List (Queries Menu)

The most direct way to view a comprehensive list of cancelled items is through the Queries menu:

• Navigate to Queries and click on the Cancelled menu.

• Click the Display button to view the list of cancelled transactions.

2\. Void Bill Report (Reports Menu)

For a specific report focused on cancelled (voided) bills over a period of time:

• Navigate to Reports and click on Void Bill.

• Select the desired Day range and click the Display button to view the voided bills.

**Additional Cancellation Tracking**

While the reports above show the results, individual cancellations are performed and tracked through these specific cashier functions:

• **Settled Bills:** These are cancelled via Cashier > Bill Settlement/Cancellation, where a Cancel Reason must be provided.

• **Provisional Bills:** These are tracked via Cashier > Provisional Bill Cancellation.
