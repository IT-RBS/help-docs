# Pending Bills

The **Pending Bills** feature is used to view all unpaid or pending bills in the system. This report helps users monitor outstanding payments and generate reports for billing follow-up and financial tracking.

## Steps to View Pending Bills

Step 1: Navigate to the **Banquet** module and select **Queries** menu.

1. Click on **Pending Bills**.

* After selecting the menu, the **Pending Bills** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m7 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the **Pending Bills** screen, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will retrieve and display the list of all pending bills.

* The report will show the outstanding billing details available in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m7.1 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After the pending bills report is displayed, the report screen will appear.

1. Click one of the following buttons to generate the report:

* **Print** – To print the pending bills report.
* **Excel** – To export the report in Excel format.

The report will be generated in the selected format.
