# Cancelled List

The **Cancelled List** feature is used to view all cancelled reservations, bookings, or transactions available in the system. This report helps users monitor cancelled records and maintain proper tracking of cancelled activities.

## Steps to View the Cancelled List

Step 1: Navigate to the **Banquet** module and select **Queries** menu.

1. Click on **Cancelled**.

* After selecting the menu, the **Cancelled List** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/m8 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the **Cancelled List** screen, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will retrieve and display all cancelled records below the screen.
2. The displayed report will contain the details of cancelled reservations, bookings, or transactions available in the system.
