# Pending KOT/Charges

The **Pending KOT/Charges** feature is used to view all pending Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) and pending charges that have not yet been settled or billed. This report helps users track outstanding food orders and additional charges efficiently.

## Steps to View Pending KOT/Charges

Step 1: Navigate to the **Banquet** module and select **Queries** menu.

1. Click on **Pending KOT/Charges**.

* After selecting the menu, the **Pending KOT/Charges** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-19 10-44-30.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the **Pending KOT/Charges** screen, click the **Display** button.

1. The system will retrieve and display the list of all pending KOTs and charges.

* The displayed report will contain details of unsettled food orders and pending charges available in the system.
