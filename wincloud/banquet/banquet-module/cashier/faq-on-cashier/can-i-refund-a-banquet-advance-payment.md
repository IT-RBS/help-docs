# Can I refund a Banquet advance payment?

Yes, you can refund a banquet advance payment by using the Advance Refund feature located in the Cashier menu.

The process involves the following steps:

• Navigate to Advance Refund under the Cashier menu.

• Click on Reservation No. and choose the specific reservation that requires a refund.

• Enter the amount to be refunded and select the appropriate payment mode.

• Click Save to finalize the transaction.

To track these transactions for auditing or reporting, you can generate a Banquet Advance Refund report by going to Reports > Banquet Advance Refund, selecting the desired date range, and clicking Display.
