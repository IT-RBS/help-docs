# What is the difference between Bill Reprint and Provisional Reprint?

The difference between Bill Reprint and Provisional Bill Reprint primarily lies in the type of document being generated and the stage of the billing process it represents, although both share similar procedural requirements.

• Target Document: Bill Reprint is used to generate a copy of a finalized or settled bill. In contrast, Provisional Bill Reprint is used specifically for provisional bills, which are typically interim documents issued before final settlement.

• Menu Location: Both functions are located under the Cashier menu.

• Requirement for a Reason: Both processes require the user to enter a reason before the system allows the reprint to proceed, ensuring accountability for generating duplicate financial documents.

• Procedural Steps:

&#x20;   ◦ For a Bill Reprint, you navigate to the menu, click Display to see the list, select the specific bill, enter the reason, and click the Bill button.

&#x20;   ◦ For a Provisional Bill Reprint, you navigate to the menu, choose the provisional bill from the list, click Select, enter the reason, and click the Print Button.

• Auditing and Tracking: Finalized bills that are reprinted are specifically tracked in the Bill Reprint Audit report. This report displays reprint details for a selected day range and can be filtered by User to monitor who performed the reprint and why. The sources do not mention a similar specific audit report for provisional reprints
