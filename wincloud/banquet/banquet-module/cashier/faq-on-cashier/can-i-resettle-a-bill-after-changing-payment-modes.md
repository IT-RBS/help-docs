# Can I Resettle a bill after changing payment modes?

Yes, you can resettle a bill specifically to change the payment or settlement mode by using the Bill Resettlement feature in the system.

The process for resettling a bill is as follows:

1\. Navigate to the Cashier menu and select Bill Resettlement.

2\. Click on Bill No and select the specific bill that requires resettlement.

3\. Change the settlement mode to the new desired payment method.

4\. Type a reason for the change.

5\. Click settle to finalize the resettlement and complete the transaction.

This feature is particularly useful when a guest decides to pay using a different method after the initial bill has already been settled.
