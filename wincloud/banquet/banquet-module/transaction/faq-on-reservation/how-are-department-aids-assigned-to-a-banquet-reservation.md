# How are Department Aids assigned to a banquet reservation?

To assign Department Aids to a banquet reservation, you must enter them during the reservation creation process under the Transactions menu. The specific steps are as follows:

1\. Navigate to Reservation under the Transactions menu and click New to start a new entry or select an existing one to edit.

2\. Within the reservation screen, click on the Venue tab.

3\. Locate the field labeled Department Aids and enter the specific requirements for the event. This field is typically grouped with other venue-specific details such as the seating arrangement and event start/end times.

4\. Once you have completed all the necessary details across the different tabs (Guest Info, Venue, F\&B, etc.), click the Save button to finalize the reservation and successfully assign the aids.

For these aids to be available for selection, they must first be defined in the Masters menu under Aids, where they are linked to specific Departments and Aids Groups.
