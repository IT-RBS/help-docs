# Guest Profile

**Guest Profile**

This option is to see or modify the guest profiles you created during reservation and walk in.

Click **New** to create new profile, **Open** to Edit profile.

Option is divided in multiple sections.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/1 (157).png>)

&#x20;Multi-screen option is self-explanatory,

**Address** section is to fill in Names and other FO requirement information along with the address.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/2 (21).png>)

**Personal** section is for personal info. And pictures such as Spouse details and anniversary.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/3 (129).png>)

**Likes/ Dislikes** section is to fill in or see the likes and dislikes of guests.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/4 (109).png>)

**Passport** is to see passport related info or any other legal document.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/5 (55).png>)

**Visits** to see the past visits with details of amount etc.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/6 (76).png>)

**Discount** section is to see or define default discounts once the guests are In-House.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/7 (21).png>)

Complaint section is to see the Complaint/suggestions/ requests.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/8 (13).png>)
