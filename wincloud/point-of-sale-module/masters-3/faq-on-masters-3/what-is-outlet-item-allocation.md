# What is Outlet-Item allocation?

Outlet-Item allocation refers to specialized methods within the system used to assign KOT printers to specific items at the outlet level, providing more granular control than standard group-based allocation.There are two primary ways to manage this:

**1. Outlet-Item Printer Allocation**

This method allows you to assign specific printers to individual items within a chosen outlet.

* **Navigation: Master3 > Outlet-Item Printer Allocation.**
* **Process:** Click Open, select the relevant outlet, then click Add to choose the printer name for those items.

**2. Outlet-Item System KOT Printer Allocation**

This is a more specific configuration where items are allocated to a KOT printer based on both the outlet and the specific computer system (terminal) used to place the order.

* **Navigation: Master3 > Outlet - Item System Kot Printer Allocation.**
* **Process:** Click New, select the Outlet name and System, then click Add to choose the specific items to be allocated.

**Comparison with Standard Allocation**

While these methods offer item-level precision, the system also supports KOT Printer Allocation by product group (via **Master1 > Kot > Kot printer Allocation**), which is used to assign printers to entire categories of products at once.

**Verification and Monitoring**

To ensure these allocations are functioning correctly, you can use the Outlet Item Audit report. This audit provides a review to:

* Monitor and verify item-wise sales and usage.
* Ensure correct item configuration and pricing.
* Maintain billing accuracy and prevent discrepancies or misuse.
