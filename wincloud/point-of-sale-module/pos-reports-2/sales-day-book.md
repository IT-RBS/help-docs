# Sales Day Book

The **Sales Day Book Report** is used to view all sales transactions recorded for customers on a particular day. This report provides a complete overview of daily sales activities in a single report format.

## Steps to View the Sales Day Book Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and click the **Reports** menu.

1. Select **Sales Day Book**.

* The Sales Day Book screen will open.

Step 2: In the Sales Day Book screen, choose the required **Advanced Filter** options.

1. Select the necessary criteria based on your reporting requirement.

* The filter options help users generate the report according to specific conditions.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/7 (75).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required **Date**.

1. Click on the **Display** button.

* The Sales Day Book Report will be generated and displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/8 (60).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Important Note:**

* This report can be generated **only on a day-wise basis**.

If you want to export the report to Excel:

1. Click on the **Excel Export Icon** available on the report screen.
2. The report will be downloaded in Excel format for further use and analysis.
