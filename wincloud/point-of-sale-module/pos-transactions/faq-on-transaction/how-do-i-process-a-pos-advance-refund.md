# How do I process a POS Advance Refund?

To process a POS Refund, follow these steps within the transactions menu:

1. **Access the Menu:** Go to the Transaction Main Menu and click on POS Refund.
2. **Start a New Transaction:** Click on the New button.
3. **Identify the Advance:** Click on the "P" icon to view the available POS Advances.
4. **Enter Refund Details:**
   * Input the Advance Amount to be refunded.
   * Select the appropriate Settlement mode.
5. **Save:** Click the Save button to complete the refund process
