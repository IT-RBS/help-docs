# How do I record a POS Advance?

To record a POS Advance in the system, follow these steps within the transactions menu:

1. **Access the Menu:** Go to the Transaction Main Menu and click on POS Advance.
2. **Start a New Entry:** Click on the New button.
3. **Select Outlet:** Choose the relevant Outlet name from the options provided.
4. **Select Guest:** Click on the Guest Search button to find and select the specific Guest name.
5. **Enter Payment Details:**
   * Input the Advance Amount.
   * Choose the appropriate Settlement mode.
6. **Save:** Click on the Save button to record the transaction
