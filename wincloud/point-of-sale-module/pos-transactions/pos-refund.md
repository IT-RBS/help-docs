# POS Refund

The POS Refund feature is used to refund advance payments that were previously collected through the POS Advance option. This helps manage guest refunds and maintain accurate transaction records.

## Steps to Process a POS Refund

Step 1: Go to the **Point of Sale** module and select the **Transaction** menu.

Step 2: Click on **POS Refund**.

* The POS Refund screen will appear.

Step 3: Click the **New** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-45-19 (1).png>)

Step 4: Select the POS Advance Record

1. Click on the **P Icon** available in the screen.

* The system will display the list of available POS Advance entries.

Step 5: Enter the **Refund Amount**.

Step 6: Choose the required **Settlement Mode** for the refund.

Step 7: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-46-00.png>)

* The POS refund transaction will be completed successfully.
