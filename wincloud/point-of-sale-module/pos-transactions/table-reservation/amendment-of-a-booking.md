# Amendment of a Booking

* To amend a reservation, click on the ellipsis (three vertical dots).&#x20;
* A pop-up appears displaying options such as Amendment, Seated, No Show, and Cancel.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (231).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Clicking the Amend button takes the user to the booking screen, where booking details can be modified.&#x20;
* After making the necessary changes, click the Save button to save the amendments.&#x20;
