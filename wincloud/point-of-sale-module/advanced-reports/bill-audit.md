# Bill Audit

The **Bill Audit Report** is used to review detailed billing information for transactions. It provides a complete audit trail of each bill generated in the system.

## Steps to View Bill Audit Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and click the **Advanced Reports** menu.

1. Select **Bill Audit**.

* The Bill Audit Report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/13 (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Use the available **sorting options** based on your requirement.

1. You can sort the data to view specific bill details more clearly.

Step 3: Generate the Report

1. After selecting the required sorting options, click on the **Display** button.
2. The system will generate the **Bill Audit Report**.

* The report will be displayed on the screen with complete bill-wise details.
