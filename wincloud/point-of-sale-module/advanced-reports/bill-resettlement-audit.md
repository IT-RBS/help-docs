# Bill Resettlement Audit

The **Bill Resettlement Audit** report helps users track and review all bill resettlement activities performed in the system.This report is useful for monitoring billing corrections, verifying user activities, and maintaining audit records for resettled transactions.

## Steps to View the Bill Resettlement Audit Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and click the **Advanced Reports** menu.

1. Select **Bill Resettlement Audit** screen will open.

* The **Bill Resettlement Audit** screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-12 16-30-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the report screen, users can filter and sort the data based on their requirements.

Step 3: After selecting the required filters and sorting options, click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the **Bill Resettlement Audit Report**.
