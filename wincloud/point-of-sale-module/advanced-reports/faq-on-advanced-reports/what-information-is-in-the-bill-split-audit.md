# What information is in the Bill Split Audit?

The Bill Split Audit report is designed to track and review transactions specifically for bills that have been split within the system. It provides a detailed view of several key data points:

* **Identification:** Bill numbers and the name of the assigned steward.
* **Order Details:** The type of Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) and the specific items billed.
* **Financials:** The total bill amount.
* **Timings:** The exact bill generation time and the settlement time.
* **Cancellations:** Details regarding any settlement cancellations.

To access this report, navigate to **Advance Reports > Bill Split Audit.** You can sort the data as needed before clicking Display to generate the report. Additionally, the system allows you to convert and save this audit in an Excel format.
