# What is the Bill Audit report?

The Bill Audit report is a comprehensive tracking tool used to review transaction details and operational efficiency. It specifically provides visibility into:

* **Identification:** Bill numbers and the assigned steward.
* **Order Details:** KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) types and specific items billed.
* **Financials:** The total bill amount.
* **Timings:** The exact bill generation time and the settlement time.
* **Cancellations:** Details regarding any settlement cancellations.

**How to Access the Report**

**To view this report, follow these steps:**

1. Navigate to the Advance menu.
2. Select Reports and then click on Bill Audit.
3. Apply any necessary sorting or filters based on your requirements.
4. Click Display to generate the report.
