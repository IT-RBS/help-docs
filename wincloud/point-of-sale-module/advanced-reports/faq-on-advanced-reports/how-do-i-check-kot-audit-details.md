# How do I check KOT Audit details?

To check KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) Audit details, follow these steps within the POS system:

1. **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to **Advance Reports > Kot Audit.**
2. **Apply Filters:** Choose any additional options you want printed in the report. You can also sort the data based on your specific requirements.
3. **Generate the Report:** Click the display button to load the audit details.

**What the KOT Audit Provides**

This report is used to monitor the lifecycle of kitchen tickets and includes the following information:

* **Identification:** KOT number and the specific outlet name.
* **Timings:** The exact time of creation as well as timing for any amendments.
* **Amendments:** Specific details regarding changes made to the ticket, with the report clearly segregating old data from amended data.
* **Cancellations:** Details regarding any cancelled KOTs.
