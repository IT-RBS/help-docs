# Applicable Stores

**Applicable Stores** are the specific stores, warehouses, or inventory locations where a particular master setting, transaction rule, or business process is applicable. This configuration helps organisations control and define the stores that can use a specific purchase type or related settings, ensuring accurate transaction processing and operational consistency.

## Steps to Configure Applicable Stores

Step 1: Navigate to the **Masters** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Applicable Stores**.

* The **Applicable Stores** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 15-01-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **Purchase Type** from the available options.

1. Once the Purchase Type is selected, the system will display the list of available stores and the existing store configuration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 15-02-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Verify that all required stores have been selected.

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10 (5).png>)

* The system will validate and save the selected store configuration.
