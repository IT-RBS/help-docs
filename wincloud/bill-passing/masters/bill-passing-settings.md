# Bill Passing Settings

**Bill Passing Settings** are configuration parameters that define how purchase bills, expense bills, or vendor invoices are verified, processed, approved, and posted within the system. These settings control the bill passing workflow and ensure that transactions comply with the organisation's accounting policies and approval procedures before payment processing or General Ledger posting.

## Steps to View and Modify Bill Passing Settings

Step 1: Navigate to the **Masters** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Bill Passing Settings**.

* The **Bill Passing Settings** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-41-48.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07.png>)

* The system will retrieve and display the current Bill Passing configuration settings.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-42-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Update the required settings based on operational requirements.

* Verify all modified settings before saving.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-43-00.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-28-07 (1).png>)

* The system will validate and update the Bill Passing Settings.
