# Purchase Type

**Purchase Type** refers to the classification of a purchase transaction based on its nature, source, or purpose. It determines how purchase transactions are processed, accounted for, and managed within the system. Configuring Purchase Types helps organisations categorise purchases accurately and apply the appropriate accounting and operational rules.

## Steps to Create a Purchase Type

Step 1: Navigate to the **Masters** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Purchase Type**.

* The **Purchase Type** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-26-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Add** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-26-30.png>)

* A new Purchase Type entry screen will appear.

Step 3: In the **General** section, enter the required Purchase Type details based on your requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-27-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Navigate to the **Accounts** section.

1. Define the required accounting configurations for the Purchase Type.
2. Select the appropriate ledger accounts and accounting parameters as required.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-27-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Verify all entered details in both the **General** and **Accounts** sections.

Step 5: After completing all required information, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 14-28-07.png>)

* The system will validate the entered details and create the new Purchase Type.
