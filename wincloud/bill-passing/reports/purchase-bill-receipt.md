# Purchase Bill Receipt

**Purchase Bill Receipts** are the records of goods or services that have been physically received and acknowledged against a purchase bill. These records confirm that the items billed by the supplier have been delivered and accepted by the organisation. This report is used for verification, stock reconciliation, and audit purposes.

## Steps to View Purchase Bill Receipts

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Purchase Bill Receipts**.

* The **Purchase Bill Receipts** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 13-07-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **Purchase Type** from the available options.

* Ensure all selected filters are correct before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 13-07-55.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Purchase Bill Receipts report based on the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 13-08-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Once the report is displayed, click the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-46-35.png>)

* The system will download the report in excel format.
