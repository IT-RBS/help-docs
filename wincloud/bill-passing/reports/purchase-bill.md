# Purchase Bill

A **Purchase Bill** is a formal invoice issued by a supplier for the supply of goods or services. It contains details such as item description, quantity, unit price, taxes, and total amount. The **Purchase Bill Report** provides a detailed view of all purchase bills based on selected filters such as type and bill status.

## Steps to View Purchase Bill Report

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Purchase Bill**.

* The **Purchase Bill Report** screen will be displayed.

Step 2: Once the screen is opened, the system will display filter options.

* These options help users refine the report based on requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-46-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required **Type** from the available options.

1. Select the **Bill Status** based on user requirement.
2. Ensure the correct filters are selected before proceeding.

Step 4: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Purchase Bill Report based on the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-46-17.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: To download the report, click the **Excel** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-46-35.png>)

* The system will export the displayed data into an Excel file.
