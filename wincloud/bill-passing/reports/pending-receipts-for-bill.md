# Pending Receipts For Bill

**Pending Receipts for Bill** refers to the quantity or value of goods or services that have been ordered and billed but have not yet been physically received or recorded in the system. This report helps users track pending deliveries against a purchase bill and ensures proper monitoring of outstanding receipts.

## Steps to View Pending Receipts for Bill

Step 1: Navigate to the **Reports** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Pending Receipts for Bill**.

* The **Pending Receipts for Bill** report screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-55-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Once the screen is opened, the system will display filter options.

* These filters allow users to refine the report based on requirements.

Step 3: Select the required **Type** from the available options.

1. Select the appropriate **Store** based on user requirement.
2. Ensure all selected filters are correct before proceeding.

Step 4: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Pending Receipts for Bill report based on the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-56-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Once the report is displayed, click the **Print** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-56-20.png>)

* The system will generate a printable version of the report.
