# Material Department Link to GL Cost Center

**Material Department Link to GL Cost Center** is a configuration used to map material departments to specific **General Ledger (GL) cost centers**. This mapping ensures that all material-related transactions are correctly posted to the appropriate cost centers, enabling accurate cost tracking, budgeting, and financial reporting.

## Steps to View and Maintain Material Department Link to GL Cost Center

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Material Department Link to GL Cost Center**.

* The configuration screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-51-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07 (3).png>)

* The system will display the existing mapping details.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-53-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Verify the assigned **Cost Center** for each material department.

1. Check the **Analysis** linked to the mapping.

Step 4: If any changes are made, ensure the details are correct.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10 (2).png>)

* The Material Department to GL Cost Center mapping will be successfully saved.&#x20;
