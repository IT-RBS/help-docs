# Product Sub Group Link to GL

**Product Sub Group Link to GL** is a configuration used to map each product sub-group to specific **General Ledger (GL) accounts**. This mapping ensures that all transactions related to products—such as purchases, sales, and stock movements—are posted to the correct GL accounts. This enables accurate financial accounting, cost control, and reporting.

## Steps to Configure Product Sub Group Link to GL

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Product Sub Group Link to GL**.

* The configuration screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-04-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07 (4).png>)

1. The system will display the list of available **Product Sub Groups**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-05-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: For the required product sub-group, click the **Search** icon.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-06-44.png>)

1. A list of available **General Ledger (GL) accounts** will be displayed.
2. Select the appropriate ledger account based on accounting requirements.
3. Confirm the selection to link the ledger to the chosen product sub-group.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-05-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After linking the required GL account(s), click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10 (3).png>)

* The selected Product Sub Group will be successfully linked to the designated General Ledger account.
