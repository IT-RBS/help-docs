# Applicable Store

**Applicable Stores** refer to the specific stores, warehouses, or inventory locations where a particular configuration, posting rule, master setting, or transaction is applicable. This feature allows organisations to define and control which stores are included in a specific process or configuration, ensuring accurate and location-specific operations.

## Steps to Configure Applicable Stores

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Applicable Stores**.

* The **Applicable Stores** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-25-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07.png>)

* The system will display the list of available stores, warehouses, or inventory locations.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-25-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required stores based on your requirements.

* Verify the selected stores to ensure the correct locations have been included.

Step 4: After selecting the required stores, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10.png>)

* The system will validate and save the configuration.
