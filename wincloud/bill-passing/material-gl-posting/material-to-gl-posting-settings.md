# Material To GL Posting Settings

**Material to GL Posting Settings** define the account mapping and posting rules used to transfer material-related transactions to the **General Ledger (GL)**. These settings determine how inventory movements, material consumption, stock adjustments, and other material transactions are recorded in financial accounting.

## Steps to View and Edit Material to GL Posting Settings

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Material to GL Posting Settings**.

* The **Material to GL Posting Settings** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-11-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07.png>)

* The system will display the Material to GL Posting configuration settings.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Modify the required settings based on your requirements.

Step 4: After completing the required modifications, click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10.png>)

* The system will validate and store the updated configuration settings.
