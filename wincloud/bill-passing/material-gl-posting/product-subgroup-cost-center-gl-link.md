# Product SubGroup Cost Center GL Link

**Product SubGroup Cost Center GL Link** is a configuration that maps each product sub-group to a specific **Cost Center** and corresponding **General Ledger (GL) accounts**. This ensures that all transactions related to the product sub-group are posted correctly to the assigned cost center and GL accounts, enabling accurate financial tracking, cost allocation, and reporting.

## Steps to Configure Product SubGroup Cost Center GL Link

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Product Sub Group Cost Center GL Link**.

* The configuration screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-23-20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-12-07.png>)

1. Select the required product sub-group from the list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-26-37.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Once the product sub-group is selected, the system will display the assigned **Cost Center** and linked **General Ledger (GL) accounts.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 12-27-00.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After confirming the ledger linking details, verify that all information is correct.

1. Click the **Save** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 11-13-10.png>)

* The Product SubGroup will be successfully linked with the selected Cost Center and GL accounts.
