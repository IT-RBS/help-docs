# Material To GL Posting

**Material to GL Posting** is a transaction used to post material-related transactions to the **General Ledger (GL)**. This process generates the necessary accounting entries to record the financial impact of material movements, inventory transactions, and stock-related activities in the organisation's accounting records.

## Steps to Perform Material to GL Posting

Step 1: Navigate to the **Material GL Posting** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Material to GL Posting**.

* The **Material to GL Posting** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 10-54-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Open** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 10-55-17.png>)

* The system will retrieve and display the material transactions that are pending posting to the General Ledger.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 10-55-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Verify the displayed transaction details.

1. Click the **Post** button.

* After the posting process is completed, the system will display a confirmation message.
