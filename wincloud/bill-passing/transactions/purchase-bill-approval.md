# Purchase Bill Approval

**Purchase Bill Approval** is the formal authorisation process in which a verified purchase invoice is reviewed and approved by an authorised user before it is posted for payment in the accounting system. This approval ensures that the invoice has been properly verified and complies with organisational policies and procedures.

## Steps to Perform Purchase Bill Approval

Step 1: Navigate to the **Transactions** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Purchase Bill Approval**.

* The **Purchase Bill Approval** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-36-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: The system will display a list of purchase bills that are pending approval.

1. Select the required **Purchase Bill** based on your requirement.

* After selecting the purchase bill, review the details before proceeding with the approval.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-36-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Once the purchase bill details have been verified, click the **Save** ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (1378).png>)button.

* The system will process the approval and update the status of the purchase bill.
