# Purchase Bill Passing Reprint

**Purchase Bill Passing Reprint** is a transaction that allows users to reprint a previously passed or approved Purchase Bill Passing document. This feature is useful for reference, verification, record-keeping, and audit purposes. Reprinting a document does not modify or affect the original transaction data stored in the system.

## Steps to View and Print a Purchase Bill Passing Reprint

Step 1: Navigate to the **Transactions** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.

1. Click on **Purchase Bill Passing Reprint**.

* The **Purchase Bill Passing Reprint** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-47-11 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **Category** from the available options

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-48-34 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Select the appropriate **Type** based on your requirement.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-49-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Enter or select the **From Date** and **To Date** to define the required period.

1. Click the **Display** button.

* The system will retrieve and display all Purchase Bill Passing records that match the selected criteria.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-49-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: From the displayed list, locate the required Purchase Bill Passing record.

1. Select the bill that you want to reprint.

* Review the details to ensure that the correct document has been selected.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-50-57.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Click the **Print** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-51-24.png>)

1. The selected Purchase Bill Passing document will be generated and displayed in a printable format.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 09-51-49.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
