# Bill Passing Post To GL

**Bill Passing Post to GL** is a transaction used to post approved and passed purchase bills to the **General Ledger (GL)**. This process creates the necessary accounting entries to record expenses, liabilities, taxes, and other financial impacts in the organisation's accounting records. Posting to the General Ledger ensures that all approved purchase transactions are accurately reflected in the financial statements.

## Steps to Perform Bill Passing Post to GL

Step 1: Open the Bill Passing Post to GL Screen

1. Navigate to the **Transactions** menu in the **Bill Passing** module.
2. Click on **Bill Passing Post to GL**.

* The **Bill Passing Post to GL** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 10-06-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click the **Post** button.![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-06-04 10-07-05.png>)

* The system will process the selected bill passing transactions and generate the corresponding General Ledger entries.
* After the posting process is completed, the system will display a confirmation message indicating that the transaction has been successfully posted.
