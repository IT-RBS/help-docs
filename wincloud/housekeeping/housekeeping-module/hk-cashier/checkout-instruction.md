# Checkout Instruction

The **Checkout Instruction** feature is used to enter and manage special instructions related to a guest’s checkout process. These instructions help housekeeping, cashier, and front office staff coordinate checkout activities efficiently.

## Steps to View and Add Checkout Instruction

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** main menu.

1. Click on **Cashier**.
2. Select **Checkout Instruction** from the submenu.
3. Once you click on **Checkout Instruction**, the system will display the Checkout Instruction screen.

* This screen allows users to enter and save checkout-related instructions.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 11-06-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Instruction** field.

1. Enter the required checkout instruction.

* Ensure the instruction entered is clear and accurate.

Step 3: After entering the instruction, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 11-08-38.png>)

* The checkout instruction will now be saved successfully in the system.
