# How to manage Checkout instructions ?

To manage Checkout Instructions within the Housekeeping module, you should follow these steps to enter and save specific guidance for a guest's departure:

1. **Access the Menu:** Go to the House Keeping Main Menu.
2. **Navigate to Cashier:** Click on the Cashier menu and then select Checkout Instruction.
3. **Enter the Information:** Click on the Instruction field and enter the relevant checkout details.
4. **Save:** Click the Save button to finalize the entry.

While this menu is used specifically for departure instructions, it is located within the same Cashier sub-menu as Guest Charges, which is used to post laundry, telephone, or miscellaneous charges to a guest's ledger.
