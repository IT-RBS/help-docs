# Room Blocking

The **Room Blocking** feature is used to block rooms temporarily for various operational purposes such as Maintenance work, Management use,Guest reservation requirements, Renovation or repair activities,VIP arrangements or any other operational reason.

* Blocked rooms will not be available for normal room allocation during the selected period.

## Steps to Create a Room Blocking Entry

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping Main Menu**.

1. Under **Transactions**, click on **Room Blocking**.

Step 2: Select the purpose of blocking from the available options.

* This defines the reason for blocking the room.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-13-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required room type from the list.

* The system will display available rooms based on the selected room type.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-16-58.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Select the **Room Number** that needs to be blocked.

Step 5: Select the start date and end date for room blocking.

**Important Note**

* During this period, the room will remain unavailable for allocation.

Step 6: Enter the reason or remarks for blocking the room(Example:AC maintenance work in progress, Reserved for VIP guest, Plumbing repair).

* Providing proper narration helps in operational tracking and future reference.

Step 7: After entering all required details, click the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-29-15 (5).png>)

* Once the room blocking is saved successfully, the system displays a confirmation pop-up message.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-18-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
