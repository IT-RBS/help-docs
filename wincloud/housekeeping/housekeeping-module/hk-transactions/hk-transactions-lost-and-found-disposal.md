# Lost  And Found Disposal

The **Lost and Found Disposal** option is used to record the disposal details of lost and found items stored in the hotel.

* The disposal transaction helps maintain proper tracking and audit records for all lost and found items.

## Steps to Process Lost and Found Disposal

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping Main Menu**.

1. Click on **Transaction**.
2. Select **Lost and Found Disposal**.

Step 2: Click on the **Lost and Found No.** search option.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 15-08-21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Select the required Lost and Found transaction from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 15-13-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Once the reference number is selected, all details related to the Lost and Found transaction will be displayed automatically.

Step 4: Click on the **Dispose** tab.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 15-19-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Select Disposal Details.

* Select the type of disposal from the available options.
* Select the dispatch method used for sending or handling the item.
* Enter or select the name of the person who handled the dispatch or disposal process.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 15-22-34.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: After entering all required disposal details, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-29-15 (1).png>)

* Once saved successfully, the Lost and Found Disposal transaction will be recorded in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 15-41-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
