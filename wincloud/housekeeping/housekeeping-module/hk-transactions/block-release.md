# Block Release

The **Block Release** feature is used to remove or release a previously blocked room. Once the block is released, the room becomes available again for normal operations such as reservation, allocation, or housekeeping activities.

## Steps to Release a Room Block

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping Main Menu**.

1. Under **Transactions**, Click on **Room Blocking**.

Step 2: Click on the **Block Release** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-36-16.png>)

Step 3: After selecting the required block type, the system displays the list of blocked rooms related to that category.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-39-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Select the Room to Release

1. Click **Select** for the particular blocked room that needs to be released.

Step 5: Enter the reason for releasing the room block.

* Proper narration should always be entered for tracking and audit purposes.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-42-54.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Click on the **Release Block** button to complete the process.

Step 7: Once the block is released successfully, the system updates the room status and displays a confirmation message.

* The room will now become available for normal hotel operations.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 12-44-39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
