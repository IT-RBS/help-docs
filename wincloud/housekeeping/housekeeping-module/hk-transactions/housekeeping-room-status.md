# Housekeeping Room Status

The **Housekeeping Room Status** option is used to update and manage the current housekeeping status of rooms in the hotel.This feature helps the housekeeping department monitor room conditions and room readiness efficiently. The updated room status is also useful for front office operations during room allocation and guest check-in.

## Steps to Update Housekeeping Room Status

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping Main Menu**.

1. Click on **Transaction**.
2. Select **Housekeeping Room Status**.

* The system displays the Housekeeping Room Status screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-16-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-17-44.png>)

Step 3: Choose the required room status from the available options.

* The available statuses may vary depending on the system configuration.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-25-44.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required status, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-29-15.png>)

* Once saved successfully, the room status will be updated in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 16-27-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
