# Lost & Found – Printing

The **Lost & Found – Printing** option is used to print the details of Lost & Found articles recorded in the system.

## Steps for Lost & Found – Printing

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping Main Menu**.

1. Click on **Transaction**.
2. Select **Lost & Found – Printing**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 17-29-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Select the required **Article** from the list.

* The list will display Lost & Found items already recorded in the system.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 17-30-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Ensure the correct Lost & Found article is selected before printing.

Step 4: Click on the **Print** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-25 17-31-00.png>)

* The system generates the Lost & Found print document for the selected article.
