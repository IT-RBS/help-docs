# Blocked Rooms

The **Blocked Rooms** report is used to view the list of rooms that are currently blocked in the system. This report helps hotel staff identify rooms that are unavailable for guest allocation due to maintenance, cleaning, reservation requirements, or other operational reasons.

## Steps to Open the Blocked Rooms Report

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** main menu.

1. Select the **Queries** menu.
2. Click on **Blocked Rooms**.

* The **Blocked Rooms** report screen will open.

Step 2: Choose the additional options that you want to include in the report.

* The system provides various filter options to customize the report according to your requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 11-33-14.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the required options and filters, click on the **Display** button.

* The **Blocked Rooms Report** will appear on the screen.
