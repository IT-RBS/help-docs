# In House Guest List

The **In House Guest List** is a report used to view details of all guests who are currently staying in the hotel. This report helps hotel staff monitor guest occupancy and access important guest information quickly.

* This report is useful for front office staff, housekeeping, management, and other hotel departments for daily operational activities.

## Steps to Open the In House Guest List

Step 1:  Go to the **Housekeeping** main menu.

1. Select the **Queries** menu.
2. Click on **In House Guest List**.

* The **In House Guest List** screen will open.

Step 2: Choose the additional options that you want to include in the report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 11-18-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required sorting option from the available list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 11-20-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required options and sorting criteria, click on the **Display** button.

* The **In House Guest List Report** will appear on the screen.
