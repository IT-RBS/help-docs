# Missing & Damage Items

The **Missing & Damage Items** master in the House Keeping module is used to define and manage items that are reported as missing or damaged. This helps in maintaining accurate inventory records and tracking items that are not usable due to loss, damage, or other operational issues.

## Steps to Create Missing & Damage Items

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** module from the main menu.

1. Click on **Masters** and select **Missing & Damage Items.**

* The **Missing & Damage Items** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-57-56.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-18-03.png>)

1. From the list, select the applicable **Store**.

* The selected store will be displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-58-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After selecting the store, click on the **Save** button.

* The Missing & Damage Item configuration will be saved successfully.
