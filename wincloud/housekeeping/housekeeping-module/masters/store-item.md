# Store Item

The **Store Item** master in the House Keeping module is used to create and manage items available in housekeeping stores. This feature helps maintain proper inventory records for housekeeping materials, guest supplies, cleaning items, linen items, and other stock-related products.

Store items can be categorized and assigned to specific store types for efficient inventory management and tracking.

## Steps to Create a Store Item

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** module from the main menu.

1. Click on **Masters** and select **Store Item.**

* The **Store Item** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-10-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-18-03 (1).png>)

1. Choose the required **Store Type** from the available list.

* The selected store type will be displayed on the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-11-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-13-03.png>)

1. To add a new category or item, click on the **Search Icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-13-32.png>)
2. Select the required item or category from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 17-13-50.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering all required item details, click on the **Save** button.

* The new store item will be created successfully and added to the Store Item list.
