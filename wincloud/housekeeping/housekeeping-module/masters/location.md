# Location

The **Location Master** in the House Keeping module is used to create and manage different locations within the property. These locations help housekeeping staff organize operations efficiently by categorizing areas such as floors, sections, storage areas, laundry rooms, and other operational locations.

## Steps to Create a Location

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** module from the main menu.

1. Click on **Masters** and select **Location.**

* The **Location** screen will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-17-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Open** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-18-03.png>)

1. Choose the required **Location Type** from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-19-24.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Add** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-20-26.png>)

1. Enter the required **Location Name** in the provided field.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 16-21-02.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After entering the location details, click on the **Save** button.

* The new location will be created successfully and added to the Location Master list.
