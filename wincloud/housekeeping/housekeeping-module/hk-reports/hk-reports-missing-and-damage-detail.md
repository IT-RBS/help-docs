# Missing & Damage Details

The **Missing & Damage Details** report provides detailed information about items that are reported as missing or damaged during daily operations. This report helps departments maintain accurate inventory records by tracking item details, quantities, dates, and remarks related to the issue.

The report supports better accountability and enables timely corrective actions by the concerned departments.

## Steps to View Missing & Damage Details Report

Step 1: Open the **Housekeeping** module.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. Select **Missing & Damage list**.

* The **Missing & Damages** screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-58-31.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2:Choose the required **From Date** and **To Date**.

1. Ensure the selected date range covers the period for which you want to view the report.

Step 3: After selecting the date range, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Missing & Damage Details report based on the selected dates.
