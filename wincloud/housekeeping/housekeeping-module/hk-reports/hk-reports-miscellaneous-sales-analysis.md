# Miscellaneous Sales Analysis

The **Miscellaneous Sales Analysis** report provides a detailed overview of miscellaneous sales transactions carried out within the property. This report helps analyze additional revenue generated from miscellaneous items and services by displaying details such as transaction date, item description, quantity, amount, and payment mode.

* The report supports management in monitoring and evaluating supplementary revenue sources effectively.

## Steps to View Miscellaneous Sales Analysis Report

Step 1: Open the **Housekeeping** module.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. Select  **Miscellaneous Sales Analysis**.

* The **Miscellaneous Sales Analysis** screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 15-19-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **Advanced Filter** option.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1365).png>)

1. Apply the required filters to refine the report data.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 15-17-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: After applying the required filters, click on the **Display** button.

* The system will generate and display the Miscellaneous Sales Analysis report based on the selected criteria.
