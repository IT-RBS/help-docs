# Guest Complaints & Suggestions

**Guest Complaints & Suggestions**

What is **Guest Complaints & Suggestions,**

Using this report the user can check the Guest complaints list, Request list & Suggestions list with the status of complains as well.

Steps To Open **Guest Complaints & Suggestions,**

1. Go to **Reports >** **Guest Complaints & Suggestions,**
2. Choose the additional option that you want printed in this report. The filter option is also available as shown below.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (68).png>)

1. Then click on display and the report will appear as shown below.

**Note** : If the user would like to convert the report to excel click ![](../../../.gitbook/assets/38.png)
