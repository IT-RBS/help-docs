# Lost & Found Status

The **Lost & Found Status** report is used to track and monitor items that are either lost by guests or found within the hotel premises. This report helps hotel staff maintain proper records of guest belongings and hotel property that have been reported as lost or found.

## Steps to View Lost & Found Status

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping** main menu.

1. Select the **Reports** menu.
2. Click on **Lost & Found Status**.

* The **Lost & Found Status** screen will open.

Step 2: Select the **Location Type** from the available drop-down list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 12-27-59 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the required **Status Type** from the available options then select the **Item Type** if required.

* These filters help refine the report based on specific requirements.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 12-30-18.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After selecting the required filters, click **Display**. The system will display the **Lost & Found Status Report**.
