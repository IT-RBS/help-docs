# Expected Departure

The **Expected Departure** report displays the list of in-house guests who are scheduled to depart from the property. Users can view departures date-wise and apply filters such as market segment, reservation status, and more to search for specific reservations.

* This report also allows users to view past departure records for reference.

## Steps to View the Expected Departure Report

Step 1: Open the **Housekeeping** module.

1. Click on **Reports**.
2. Select **Expected Departure**.

* The **Expected Departure** screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 15-29-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: To view departures for a different date:

1. Click on the **Calendar Icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1364).png>)
2. Select the required departure date from the calendar.

* This helps in viewing departures based on the selected time period.

Step 3: Click on the **Filter Icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1363).png>)

1. Select the required search criteria.

* This helps in locating specific reservations quickly.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-42-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Options icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-17-02.png>)

To add additional information to the report:

1. Select the required fields from the available list.

* The selected fields will appear as additional columns in the report. These columns display information entered during the reservation process.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-43-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After applying all required filters and selections, click **Display** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-44-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Click the **Excel icon**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-22-55.png>) to download it in Excel format, or click the **Print icon**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-21-15.png>) to open the print preview and proceed with printing.
