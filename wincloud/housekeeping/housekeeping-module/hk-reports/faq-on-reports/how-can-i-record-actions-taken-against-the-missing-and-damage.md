# How can I record actions taken against the Missing & Damage?

Yes, you can record actions taken specifically within the Missing & Damage transaction in the Housekeeping module.When reporting a missing or damaged item, the system requires you to enter the specific nature of the resolution:

* **Action Taken Details:** You must select how the incident is being handled, with options including charged to staff, charged to guest, or charged to Company.
* **Associated Financials:** You can record the amount charged to the relevant party, which is then passed to the front office.
* **Follow-up:** You can also specify a replacement date for the missing or damaged item.

These recorded actions are later tracked in the Missing & Damage Details report, which is designed to support timely corrective action by the concerned departments and maintain accurate inventory control
