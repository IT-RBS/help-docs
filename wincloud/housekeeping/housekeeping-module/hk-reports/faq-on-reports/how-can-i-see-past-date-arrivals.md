# How can I see past date arrivals?

To view arrivals from a past date, you must access the Expected Arrivals report within the Housekeeping module. This report is designed to show upcoming confirmed reservations but also allows you to look back at previous dates.Follow these steps to see past date arrivals:

1. **Navigate to the Report:** Go to the HOUSE KEEPING module, select REPORTS, and then click on EXPECTED ARRIVALS.
2. **Change the Date:** Click on the calendar/date icon and select the specific past date you wish to view.
3. **Select Past Arrivals:** Ensure you select the specific viewing option (represented by an icon in the system) designated for displaying past date arrivals in the report.
4. **Display the Report:** Click the view/display icon to generate the report once your date and filters are applied.

Additionally, you can use the filter icon to search for specific reservations or click the more details icon to add specific columns of information that were entered during the initial reservation process. The resulting report can be printed or converted to Excel
