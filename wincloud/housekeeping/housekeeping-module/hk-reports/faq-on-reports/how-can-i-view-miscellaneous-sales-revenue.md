# How can I view miscellaneous sales revenue?

Yes, you can view miscellaneous sales revenue by accessing the Miscellaneous Sales Analysis report within the Housekeeping module.

**Purpose of the Report:**

This report provides a comprehensive overview of sales transactions and is designed to help analyze revenue generated from miscellaneous items or services. It supports management in monitoring additional revenue streams by capturing the following details:

* Transaction Date.
* Item Description.
* Quantity.
* Amount.
* Payment Mode.

How to View the Report To generate this analysis, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the HOUSE KEEPING module.
2. Go to the REPORTS menu and select MISCELLANEOUS SALES ANALYSIS.
3. Click on the Advance Filter option if you need to narrow your search.
4. Click the Display button to view the results.

Additionally, miscellaneous charges themselves are posted to guest ledgers through the Guest Charges menu under the Cashier section, which allows for the manual entry of miscellaneous revenue missed during standard posting
