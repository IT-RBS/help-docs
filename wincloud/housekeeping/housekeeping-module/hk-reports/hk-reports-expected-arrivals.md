# Expected Arrivals

The **Expected Arrivals** report displays all upcoming confirmed reservations that are scheduled to arrive on a selected date. This report helps the housekeeping team plan room readiness, staffing, and resource allocation in advance.

* You can also view **past arrival data**, apply filters, and export or print the report for operational use.

### Steps to View Expected Arrivals

Step 1: Go to the **Housekeeping main menu**.

1. Select the **Reports** menu.
2. Click on **Expected Arrivals**.

* The **Expected Arrivals** screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-10-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **calendar/date icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-12-02.png>)

1. Select the required date from the calendar.

* The system will automatically display the arrivals for the selected date.

Step 3: Click on the **filter icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-12-56.png>)

1. To view previous arrival details, select the **Past Date option/icon**.
2. Select the required filter criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-15-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click on the **Options icon**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-17-02.png>)

1. Select the required fields from the available list.

* The system will update the report by adding the selected columns (if the data is available in the reservation).

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-17-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After applying filters or selecting columns, click on **Display** button.

* The system will reload and display the updated Expected Arrivals report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-19-51.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Click the **Excel icon**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-22-55.png>) to download it in Excel format, or click the **Print icon**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-26 14-21-15.png>) to open the print preview and proceed with printing.
