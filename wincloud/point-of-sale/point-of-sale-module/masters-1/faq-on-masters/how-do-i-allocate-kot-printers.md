# How do I allocate KOT printers?

To allocate KOT printers, you can use several different methods depending on whether you want to assign them by product group, by specific items within an outlet, or by a specific computer system.

**1. Allocation by Product Group**&#x20;

This is the standard method used to assign KOT printers to entire groups of products.

* **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to **Master1 > Kot > Kot printer Allocation.**
* **Access the List:** Click the Open button.
* **Select Printer:** Click on printer location to see the list of previously created KOT printers.
* **Assign Groups:** Select the appropriate product group for each printer location.
* **Save:** Click the save button to update the configuration.

**2. Allocation by Outlet and Item**

If you need more granular control, you can allocate printers to specific items within a particular outlet.

* **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to **Master3 > Outlet-Item Printer Allocation.**
* **Access the Menu:** Click the Open button.
* **Select Outlet:** Choose the relevant outlet and click the add button.
* **Assign Printer:** Select the specific printer name and click Save.

**3. Allocation by System, Outlet, and Item**

This method allows you to allocate items to a KOT printer based on both the outlet and the specific computer system (terminal) being used to place the order.

* **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to **Master3 > Outlet - Item System Kot Printer Allocation.**
* **Start New Entry:** Click the New button.
* **Define Parameters:** Select the Outlet name and the specific System.
* **Select Items:** Click the Add button and choose the items to be allocated.
* **Save:** Click the save button to finalize
