# KOT Reprint Audit

The **KOT Reprint Audit** report is used to monitor and review instances where Kitchen Order Tickets (KOTs) have been reprinted. This report helps ensure that KOT reprints are properly authorized and used only for genuine operational requirements.

## Steps to View KOT Reprint Audit

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and click the **Advanced Reports** menu.

1. Select **KOT Reprint Audit**.

* The KOT Reprint Audit screen will open.

Step 2: Apply Sorting Options

1. Use the available **sorting options** based on your requirement.
2. Select the necessary filters to organize the report data.

These options help users generate a more accurate and customized report.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/16 (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Generate the Report

1. After selecting the required options, click on the **Display** button.
2. The system will generate and display the **KOT Reprint Audit Report**.

The report will show all KOT reprint details for review and analysis.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-12 16-52-06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* If you want to export the report to Excel:

1. Click on the **Excel Export Icon** available on the report screen.
2. The report will be downloaded in Excel format.
