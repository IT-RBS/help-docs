# How to check item-wise sales?

To check item-wise sales, you can use the specialized Item Wise Sales report or the Outlet Item Audit found in the Advance module.

**Item Wise Sales Report**

The Item-wise Sales report provides comprehensive information for a selected period, allowing you to identify which items are the most popular. It lists all items sold during that timeframe along with their:

* Net quantity.
* Cost value.
* Sales value.
* Discount value.

**Steps to access the report:**

1. Navigate to **Advance Reports > Item Wise Sales.**
2. Select any additional options or filter settings you want printed in the report.
3. Apply any necessary sorting based on your requirements.
4. Click the display button to generate the report.

**Outlet Item Audit**

Alternatively, you can use the Outlet Item Audit to monitor and verify item-wise sales and usage at a specific outlet. This audit ensures correct item configuration, pricing, and billing accuracy while helping to prevent discrepancies or misuse.Steps to access the audit:

1. Navigate to **Advance Reports > Outlet Item Audit.**
2. Click the display button to view the report
