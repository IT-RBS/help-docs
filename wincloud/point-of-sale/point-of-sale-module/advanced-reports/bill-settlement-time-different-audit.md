# Bill Settlement Time Different Audit

The **POS Bill Settlement Time Difference Audit** report is used to identify and analyze the time difference between POS bill generation and payment settlement.

* This report helps management monitor delays in bill settlement and review operational efficiency at the billing counter.

## Steps to View POS Bill Settlement Time Difference Audit

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and click the **Advanced Reports** menu.

1. Select **POS Bill Settlement Time Difference Audit**.

* The report screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-12 16-54-27.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Apply Sorting and Filter Options

1. Use the available **sorting options** to filter or organize the report based on your requirement.
2. Select the necessary criteria before generating the report.

These options help users analyze settlement time differences more effectively.

Step 3: Generate the Report

1. After selecting the required options, click on the **Display** button.
2. The system will generate the report and display it on the screen.
