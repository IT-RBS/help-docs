# Can I view month-wise sales summary?

Yes, you can view month-wise sales summaries using specific reports and analysis tools within the system:

**1. Sales Day Book Month Wise Report**

This specialized report allows you to view transaction details on a monthly basis.

* Details Provided: It shows a day-wise summary of sales, including product group-wise data and GST details.
* How to Access:
  1. Navigate to **Reports > Sales Day Book Month Wise.**
  2. Select the specific outlet for which you want to generate the report.
  3. Use the Advance Filter options if you need to sort the data further.
  4. Click Display to generate the report.

**2. Cover Analysis**

If you need to analyze sales volume in terms of "covers" (guest packs) over time, this report provides both month-wise and day-wise views.

* **Details Provided:** It allows you to check how many packs were sold per day or per month, and includes session-wise and outlet-wise details.
* **How to Access:**
  1. Navigate to **Analysis > Cover Analysis.**
  2. Choose any additional options for the report, such as selecting MTD/YTD to generate a yearly view,.
  3. Click Display to load the data.

**3. Sales Analysis**

For a broader overview of sales trends, you can also use the general Sales Analysis tool located under the Analysis main menu.Both the Sales Day Book Month Wise and Cover Analysis reports can be converted and saved into Excel format by clicking the corresponding icon,.
