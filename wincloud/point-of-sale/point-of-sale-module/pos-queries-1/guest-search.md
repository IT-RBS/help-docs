# Guest Search

The **Guest Search** feature helps users search and view guest details using advanced filtering options. Follow the steps below to access and use the Guest Search screen successfully.

## Steps to View Guest Search

Step 1: Navigate to the **Point of Sale** module and select **Queries** menu.

1. Click on **Guest Search**.

* The Guest Search screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/30 (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on **Advanced Filter**![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-25-51.png>)to open additional search options.

* The Advanced Filter section allows users to apply detailed search criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/31 (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Option** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-40-13.png>)

* Additional search and display settings will appear.

Step 4: Locate the **Display** button or dropdown menu.

1. Click on **Display**.
2. Select **Guest InHouse** from the available options.

* The system will now display all guests who are currently checked in and staying in-house.
