# How do I view session wise statistics?

To view Session Statistics, you need to navigate to the Queries module in the system.

Follow these steps to access the information:

1. Go to the Queries Main Menu.
2. Click on Session Statistics.
3. A screen will appear displaying the relevant statistics.

For related session information, you can also manage POS sessions (adding session IDs and names) under **Master1 > POS – Session**. Additionally, the Cover Analysis report (found under **Analysis > Cover Analysis**) can provide session-wise guest pack details
