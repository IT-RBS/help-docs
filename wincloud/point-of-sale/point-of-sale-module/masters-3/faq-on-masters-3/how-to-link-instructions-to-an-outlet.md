# How to link instructions to an outlet?

To link instructions to a specific outlet, you must use the Outlet - Instruction Link menu located in the master settings.&#x20;

Follow these steps to complete the link:

1. **Navigate to the Menu:** Go to **Master3 > Outlet - Instruction Link.**
2. **Access the Interface:** Click on the Open button.
3. **Select the Outlet:** Choose the appropriate Outlet Name from the list.
4. **Add the Link:** Click the Add button.
5. **Define and Save:** Enter the Link Type and Link Name, then click the Save button to finalize the connection.

In addition to linking instructions at the outlet level, the system also allows you to configure Item Instructions for specific products or create Instruction Groups for broader categorization
