# POS Advance

The **POS Advance** feature is used to collect advance payments from guests before billing or service completion. This helps maintain advance payment records for future transactions.

## Steps to Create POS Advance

Step 1: Go to the **Point of Sale** module and select the **Transaction** menu.

1. Click on **POS Advance**.

* The POS Advance screen will open.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1317).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the **New** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-45-19.png>)

1. Select the required **Outlet Name** from the list.

* This ensures the advance is recorded for the correct outlet.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1318).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click on the **Guest Search** button.

1. Select the required **Guest Name** from the list.

* This links the advance payment to a specific guest.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1319).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter the **Advance Amount** to be collected.

1. Select the **Settlement Mode** (such as cash, card, or other available payment methods).

* Ensure all details are entered correctly before saving.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1320).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: After entering all required details, click on the **Save** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-06-11 15-46-00.png>)

1. The POS Advance entry will be successfully recorded in the system.
