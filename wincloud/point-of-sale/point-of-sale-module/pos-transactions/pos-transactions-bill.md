# Billing

Billing is the second process in the **Point of Sale (POS)** system after generating a **KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket).**&#x49;n the software, the **Bill** option is referred to as **“CHECK”**.

* Once the KOT is created, the user can proceed with bill generation and settlement.

## Steps to Create a Bill (CHECK)

Step 1: First, create the required **KOT** for the selected table.

1. Ensure all ordered items are entered correctly before proceeding to billing.

Step 2: After creating the KOT, return to the table selection screen.

1. Click on the required **Table Number** for which the bill needs to be generated.

Step 3: On the KOT screen, click on the **CHECK** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-44-44.png>)located at the bottom of the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-43-26.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: After clicking **CHECK**, the system will redirect you to the **Billing Screen**.

1. Verify all bill details carefully before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-46-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: At the bottom of the billing screen, click on the **Print** option.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-49-35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Click on the respective **Table Number**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-51-36.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Choose the required **Settlement Mode** based on the customer’s payment method.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-54-27.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* After selecting the payment method, click on **SETTLE** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-40-05.png>)
* The system will complete the billing process successfully.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-56-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
