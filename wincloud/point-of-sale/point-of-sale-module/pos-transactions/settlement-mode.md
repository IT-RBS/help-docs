# Settlement Mode

**Settlement Mode** appears after the KOT bill has been generated. It is used to complete the payment process for the customer bill.

## Steps to Perform Settlement

Step 1: Generate the KOT bill.

Step 2: Select the required **Settlement Mode** (Cash, Card, UPI, etc.).

1. The settlement screen will appear.
2. Enter the payment details if required.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1369).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Click the **Settle** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 17-51-48.png>)

* Once the settlement is completed successfully, a confirmation popup message will appear.

### KOT OPTIONS

1\. **KOT Reprint**

KOT Reprint is used to print a duplicate copy of the Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT).

**Steps to Reprint KOT**

1. Open the **KOT Reprint** option.
2. Select the required **Table Number**.
3. Enter the reason for reprinting.
4. Click the **print** button.
5. The KOT will be printed again.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-03-43.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\. **KOT Cancellation**

KOT Cancellation is used to cancel an existing KOT.

**Steps to Cancel KOT**

1. Open the **KOT Cancellation** option.
2. Select the KOT **Table Number**.
3. Enter the cancellation reason.
4. Click the **Cancel** button.
5. The selected KOT will be cancelled successfully.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-08-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\. **KOT Date Transfer**

KOT Date Transfer is used to transfer a KOT from one business date to another.

**Steps for KOT Date Transfer**

1. Open the **KOT Date Transfer** option.
2. Select the KOT details.
3. Choose the current date and the new transfer date.
4. Click the **Transfer** button.
5. The KOT will be transferred to the selected date.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-13-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

&#x34;**. KOT Item Transfer**

KOT Item Transfer is used to transfer selected items from one table to another table.

**Steps for KOT Item Transfer**

1. Open the **KOT Item Transfer** option.
2. Select the source table.
3. Select the items to transfer.
4. Choose the destination table.
5. Click the **Transfer** button.
6. The selected items will move to the new table.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-16-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. **No Charge KOT to Charge KOT**

This option converts a non-chargeable KOT into a chargeable KOT.

**Steps to Convert No Charge KOT to Charge KOT**

1. Open the **No Charge KOT to Charge KOT** option.
2. Select the required KOT.
3. Confirm the conversion.
4. Click the **Save** button.
5. The KOT will become chargeable.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-18-53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. **KOT to No Charge KOT**

This option converts a chargeable KOT into a non-chargeable KOT.

**Steps to Convert Charge KOT to No Charge KOT**

1. Open the **KOT to No Charge KOT** option.
2. Select the required KOT.
3. Confirm the conversion.
4. Click the **Save** button.
5. The KOT will become non-chargeable.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-22-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7\. **KOT Table Transfer to Other Outlet**

This option transfers a KOT from the current outlet to another outlet.

**Steps for KOT Transfer to Another Outlet**

1. Open the **KOT Table Transfer to Other Outlet** option.
2. Select the source table/KOT.
3. Choose the destination outlet.
4. Click the **Transfer** button.
5. The KOT will move to the selected outlet.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-29 18-26-12.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8\. **KOT Table Transfer**

KOT Table Transfer is used to move an active KOT from one table to another table within the same outlet.

**Steps for KOT Table Transfer**

1. Open the **KOT Table Transfer** option.
2. Select the current table number.
3. Choose the new table number.
4. Click the **Transfer** button.
5. The KOT will be moved successfully.
