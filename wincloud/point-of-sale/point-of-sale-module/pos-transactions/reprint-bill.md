# Reprint Bill

The **Bill Reprint** option is used to print a duplicate copy of an already generated bill. This feature helps users reprint bills when customers request another copy or when the original print is unclear or lost.

## Steps to View and Reprint a Bill

Step 1: Go to the **Point of Sale** module and select the **Transaction** menu.

1. Click on **Bill Reprint**.

* The Bill Reprint screen will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/21 (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Choose the required **Outlet Name** from the list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/22 (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Select the required **System Name** from the available list.

* The system name identifies the billing terminal or POS system from which the bill was generated.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/23 (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Click the **Display** button.

* The system will display the list of bills available for the selected outlet and system

Step 5: Choose the required bill from the displayed list.

* Verify the bill details carefully before proceeding.

Step 6: Enter the reason for reprinting the bill in the **Reprint Reason** field.

* Entering a valid reason helps maintain proper audit records.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/24 (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Click on the **Print** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2026-05-27 10-35-13.png>)

* The selected bill will be reprinted successfully.
