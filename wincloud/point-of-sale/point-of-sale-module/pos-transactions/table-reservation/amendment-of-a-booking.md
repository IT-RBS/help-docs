# Amendment of a Booking

* To amend a reservation, click on the ellipsis (three vertical dots).
* A pop-up appears displaying options such as Amendment, Seated, No Show, and Cancel.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (231).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Clicking the Amend button takes the user to the booking screen, where booking details can be modified.
* After making the necessary changes, click the Save button to save the amendments.
