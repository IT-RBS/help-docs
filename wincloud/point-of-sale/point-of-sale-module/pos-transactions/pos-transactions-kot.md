# KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket)

**KOT** stands for **Kitchen Order Ticket**. It is one of the most important procedures in the restaurant billing process. A KOT contains the list of food and beverage items ordered by the guest. Once the KOT is created, it is sent directly to the kitchen for preparation and is also used for billing purposes.

* The KOT can be modified before the final bill is generated, based on the guest’s requests or order changes.

## Steps to Create a KOT

Step 1: Go to the **Transactions** menu.

1. Click on **KOT**.

* This will redirect you to the KOT login screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-24-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: On the login screen:

1. Select the preferred **Outlet**.
2. Select the **Table Server** or waiter.
3. Click **Login** to continue.

* This will open the table selection screen.

Step 3: From the displayed list of tables, select the required **Table Number**.

1. The selected table will open for order entry.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-27-41.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Enter the number of **covers** (number of guests seated at the table).

1. Confirm the entry to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-28-52.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Add Food Items to the KOT.

1. The order entry window allows you to add the food and beverage items ordered by the guest.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-31-00.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Menu Navigation. The menu is organized into:

* **Group**
* **Sub-Group**
* **Items**

Example:

* Chinese → Non-Veg → Chicken Fried Rice

This categorization helps in quickly locating menu items.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-32-41.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Select the required quantity from the **Quantity Column**.

1. Then select the food item.

**Important Notes:**

* Always select the quantity first and then the item.
* If the item is selected first, the system automatically assigns the default quantity as 1.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-34-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Functional Buttons and Their Usage**

* The following buttons and options are available while creating a KOT.

Step 7: The **MESSAGE** button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1339).png>) is used to add special instructions for a specific item (Examples: Less Oil, No Onion, With Ice Cubes, Extra Cheese).

* These instructions are sent directly to the kitchen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-40-03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: The **INSTRUCTION** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1342).png>)is used to add predefined instructions available in the system (Examples: Medium Spicy, Sharing 1/2, No Sugar).

* This helps maintain consistency in order preparation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-43-05.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 9: The **BEVERAGE INSTRUCTION** option ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1345).png>)is used to add serving instructions for beverages (Examples: Serve Chilled, Without Ice, Serve Later).

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-53-19.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 10: The **PACK** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-01-49.png>)is used to select package or combo options available for the item.

* This is mainly used for Combo meals, Meal packages and Special offers.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 10-59-07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 11: The **DIRECT** button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-04-39.png>)allows you to directly search and select menu items without navigating through groups and sub-groups.

* This option helps save time during busy restaurant operations.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-05-48.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 12:The **OPEN +** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-16-57.png>)is used to create an open item with a custom sales rate.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-10-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 13:The **REPEAT** button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-18-07.png>) is used to repeat previously selected items in the KOT.

* This helps quickly duplicate orders without selecting the items again.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-11-59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 14: The **FREE** button![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-18-56.png>) is used to mark an item as complimentary or free of charge (Example: Complimentary Dessert, Free Soft Drink).

* The selected item will not be charged in the bill.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-14-46.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 15: After adding all food and beverage items, click on **Save KOT**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-38-22.png>)

* The KOT will be sent to the kitchen for preparation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-21-28.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 16: The **CHECK & SETTLE** button ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-36-49.png>)is used to directly move the KOT to settlement mode without printing the KOT bill.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-26-25.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 17: If the **E-Invoice** option is enabled:

* The KOT bill can be sent directly to the guest through **WhatsApp**.
* This provides a quick and paperless billing experience.

Step 18: After selecting the settlement mode, click on the **Settle** button.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 11-40-05.png>)

1. The billing process will be completed successfully.
