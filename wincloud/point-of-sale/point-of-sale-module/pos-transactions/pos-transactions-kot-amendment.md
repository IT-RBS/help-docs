# KOT Amendment

A **KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket)** can be amended after creation, provided the bill has not been raised for that particular KOT.

**Important Conditions:**

* A KOT can be amended only when the bill has **not** been generated.
* If the bill was generated and later cancelled, the original KOT remains active and modifications are still allowed.
* Once a KOT is created, the corresponding **Table Number** changes color, making it easy to identify active KOTs.
* A KOT moves to **Delayed Status** when it remains pending without billing for a certain period.

## Steps to Perform a KOT Amendment

Step 1: Identify the KOT Status

1. Open the POS screen.
2. Locate the status icons displayed on the screen.
3. Click directly on a status icon to view the list of tables under that status.
4. After KOT creation, the color of the respective table number changes automatically (Example: Table No. 001).

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-35-15.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on the table number for which the KOT was created.

1. The KOT details screen will appear.
2. All selected items in the KOT will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-38-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Removing an Item from a KOT**

Step 3: In the KOT screen, select the item you want to remove.

1. Click on **QTY**.
2. Reduce the quantity to **0** or remove the item as required.
3. Click **Save KOT**.![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1351).png>)

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-44-16.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: A pop-up window will appear.

1. Enter a valid **Amend Reason**.
2. Click **OK** to save the changes.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-45-04.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Changing the Quantity of an Item**

Step 5: Open the required KOT by clicking the table number.

1. Select the item whose quantity needs to be changed.
2. Click on **QTY**.
3. Increase or decrease the quantity as required.
4. Click **Save KOT**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-56-29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Provide Amendment Reason

1. A pop-up window will appear.
2. Enter a valid **Amend Reason**.
3. Click **OK**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1355).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The updated quantity will now reflect in the KOT.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 14-58-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Transferring KOT Items from One Table to Another**

Step 7: On the table display screen, click **OPTIONS**

1. Under the **KOT** section, select **KOT ITEM TRANSFER**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-08-45.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: Transfer the Items

1. Select the **From Table Number**.
2. Mark the required items to be transferred.
3. Enter the transfer quantity in the **TR QTY** column.
4. Select the **TRANSFER TO** table number.
5. Enter a valid transfer reason.
6. Click **TRANSFER**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-12-33.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The selected items will now be moved to the destination table.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-14-40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Convert Chargeable KOT to No Charge KOT**

Step 9: Click **OPTIONS** at the top of the table window.

1. Under the **KOT** section, select **KOT TO NO CHARGE KOT**.

* This option converts a regular chargeable KOT into a non-chargeable KOT.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-26-05.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 10: Enter the required details.

1. Provide a valid reason in the **Narration Box**.
2. Click **CONVERT**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-29-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The selected KOT will now become a No Charge KOT.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-31-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Convert No Charge KOT to Chargeable KOT**

Step 11: Click **OPTIONS** at the top of the table window.

1. Under the **KOT** section, select **NO CHARGE KOT TO KOT**.

* This option converts a non-chargeable KOT back into a chargeable KOT.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1358).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 12: Select the required pending No Charge KOT.

1. Click **CONVERT**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-36-10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* The system will automatically convert the selected No Charge KOT into a regular chargeable KOT.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-39-13.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
