# KOT Cancellation

The **KOT Cancellation** option is used to cancel a Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) that has already been generated. The system allows cancellation for both Chargeable KOT and Non-Chargeable (No Charge) KOT.

* A valid reason must be entered before the cancellation is completed.

## Steps to Perform a KOT Cancellation

Step 1: Go to the main screen where the **table numbers** are displayed.

1. At the top of the screen, click on the **OPTIONS** button.
2. After clicking **OPTIONS**, a menu will appear.
3. Under the **KOT** column, select **KOT CANCELLATION**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 15-48-09.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Cancelling a Chargeable KOT**

Step 3: Once the **KOT Cancellation** screen opens, all **pending chargeable KOTs** will be displayed.

1. Select the KOT that needs to be cancelled.

Step 4: After selecting the KOT, go to the **REASON BOX** located at the bottom of the screen.

1. Enter a valid reason for the cancellation.

Step 5: Before cancelling, verify the selected KOT details in the preview section.

1. Ensure that the correct KOT has been selected.

Step 6: After verifying the details and entering the reason, click on **CANCEL KOT**.

1. The selected KOT will be cancelled successfully.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1359).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Cancelling a Non-Chargeable (No Charge) KOT**

Step 1: Click **OPTIONS** from the main screen.

1. Select **KOT CANCELLATION** under the KOT section.

Step 2: Click on the **NO CHARGE KOT** button available in the KOT Cancellation window.

1. All pending non-chargeable KOTs will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot From 2026-05-22 16-23-22.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Choose the non-chargeable KOT that needs to be cancelled.

Step 4: In the **REASON BOX**, enter the reason for cancellation.

Step 5: Check the preview to confirm the correct KOT has been selected.

1. Click **CANCEL KOT** to complete the cancellation process.
