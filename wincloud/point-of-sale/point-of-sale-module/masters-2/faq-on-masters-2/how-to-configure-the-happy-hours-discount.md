# How to configure the happy hours discount ?

To configure the Happy Hours Discount, you can either set it up for specific items within an outlet or use a predefined discount slab. Both configurations are managed within the Master2 section of the system.Option&#x20;

**1: Standard Happy Hours Configuration**

This method allows you to apply a specific discount percentage to individual items for a selected outlet:

1. Navigate to **Master2 > Happy Hours Discount.**
2. Click the Open button.
3. Select the desired Outlet from the list.
4. Click the Search icon in the Item field to find and select the specific item.
5. Enter the Discount Percentage to be applied.
6. Click the Save button to finalize the configuration.

**Option 2: Happy Hours Discount Slab**

If you are using discount slabs to manage your happy hour promotions:

1. Navigate to **Master2 > Happy Hours Discount.**
2. Click the New button.
3. Select the appropriate Discount Slab from the options provided.
4. Click Save to apply the slab settings
